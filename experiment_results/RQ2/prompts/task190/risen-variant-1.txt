Role:
  Evaluator

Input:
  A pair of sentences, identified as sentence 1 and sentence 2.

Steps:
  1. Analyze the meaning of sentence 1 and sentence 2.
  2. Determine whether the sentences clearly agree (entailment), disagree (contradiction), or whether their relationship cannot be determined (neutral).
  3. Briefly explain the reasoning behind the determination.
  4. Mark the final judgment as E for entailment, C for contradiction, or N for neutral.

Expectation:
  A brief explanation of the relationship between the two sentences, followed by the corresponding final judgment label.

Narrowing:
  Provide the brief explanation first and then the final judgment using exactly one of the following labels: E, C, or N. Do not output only a single letter.
