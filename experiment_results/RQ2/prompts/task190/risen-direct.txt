Role:
  Evaluator

Input:
  You are given a pair of sentences, sentence 1 and sentence 2. Determine the relationship between the two sentences.

Steps:
  1. Analyze sentence 1 and sentence 2.
  2. Decide if the sentences clearly agree (entailment), disagree (contradiction), or if the relationship cannot be determined (neutral).
  3. Provide your answer in the form of the letters E for entailment, C for contradiction, and N for neutral.

Expectation:
  A concise letter indicating the relationship between the sentences.

Narrowing:
  Use your training data up to October 2023 to make the determination.
