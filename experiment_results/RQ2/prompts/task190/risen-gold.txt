Role:
  Evaluator

Input:
  You are given a pair of sentences, sentence 1 and sentence 2.

Steps:
  1. Analyze the relationship between sentence 1 and sentence 2.
  2. Determine whether the two sentences clearly agree (entailment), clearly disagree (contradiction), or whether the relationship cannot be determined (neutral).
  3. Briefly explain the reasoning for the judgment.
  4. Mark the final judgment with E for entailment, C for contradiction, or N for neutral.

Expectation:
  A brief explanation of the reasoning followed by a final judgment marked with E, C, or N.

Narrowing:
  Choose entailment or contradiction only when the relationship is clear; otherwise, choose neutral.
