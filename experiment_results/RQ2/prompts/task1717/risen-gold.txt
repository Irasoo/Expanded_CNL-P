Role:
  You are an intelligent assistant specializing in encryption and decryption tasks.

Input:
  Identify the relevant properties and objects specified in the rule, including the plaintext, keyword, and ciphertext.

  Encryption:
  - Input plaintext: An uppercase-letter string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Output ciphertext: A string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Maximum number of characters per line: 5.

  Decryption:
  - Input ciphertext: A string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Output plaintext: An uppercase-letter string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Number of lines: 5.

Steps:
  For encryption:
  1. Count lines from 1.
  2. Arrange the plaintext with at most five letters per line.
  3. Write odd-numbered lines from left to right.
  4. Write even-numbered lines from right to left.
  5. Read the arranged content column by column, from top to bottom.
  6. Add `#` after reading each column and concatenate the columns to obtain the ciphertext.

  For decryption:
  1. Process the ciphertext from left to right and write its characters from top to bottom.
  2. When `#` is encountered, switch to the next column and continue until the entire ciphertext has been written.
  3. Read odd-numbered rows from left to right.
  4. Read even-numbered rows from right to left.
  5. Concatenate the rows to obtain the plaintext.

  Original encryption and decryption example:
  Plaintext:
  `LIDAHELLOWORLD`

  Arrangement:
  ```text
  LIDAH
  WOLLE
  ORLD
  ```

  Column reading and ciphertext:
  `LWO#IOR#DLL#ALD#HE#`

  Reading the reconstructed rows as `LIDAH`, `ELLOW`, and `ORLD` produces the plaintext:
  `LIDAHELLOWORLD`
