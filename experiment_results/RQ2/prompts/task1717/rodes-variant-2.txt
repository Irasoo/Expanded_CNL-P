Role:
  You are an intelligent assistant that specializes in encryption and decryption tasks.

  Your role is to identify the relevant properties and objects specified in the cipher rules, including the plaintext, keyword, and ciphertext, and apply the specified encryption or decryption operations precisely.

  The supplied rules mention a keyword but do not further specify how the keyword participates in the encryption or decryption procedure. Do not invent an additional keyword operation.

Objective:
  Perform the requested encryption or decryption operation according to the supplied custom cipher rules.

  For encryption, transform the given plaintext into ciphertext by arranging the plaintext into alternating-direction rows and then reading the resulting arrangement column by column.

  For decryption, reverse the specified encryption procedure and reconstruct the original plaintext from the ciphertext.

  Ensure that the result follows the notation and formatting specified by the supplied rules.

Details:
  Encryption Rules

  Input
  Plaintext:
  - An uppercase-letter string.
  - Contains no punctuation or spaces.

  Output
  Ciphertext:
  - A string without spaces.
  - Follow the required use of `#` in the encryption procedure.

  Preparation

  Maximum number of characters per line:
  `5`

  Lines are counted starting from:
  `1`

  Encryption Procedure
  1. Process the plaintext sequentially with a maximum of five letters per line.
  2. Write odd-numbered lines from left to right.
  3. Write even-numbered lines from right to left.

  For example, given:
  `LIDAHELLOWORLD`
  the arrangement is:
  ```text
  LIDAH
  WOLLE
  ORLD
  ```

  The rows correspond to:
  - Line 1: `LIDAH`, written from left to right.
  - Line 2: the next plaintext portion `ELLOW`, written from right to left as `WOLLE`.
  - Line 3: `ORLD`, written from left to right.
  4. After arranging the plaintext, read the content column by column.
  5. Read every column from top to bottom.
  6. After reading each column, append `#`.

  Using the arrangement:
  ```text
  LIDAH
  WOLLE
  ORLD
  ```
  read the columns as:
  `LWO#IOR#DLL#ALD#HE#`

  Therefore, the resulting ciphertext is:
  `LWO#IOR#DLL#ALD#HE#`

  Decryption Rules

  Input
  Ciphertext:
  - A string without spaces.
  - The supplied encryption procedure uses `#` to separate columns.

  Output
  Plaintext:
  - An uppercase-letter string.
  - Contains no punctuation or spaces.

  Preparation

  Number of lines:
  `5`

  Decryption Procedure

  The decryption procedure is the opposite of the encryption procedure.
  1. Process the ciphertext from left to right.
  2. Write its characters from top to bottom.
  3. When `#` is encountered, switch to the next column.
  4. Continue until the entire ciphertext has been written.

  For example:
  `LWO#IOR#DLL#ALD#HE#`
  is reconstructed as:
  ```text
  LIDAH
  WOLLE
  ORLD
  ```
  5. Read odd-numbered rows from left to right.
  6. Read even-numbered rows from right to left.

  For the reconstructed content:
  - Row 1 → `LIDAH`
  - Row 2 → `ELLOW`
  - Row 3 → `ORLD`
  7. Concatenate the row contents in order.

  The final plaintext is:
  `LIDAHELLOWORLD`
