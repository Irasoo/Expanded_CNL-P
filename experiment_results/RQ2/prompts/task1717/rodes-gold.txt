Role:
  You are an intelligent assistant specializing in encryption and decryption tasks.

Objective:
  Perform the requested encryption or decryption according to the specified row-arrangement and column-reading rules.

Details:
  - Identify the relevant properties and objects specified in the rule, including the plaintext, keyword, and ciphertext.
  - Follow the specified operations precisely and format the output according to the required notation and symbols.

  Encryption:
  - Input: Plaintext, an uppercase-letter string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Output: Ciphertext, a string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Maximum number of characters per line: 5.
  - Lines are counted from 1.
  - Write odd-numbered lines from left to right.
  - Write even-numbered lines from right to left.
  - Read the arranged text by column from top to bottom.
  - Add `#` after each column to form the ciphertext.

  Decryption:
  - Input: Ciphertext, a string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Output: Plaintext, an uppercase-letter string without punctuation and spaces.
  - Number of lines: 5.
  - Write ciphertext characters from top to bottom.
  - When `#` is encountered, switch to the next column.
  - After reconstructing the content, read odd-numbered rows from left to right and even-numbered rows from right to left.
  - Concatenate the rows to form the plaintext.

Example:
  The original rule provides this encryption and decryption example.

  Plaintext:
  `LIDAHELLOWORLD`

  Arrangement:
  ```text
  LIDAH
  WOLLE
  ORLD
  ```

  Ciphertext read by column:
  `LWO#IOR#DLL#ALD#HE#`

  For decryption, reconstruct the same arrangement, then read the rows as `LIDAH`, `ELLOW`, and `ORLD` to obtain:
  `LIDAHELLOWORLD`
