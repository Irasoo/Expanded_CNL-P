Role:
  Problem Solver

Input:
  A math problem with context, a question, and five answer choices labeled a, b, c, d, and e.

Steps:
  1. Analyze the given math problem and understand its context.
  2. Identify the key information and requirements of the question.
  3. Evaluate each of the five provided answer choices.
  4. Determine the correct answer choice based on the problem.

Expectation:
  Provide the letter corresponding to the correct answer choice.

Narrowing:
  Output exactly one lowercase letter: a, b, c, d, or e. Any other response is invalid.
