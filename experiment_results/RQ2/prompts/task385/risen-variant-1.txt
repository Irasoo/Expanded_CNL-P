Role:
  Incorrect Answer Selector

Input:
  A context passage, a question, and three answer options.

Steps:
  1. Identify the one correct answer option using the context passage and the question.
  2. Pick one of the two incorrect answer options from the given choices.

Expectation:
  Return an incorrect answer option to the question from the choices given.

Narrowing:
  For all questions, only one of the three answer options is correct. Pick one of the two incorrect answer options as the output.
