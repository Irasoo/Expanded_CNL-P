## Checkout Button Guardrail Test

- **Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text will increase purchase conversion rate.
- **Change tested:** Control used **“Continue”**; Variant used **“Buy Now.”**

- **Duration:** 14 days
- **Sample sizes:** Control **10,000**; Variant **10,000**

### Results
- **Control conversion:** 10.0% (1,000/10,000)
- **Variant conversion:** 12.0% (1,200/10,000)
- **Absolute difference:** **+2.0 percentage points**
- **Relative lift:** **+20.0%**
- **Two-sided p-value:** **0.00000619**
- **95% confidence interval (Variant minus Control):** **+1.13 to +2.87 percentage points**

The primary-metric increase is **statistically significant** because the p-value is below 0.05 and the confidence interval excludes zero. The test is labeled **underpowered**, but this is a limitation and does not override the significant result. **No sample ratio mismatch** was detected.

### Guardrail assessment
There is a **guardrail concern**: customer support contact rate increased from **2.0% to 5.0%**, which the product team considers a meaningful negative effect.

### Recommendation
**investigate**

The variant significantly improved purchase conversion, but the explicitly reported guardrail degradation requires investigation. Examine the support contacts associated with the increase, identify whether specific issues are linked to the variant experience, and assess potential mitigations before making a rollout decision.
