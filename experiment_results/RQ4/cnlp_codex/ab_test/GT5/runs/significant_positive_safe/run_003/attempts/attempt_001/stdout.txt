## Checkout Button Positive Test

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from **“Continue”** to **“Buy Now”** will increase purchase conversion rate.  
**Tested change:** Control used “Continue”; Variant used “Buy Now.”  
**Primary metric:** Purchase conversion rate (successful purchases / sample size).

### Results
- **Duration:** 14 days
- **Control:** 10,000 users; 1,000 purchases
- **Variant:** 10,000 users; 1,200 purchases
- **Control conversion rate:** 10.0%
- **Variant conversion rate:** 12.0%
- **Absolute difference:** +2.0 percentage points
- **Relative lift:** +20.0%

### Statistical assessment
- **Two-sided p-value:** 0.00000619
- **95% confidence interval for Variant minus Control:** +1.13 to +2.87 percentage points
- **Statistical significance:** Statistically significant (p < 0.05).
- **Power status:** Underpowered. This is diagnostic only; the observed result is nevertheless statistically significant.
- **SRM status:** No sample ratio mismatch detected. Traffic was split evenly, and the experiment is not inconclusive due to SRM.

### Guardrails
Revenue per user, page load time, and customer support contact rate were stable, with no meaningful negative effects observed. **No guardrail concern.**

### Recommendation: Ship
Ship the **“Buy Now”** checkout-button text. The Variant increased purchase conversion from 10.0% to 12.0%, a statistically significant improvement of 2.0 percentage points, with a 95% confidence interval that remains positive. No SRM or meaningful guardrail degradation was reported.
