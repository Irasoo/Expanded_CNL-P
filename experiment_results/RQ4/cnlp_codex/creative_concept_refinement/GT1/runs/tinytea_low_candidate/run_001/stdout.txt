==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Tiny Break: Five Cities, One Sip**

TinyTea turns a cold bottle into a globally recognizable three-second pause. In every city, a participant holds the chilled bottle beside their face, takes one sip, and moves it toward the camera until the TinyTea label fills the frame. That single transition links the celebrity, students, live events, billboards, AR experience, and social content without adding separate campaign mechanics.

On launch day, simultaneous public Tiny Break events take place in five major international cities. Each event uses the same compact setup: a sampling point, a marked filming position, and one custom interactive digital billboard. When a participant completes the bottle transition at the filming position, their image appears in the city’s rotating billboard sequence alongside clips from the other four events. The billboard prompt remains simple: “Take a Tiny Break.”

A globally famous celebrity appears in person at the principal event and performs the same ritual with a cold TinyTea, establishing the behavior students are asked to copy. One professional cinematic crew captures the celebrity performance, standardized bottle close-ups, and the five-city launch sequence as one master vertical film, with short edits prepared for Instagram, TikTok, and university community feeds.

The dedicated TinyTea AR application uses the bottle as its single trigger. Scanning the label opens a live miniature view of the five-city sequence, then lets students record their own three-second bottle transition. The resulting video uses the same label-fill ending, making participation immediately recognizable and keeping the cold, lightly sweetened tea at the center of every post.

The local student launch is built around one prompt: “Where do you take your Tiny Break?” University communities distribute the master film and invite students to recreate the ritual in libraries, study areas, cafés, and campus walkways. Sampling connects the online behavior to the product’s practical benefit: a lightly sweetened bottled tea served cold for a brief pause between classes or study sessions.

To fit the seven-day production window, every city uses the same pre-approved event layout, filming mark, billboard interaction, AR trigger, shot list, and vertical edit template. The five-city infrastructure, celebrity appearance, venues, travel, billboard placements, AR development, and professional crew are mandatory in-kind production prerequisites and must be contractually confirmed before the seven-day schedule begins. TinyTea’s sub-$1,000 campaign budget is reserved for locally printed prompts, sampling materials, and university-community distribution; the campaign does not proceed until those external commitments are secured.

=== Evaluation ===
Originality: 6/10
Strategic fit: 3/10
Audience impact: 6/10
Executability: 1/10
Overall score: 4.1/10
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The bottle-to-camera transition provides a clear visual system that connects the product, celebrity, events, billboards, AR, and social content, giving a familiar short-form participation mechanic a meaningful TinyTea-specific role. However, celebrity-led challenges, synchronized city activations, interactive billboards, and label-triggered AR are established approaches, so the concept is differentiated more by integration than by originality. The campus prompt, between-class occasion, cold serving cue, and reusable vertical format fit the student audience and awareness objective, but the international scale is disproportionate to a local launch and the lightly sweetened benefit receives little persuasive emphasis. The three-second ritual should be understandable and visually recognizable, although the complicated five-city and AR framework may distract from the simple local product message. Executability is the decisive weakness: a globally famous celebrity appearance, five international events, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, travel, venues, sampling, and professional cinematic production cannot credibly be delivered within seven days for less than $1,000. Treating all major costs and capabilities as mandatory in-kind prerequisites invents unprovided resources and leaves the campaign unable to proceed unless unsupported external commitments materialize.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the consistent bottle transition, cold-product close-up, and campus Tiny Break prompt, but produce a detailed seven-day execution specification showing how every mandatory element is created and paid for within the stated budget. Because the brief provides no partners or donated resources, do not assume in-kind celebrity, venue, travel, billboard, development, or production support. Clarify the celebrity's in-person role across the campaign, define a genuinely minimal but dedicated AR application, and specify one repeatable billboard interaction and event format. Strengthen the product message by making the lightly sweetened taste and cold between-class refreshment apparent in the central three-second behavior rather than only in supporting copy. As written, the mandatory requirements and budget are irreconcilable; the concept should explicitly flag that feasibility conflict instead of presenting unconfirmed external commitments as a production solution.
