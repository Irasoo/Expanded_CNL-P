==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: Keep It Light**

TinyTea makes one recognizable promise the center of its launch: when a student’s day becomes too much, take it back to something light, cold, and lightly sweetened. Every execution follows the same simple reveal. A crowded scene of notifications, assignments, plans, and visual clutter builds around a person. One swipe removes the excess, revealing a cold bottle of TinyTea and the line: **“Keep it light.”**

A globally famous celebrity appears in person at the campaign’s live launch and performs the reveal in a short, professionally produced hero film. The same compact format is repeated at live public events in five major international cities, where custom interactive digital billboards show an overcomplicated student-day scene. People use the dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application to sweep away the clutter on screen; the billboard responds with the same final reveal: a cold TinyTea bottle, the product benefit, and **“Keep it light.”**

The campaign is built from one reusable visual system: one reveal sequence, one line, one bottle payoff, and one short AR interaction. The billboard artwork, AR effect, event format, and social edits are adapted from the same master creative so the execution remains consistent and repeatable within the seven-day production window. Each city produces short vertical clips of the public reveal for Instagram and TikTok, while local university communities recreate the format with familiar moments such as exam-week notifications, overloaded group chats, and crowded study plans resolving into a cold TinyTea.

TinyTea is never incidental: the clutter represents the pressure of student life, while the cold, lightly sweetened bottle is the visible reward and the final meaning of “light.” The celebrity and international events create launch attention; the simple AR reveal and student-made versions turn that attention into a recognizable first-month awareness device for local university students.

=== Evaluation ===
Originality: 6/10
Strategic fit: 3/10
Audience impact: 6/10
Executability: 1/10
Overall score: 4.1/10
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The clutter-to-light reveal is a coherent, differentiated device tied to the lightly sweetened, cold product, but swiping away digital clutter and using a broad 'Keep it light' platform are familiar ideas, so originality is solid rather than exceptional. The concept clearly depicts the bottle, product benefit, student pressures, and social formats relevant to local university students. However, its international celebrity-and-five-city emphasis is disproportionate to the local awareness objective, and it does not credibly reconcile the mandatory celebrity appearance, five international live events, custom interactive billboards, dedicated AR app, cinematic production, seven-day deadline, and total budget below $1,000. Those direct conflicts sharply reduce Strategic Fit and make Executability effectively unworkable. The relatable exam, notification, and group-chat scenarios should be clear and engaging for students, but the emotional hook is familiar and the inaccessible international spectacle may weaken local relevance.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the single clutter-to-bottle reveal, the 'Keep it light' line, and the student-specific social adaptations, but provide a concrete production design showing how every mandatory element can exist within the stated budget and seven-day schedule. This would require verified no-fee access to the celebrity, events, billboards, AR development, travel, venues, and professional production; because the brief supplies none of those resources, the concept cannot currently be made credible without changing the brief or securing documented donated support. Strengthen audience impact by making local university students the primary participants and by defining one sharper, repeatable behavior around serving TinyTea cold. Reduce the narrative emphasis on international spectacle and explain exactly how footage and interactions from all five cities translate into locally recognizable Instagram, TikTok, and campus-community content during the first launch month.
