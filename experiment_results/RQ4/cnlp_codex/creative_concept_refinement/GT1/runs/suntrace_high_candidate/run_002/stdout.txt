=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Creative Concept: “Leave a SunTrace”**

SunTrace makes the invisible reason to reapply visible at the moment young adults naturally pause outdoors. Each short vertical video follows one person through one familiar campus activity: applying SunTrace SPF 50, placing one included UV-sensitive sticker on the skin, stepping outside, then checking the sticker at the first break.

The video opens with a tight close-up of the sticker being removed. The surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker’s shape while the area beneath it remains unchanged. A clear on-screen label appears during the reveal: **“UV exposure reminder. Not a damage measurement.”** This makes the demonstration’s purpose explicit without suggesting that it measures exact UV damage or proves sunscreen performance.

The video then quickly shows the sequence that created the reveal: **apply SunTrace before going out, place the sticker, start the activity, pause, remove the sticker, and reapply SunTrace according to the product label before returning outside.** The product remains central throughout: SunTrace SPF 50 is the protection applied before and during the day, while the included sticker turns an invisible exposure reminder into a visible pause cue.

The repeatable format is **“Pause. Check your SunTrace. Keep going.”** Every execution uses one participant, one sticker, one ordinary university setting, and one natural break, such as between classes, after a game, or during an outdoor lunch. Close framing of the sticker area, consistent daylight, and a pre-tested activity duration keep the reveal legible without requiring special locations, extra equipment, or complicated continuity.

The closing text reads: **“Invisible protection is easy to forget. Make your next outdoor pause a SunTrace check.”** The visual ritual gives young adults a simple behavior they can repeat: when they stop, they remember to reapply before continuing their day outside.

Evaluation reasons: The concept earns strong originality because it turns SunTrace's distinctive sticker demonstration into a memorable reveal and a repeatable pause ritual, rather than relying on generic sunscreen education. The product-specific title and opening close-up add distinction, although the basic reveal is inherent to the product rather than a wholly new creative mechanism. Strategic fit is strong: SunTrace SPF 50 remains central, the sequence directly addresses forgotten reapplication, campus activities suit the 18-30 audience, and the explicit disclaimer respects the prohibition on presenting the demonstration as an exact measure of UV damage. Audience impact is high because the reveal is immediate, visually clear, and connected to a simple behavior at natural outdoor breaks. Executability is solid within short vertical video and a low-to-medium budget, using one participant, an ordinary location, and real product materials. However, reliably producing a clear temporary reveal under changing daylight, skin conditions, activity durations, and sunscreen application introduces practical variability. The multi-step sequence and disclaimer may also become dense in a very short edit.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the reveal-first structure and pause cue, but simplify each video to one activity, one break, and three unmistakable beats: reveal, reapply, continue. Establish a repeatable shooting protocol using only the allowed product and stickers, including consistent framing, label-compliant timing, and conditions in which the real demonstration is reliably visible; avoid implying that a pre-tested duration predicts UV exposure or reapplication needs. Keep the disclaimer readable for long enough to register and use restrained wording throughout so the sticker is clearly an exposure reminder rather than a performance test or damage measurement. Strengthen the behavioral hook by making the same natural pause trigger and closing line consistent across every campus execution.
