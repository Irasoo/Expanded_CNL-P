==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The One-Minute Cold Break, Everywhere**

TinyTea turns its cold, lightly sweetened bottled tea into a shared one-minute reset for university students: open a cold bottle, pause for one minute, and invite someone nearby to join.

During the first seven days after launch, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live public TinyTea event in each of five major international cities. Every event uses the same compact format: the celebrity opens a cold TinyTea, starts a visible 60-second countdown, and prompts the crowd to take the same one-minute break. The format is designed as one repeatable production system, with no additional performances, storylines, or event mechanics required from city to city.

A custom interactive digital billboard in each city carries the same countdown interface, adapted only with the relevant city identifier. Passersby activate or join the one-minute countdown and see the celebrity-led cold-break prompt. The dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application contains one simple experience: students point it at a cold TinyTea bottle to start the same 60-second countdown, record their break, and share it.

Professional cinematic production follows one locked shot sequence across all five events: bottle opened cold, countdown started, celebrity and students pausing, and the final invitation to repeat the break. The same edit structure supplies Instagram and TikTok content, while local university communities use the identical prompt to create student-led versions during the month after launch.

TinyTea remains the essential action in every execution. The celebrity, public events, billboards, AR application, cinematic films, and student posts all demonstrate one recognizable behavior: take a one-minute break with a cold, lightly sweetened TinyTea. By using one ritual, one countdown, one AR interaction, one billboard system, and one cinematic format across five cities, the concept keeps the mandatory global presence and seven-day production window focused on a single repeatable idea rather than multiple custom activations.

Evaluation reasons:
The one-minute cold-break ritual is clear, memorable, and meaningfully tied to opening and drinking a cold TinyTea, giving the product a central role rather than using it as incidental placement. The single countdown creates useful consistency across events, billboards, the augmented-reality application, cinematic content, Instagram, TikTok, and university communities. Strategic fit is weakened because the five-city international activation is disproportionate to the local awareness objective and local university audience, although the concept correctly preserves every mandatory requirement and extends the ritual into local university communities. The simple, participatory behavior should be understandable and shareable for students aged 18-25, but the concept does not yet establish a specifically local or university-relevant reason to participate beyond taking a generic short break. Executability is critically weak: five international in-person celebrity appearances, five custom interactive digital billboards, a dedicated augmented-reality application, professional cinematic production, and all production within seven days cannot realistically be delivered for a total production budget below $1,000. Repetition reduces creative complexity but does not resolve the fundamental costs, logistics, development, travel, media access, or production-time conflicts created by the mandatory constraints.

Refinement suggestions:
Preserve the one-minute cold-break ritual, the cold bottle opening, the countdown, and the unified visual sequence because these are the concept's strongest and most product-specific elements. Strengthen local audience relevance by making the month-long university-community participation the narrative destination of every international execution: each celebrity event, billboard interaction, AR recording, and social edit should end with the same direct invitation for local students to perform and share the TinyTea break on campus. Define one concise participation prompt and one fixed vertical-video template so the behavior is immediately recognizable on Instagram and TikTok without adding production requirements. For executability, specify the minimum viable version of every mandatory element through a single locked script, identical event run-of-show, reusable billboard interface, one-function AR application, and one cinematic shot list. However, no refinement can credibly reconcile all mandatory elements with the below-$1,000 budget and seven-day completion window; those constraints remain mutually incompatible even at minimum scope and should be explicitly identified as an unresolved feasibility condition rather than obscured by claims of production efficiency.
