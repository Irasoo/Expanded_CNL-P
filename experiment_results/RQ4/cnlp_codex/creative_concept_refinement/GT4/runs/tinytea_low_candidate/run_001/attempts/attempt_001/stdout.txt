==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: Small Bottle, Big Chill**

TinyTea launches with one repeatable ritual: **open, sip, hand off the chill.** A globally famous celebrity appears in person at live public events in five major international cities across seven days, opening a chilled bottle of TinyTea, taking a sip, and handing the bottle toward the camera as if passing it directly to the next city. The final handoff lands with local university students, making the global sequence resolve in TinyTea's actual launch community.

Professional cinematic production treats every appearance as one chapter of the same handoff, using the bottle opening, visible cold serve, and sip as the consistent visual language. This keeps the lightly sweetened bottled tea central rather than making the international scale the message: the recurring payoff is that TinyTea is ready to serve cold whenever the handoff reaches you.

Each city's custom interactive billboard shows the current handoff moment. When people interact, the bottle is passed onward to the next location and directs them to the dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application. In the app, users place the TinyTea bottle in their surroundings, activate the traveling chill signal, and record their own handoff clip for Instagram and TikTok.

TinyTea's local Instagram, TikTok, and university-community activity follows the five-city sequence as a countdown to the campus handoff. Students are invited to create their own cold-serve handoff clips, showing where they would open and serve TinyTea cold. The first-month awareness objective is supported by a single recognizable action that is repeated across the celebrity appearances, live events, billboards, AR application, cinematic content, and student social participation.

This is a conditional creative platform: production can proceed only when the mandatory celebrity, five live international events, five custom billboards, dedicated AR application, cinematic production, and seven-day delivery are confirmed with resources beyond the stated below-$1,000 production budget.

Evaluation reasons:
The handoff device gives the concept a single, understandable visual system and keeps the cold bottle, opening, and sip visible across executions. However, celebrity-led global handoffs, interactive billboards, and AR participation are familiar launch conventions rather than a distinctive expression of TinyTea's specific benefit, so originality is limited. The concept does connect to local university students through a campus resolution and social handoff clips, but the primary scale and attention are directed toward five international cities rather than the stated local awareness objective. It also does not create a particularly strong reason for students to notice or share beyond participating in a broad handoff trend. Most critically, the mandatory celebrity appearance, five international live events, custom billboards, dedicated AR app, professional cinematic production, and seven-day delivery fundamentally conflict with the below-$1,000 total production budget. The conditional caveat acknowledges this conflict but does not make the requested campaign executable under the supplied brief.

Refinement suggestions:
Preserve the repeatable 'open, sip, hand off the chill' action because it provides a coherent product-centered through-line. To improve strategic fit and audience impact, make the local university student handoff the narrative focus in all social and community communications, with each global execution explicitly resolving into local campus awareness rather than functioning as the main story. Define a more specific, immediately legible student-facing payoff within the existing cold-serve ritual, using only the stated benefit. To improve executability, the concept needs a concrete operating plan that reconciles every mandatory international, celebrity, billboard, AR, cinematic, and seven-day requirement with the stated budget; based on the brief as written, that reconciliation is not feasible. The concept should clearly identify this irreconcilable constraint to stakeholders before production rather than treating unconfirmed outside resources as part of the executable plan.
