=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The Cold-Sip Signal**

TinyTea turns one small, recognizable action into a shared launch signal for university students: take a cold sip, pause, and raise the bottle. The gesture makes the product benefit immediately visible while preserving the current concept’s contrast between a small local bottle and a global-sized moment.

The campaign opens with a professional cinematic film of a globally famous celebrity appearing in person at a TinyTea launch event. The celebrity takes a cold sip of lightly sweetened TinyTea and raises the bottle. This starts the **Cold-Sip Signal** across live public events in five major international cities. At each event, students and attendees repeat the same gesture with TinyTea. Custom interactive digital billboards in each city respond to the collective bottle raise with the message: **“TinyTea. Make the cold moment count.”**

Every execution uses the same compact creative system: one cold sip, one bottle-raise gesture, and one message. The dedicated augmented-reality mobile application mirrors the signal by letting users point their phones at a TinyTea bottle or campaign content to activate the bottle-raise animation and connect their university community to the five-city launch. Users are prompted to share their own Cold-Sip Signal on Instagram and TikTok.

Local university communities are the campaign’s center of participation. During TinyTea’s first month, student-facing posts and short clips invite them to join the gesture, recognize the bottle in social content, and share a cold-sip moment with their peers. The celebrity and international events provide scale, while the simple repeatable action keeps TinyTea and its cold, lightly sweetened taste at the heart of the idea.

The production is designed as one synchronized, modular system for the required seven-day schedule: a single master cinematic treatment, one repeated event action, one adaptable interactive billboard behavior, and one focused AR interaction. The celebrity appearance, five-city live events, custom interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day completion remain mandatory conditions of the concept. With the stated production budget below $1,000 and no confirmed access or donated resources, this remains a creative direction that requires those conditions and resources to be secured before production.

Evaluation reasons:
The Cold-Sip Signal is a clear, repeatable product-specific gesture that makes the cold-serving benefit visible and gives TinyTea a recognizable social behavior, supporting a moderately strong originality score. Its strongest strategic element is the direct connection between taking a cold sip, raising the bottle, and the lightly sweetened iced tea experience. However, the concept gives disproportionate emphasis to a globally famous celebrity, five major international cities, international live events, digital billboards, and a dedicated AR application, while the brief identifies local university students and local university communities as the audience and sets a very low budget. This weakens strategic fit because the required scale does not clearly serve the stated local awareness objective efficiently. The gesture is immediately understandable and adaptable to Instagram, TikTok, and student communities, so it could generate attention and participation, but the international spectacle may distract from TinyTea's local launch and the audience impact is therefore moderate rather than exceptional. Executability is critically weak: the concept requires a celebrity appearing in person, five-city live events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days, all within a production budget below $1,000 and without confirmed access to donated resources. The concept acknowledges this conflict but does not resolve it, so the mandatory execution cannot currently be treated as practical.

Refinement suggestions:
Preserve the Cold-Sip Signal, its single gesture, and its direct link to the cold bottled tea benefit. Make local university participation the narrative and visual center: frame the celebrity and five-city events as the required trigger for a signal that is completed by TinyTea's local student communities, with every social prompt directing attention back to local campus participation during the first month. Keep the message and interaction system as simple as possible so the idea remains coherent across Instagram, TikTok, live events, billboards, and AR. To improve strategic precision, define the student-facing invitation around a specific cold-sip moment that naturally fits university community activity, without adding unprovided product claims or incentives. The fundamental executability problem cannot be solved through creative refinement while all mandatory constraints, the sub-$1,000 budget, the seven-day schedule, and the absence of confirmed resources remain unchanged. The concept should therefore be presented as contingent on securing the required celebrity access, international event and billboard resources, AR development capability, cinematic production, and simultaneous production capacity; otherwise its score must remain very low even after the creative system is tightened.
