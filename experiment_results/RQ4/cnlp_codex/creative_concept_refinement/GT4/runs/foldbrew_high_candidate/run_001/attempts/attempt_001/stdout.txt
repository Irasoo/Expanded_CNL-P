=== Final Creative Concept ===
## Don’t Open It. Drop It.

FoldBrew turns the most familiar instruction in instant coffee on its head: instead of opening the package, the user drops it directly into hot water. The campaign builds memorability around one simple command, **“Don’t open it. Drop it.”**, making the sleeve’s disappearance the visual proof of the product benefit.

The principal execution is a series of tightly framed 6–15 second vertical videos set in ordinary office environments. Each begins with a hand holding a FoldBrew sleeve over a cup while appearing ready to tear it open. On-screen text interrupts: **“Don’t open it.”** The entire sleeve is then placed into hot water. The camera stays close as the packaging visibly dissolves and the water becomes coffee, followed by the end line: **“FoldBrew. The package becomes the coffee.”**

Variations keep the same recognizable structure while using familiar workplace moments: arriving at the desk, preparing for a meeting, or taking a short afternoon break. Some films open with the question **“Where’s the coffee?”** before revealing that it is inside the dissolving sleeve; others use a continuous close-up or seamless loop so the dissolve can be watched repeatedly. Every version ends with the practical benefit stated plainly: **“No machine. No pod. No disposable sachet.”**

For Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video, the dissolve is always shown clearly in real time or through a concise close-up sequence, making the unusual behavior the campaign’s consistent visual signature. FoldBrew is not merely featured in the content: its package performs the central action and becomes the drink itself. This gives urban office workers a fast, lower-waste alternative to their usual takeaway coffee routine while turning first trial into a simple, distinctive act they can immediately remember: **don’t open it; drop it.**
