=== Final Creative Concept ===
# **TinyTea: The Seven-Second Tiny Break**

## **Core Idea**

A globally famous celebrity interrupts a visibly demanding international schedule for one deliberately small ritual: a seven-second break with a cold TinyTea.

At every activation, the same countdown and product action communicate the benefit immediately:

**“Seven seconds. Cold tea. Light sweetness. Back to it.”**

The contrast between a five-city global production and one tiny, repeatable break makes the idea specific to TinyTea. For students moving between classes, the celebrity’s ritual becomes a recognizable social format they can recreate without needing access to the international events.

The campaign retains a strict launch gate: production begins only after documentary evidence confirms that every mandatory element can be completed within seven days and that total campaign production spending will remain below `$1,000`.

## **Main Execution**

In each of the five required international cities, the celebrity appears in person at a live public event and starts the same seven-second ritual. A custom interactive digital billboard displays a large countdown as the celebrity opens a cold TinyTea, takes a sip, and delivers the campaign line. The event then resumes immediately, reinforcing TinyTea as a small, refreshing pause rather than a lengthy escape.

The dedicated augmented-reality application reproduces this interaction. Pointing the app at a TinyTea bottle starts the seven-second countdown and reveals the words **“Cold tea. Light sweetness.”** This creates one consistent mechanism across the celebrity appearance, events, billboards, application, and social content.

Professional cinematic crews capture each event vertically as well as in standard formats. The primary Instagram and TikTok edits open immediately on the celebrity stopping mid-schedule, followed by the countdown, the cold bottle, the sip, and the benefit line. Each edit identifies its city but ends by bringing the behavior back to local students: **“Take your Tiny Break between classes.”**

Local university communities receive short city edits, still images, and a seven-second posting format timed for the first month after launch. Students can recreate the ritual with TinyTea and the same countdown, allowing the required international production to generate a simple, locally relevant awareness behavior.

## **Seven-Day Production Path**

The seven-day clock starts only after the launch gate is passed:

- **Day 1: Creative and legal lock.** Approve the celebrity script, product presentation, five event plans, billboard interaction, AR behavior, social templates, and usage rights.
- **Day 2: Shared interaction build.** Produce the master seven-second countdown and adapt it for the billboards, AR application, event screens, and social edits.
- **Day 3: Technical rehearsal.** Test the application, all five billboard installations, event cues, product handling, capture workflow, and publishing pipeline.
- **Days 4-6: Live activation and production.** Complete all five celebrity appearances and events, with footage transferred to post-production immediately after each appearance.
- **Day 7: Final delivery and release.** Complete professional edits, verify rights and platform delivery, and distribute the localized assets to Instagram, TikTok, and university communities.

## **Launch Gate**

Before Day 1, TinyTea’s campaign owner, legal approver, and production lead must sign a single dependency matrix. Passing the gate requires all of the following evidence:

| Mandatory element | Minimum evidence required |
|---|---|
| Celebrity | Executed appearance and usage-rights agreement covering all five cities, plus confirmed travel, security, and seven-day availability |
| Five live events | Executed venue agreements, permits, staffing plans, public-safety approvals, and fixed production schedules |
| Interactive billboards | Confirmed inventory in all five cities, approved technical specifications, installation commitments, and interaction test plans |
| Dedicated AR application | Executed development scope, named delivery team, working prototype, testing plan, distribution route, and seven-day release commitment |
| Cinematic production | Confirmed crews, equipment, capture schedule, post-production capacity, delivery formats, and footage rights |
| Budget | Signed cost ledger demonstrating that the complete production remains below `$1,000`, supported by written no-cost or in-kind agreements for every resource not paid from that budget |
| Schedule | One integrated critical path showing that the celebrity, events, billboards, application, filming, approvals, and delivery can all be completed within seven days |

Any missing or conditional evidence produces an immediate no-go decision. TinyTea does not spend the budget, announce the activation, imply celebrity participation, or publish a launch date. In that case, the completed deliverable is the signed feasibility decision and dependency matrix, not an unsubstantiated awareness campaign.

## **Product And Brand Role**

TinyTea is the essential action in every version of the idea. The celebrity’s break begins by opening the bottle, the tea is served cold, and the line explicitly communicates light sweetness. The billboard and AR interactions only activate around the same TinyTea ritual, while every social edit keeps the bottle, sip, and benefit visible within seven seconds.

## **Audience And Objective Connection**

The short countdown reflects the limited breaks university students have between classes and gives them an easy format to recognize and repeat on Instagram and TikTok. The celebrity and five-city events supply the initial awareness hook, while localized university distribution turns that spectacle into a relevant campus behavior during TinyTea’s first month. The repeated line makes both product benefits clear without adding competing messages: **cold tea, light sweetness, and a tiny break before getting back to the day.**

=== Evaluation ===
Originality: 7/10
Strategic fit: 4/10
Audience impact: 6/10
Executability: 1/10
Weighted overall score: 4.70/10
Weak criteria: executability
Reasons: The seven-second ritual is a clear, product-connected organizing idea, and the contrast between an oversized international activation and a tiny break gives the familiar celebrity-and-countdown format a memorable twist. TinyTea remains central through the bottle opening, cold serving cue, sip, and lightly sweetened benefit line. Strategic fit is weakened severely by the conflict between the mandatory scope and the production budget below $1,000: the launch gate acknowledges that conflict but does not solve it, and a no-go decision would not achieve first-month awareness. The concept otherwise connects reasonably well to students, short-form social channels, and between-class occasions. The seven-second format should be easy to understand and recreate, but the audience impact is uncertain because no celebrity-to-student relevance is established, the international events are remote from the local audience, and the ritual offers limited participation motivation beyond imitation. Execution is not credible under the supplied budget and seven-day requirement. Five international events, in-person celebrity appearances, custom billboards, a dedicated AR application, rights, travel, security, permits, crews, and cinematic post-production cannot realistically be delivered for the stated production spend. Unspecified in-kind support cannot be treated as available, while moving agreements, prototypes, and planning ahead of Day 1 also makes the claimed seven-day production path less convincing.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the seven-second TinyTea ritual, immediate product visibility, and single benefit message, but make the student response more purposeful by adding one consistent, low-friction action tied to university communities, such as posting a seven-second between-class TinyTea break through the required AR application. Specify how the selected celebrity is relevant to local students and ensure every international edit quickly connects the spectacle to a recognizable campus moment. Define the seven-day period honestly to include every activity that qualifies as production, including application development, technical adaptation, rehearsals, filming, and post-production. Because no mandatory element may be removed, do not present the campaign as executable unless binding agreements document that the celebrity, venues, billboard inventory, development, travel, security, rights, and production resources are genuinely available within the sub-$1,000 total. If that evidence cannot be obtained, the concept should be labeled infeasible rather than implying that the launch gate itself fulfills the awareness objective.
