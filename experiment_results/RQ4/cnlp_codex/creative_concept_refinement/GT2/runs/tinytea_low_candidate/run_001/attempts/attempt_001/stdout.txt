==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

===== 最终创意概念 =====
**TinyTea “Pause Before the Next Thing”**

TinyTea turns its recognizable cold-moment ritual into a shared student reset: open a chilled bottle, take a sip, and pause before the next class, study block, or meetup. The globally famous celebrity performs this same moment in person across five major international cities, making the pause a simple launch signal that local university students can recognize and recreate.

The principal execution is a seven-day **Pause Before the Next Thing Relay**. At one live public TinyTea event in each city, the celebrity opens a bottle of lightly sweetened TinyTea, takes a cold sip, pauses, and delivers the invitation: **“Take your TinyTea pause before the next thing.”** Professional cinematic production captures the identical sequence at every event, emphasizing the cold bottle, the sip, and the brief pause. The resulting short-form edits are distributed through Instagram, TikTok, and local university communities during TinyTea’s first month after launch.

Each city also features a custom interactive digital billboard using the same focused journey. A student interacts with the billboard, sees the celebrity take the TinyTea pause, and receives one prompt to recreate it. The dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application then overlays the celebrity’s pause moment when activated from the billboard or event material and lets the student record their own version for sharing on Instagram or TikTok.

The product remains essential to the idea: the pause begins with a real chilled bottle of lightly sweetened iced tea, and the visual payoff is the contrast between the student’s busy moment and the brief, refreshing cold sip. The international events provide a common launch signal, while the student-facing meaning stays local and immediate: TinyTea is the bottle used to pause before whatever comes next.

All five cities use one script, one bottle action, one cinematic treatment, one billboard interaction, one AR behavior, and one sharing prompt. This keeps the mandatory celebrity appearances, live events, interactive billboards, AR application, and professional production focused on a single repeatable idea, while connecting each city’s execution to local university communities and extending awareness through student-created Instagram and TikTok videos throughout the first month.

弱项: executability
评估理由: The shared pause ritual is clearly connected to TinyTea's cold, lightly sweetened bottle and gives the product a repeatable role across Instagram, TikTok, and university communities. The student situation is immediate and understandable, and the celebrity, live events, billboards, and AR participation provide attention hooks. However, the relay structure and celebrity-led global event format are familiar campaign devices rather than exceptionally distinctive ideas. Strategic fit is weakened by the mismatch between a small local beverage company targeting local university students and a five-city international activation. Executability is critically weak: the concept accepts mandatory celebrity appearances in five cities, live public events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day completion despite the total production budget being below $1,000. The concept does not provide a credible way to satisfy those simultaneous constraints.
优化建议: Preserve the simple TinyTea pause ritual, the cold-sip visual payoff, the single invitation, and the consistent student participation prompt. Make every mandatory element express exactly that one action, with no additional story, features, or production complexity. Before refining the creative further, establish a feasible production and rights plan that proves the globally famous celebrity, five in-person events, five custom interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day timeline can all be delivered within the stated budget. If that proof is unavailable, the brief contains an irresolvable executability conflict; raising the budget, extending the timeline, or removing a mandatory requirement would be necessary, and those changes cannot be made within the current brief.
