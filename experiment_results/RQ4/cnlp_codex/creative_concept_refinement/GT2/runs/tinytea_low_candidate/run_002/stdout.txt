==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

===== 最终创意概念 =====
**TinyTea: The Five-City Cold Open**

TinyTea turns one simple study-break action into a synchronized global relay: open a chilled bottle, take a lightly sweetened sip, and reset before the next class, assignment, or late-night study session. The bottle-opening sound, cold bottle, and short prompt, “Take your Cold Open,” remain the campaign’s single recognizable device.

Within the seven-day production window, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at a brief live Cold Open event in each of five major international cities. Every appearance uses the same tightly scripted sequence: the celebrity opens a chilled TinyTea bottle, takes a sip, says “Take your Cold Open,” and starts a countdown to the next city. The sequence is captured as professional cinematic footage using the same compact shot structure and visual treatment at every event, allowing one master relay film and short vertical edits for Instagram and TikTok.

Custom interactive digital billboards in each city show the synchronized countdown, the celebrity’s Cold Open moment, and the chilled TinyTea bottle. Each billboard directs viewers to TinyTea’s dedicated augmented-reality mobile application. The app has one focused interaction: when a user points it at a TinyTea bottle, campaign video, or billboard, it triggers the Cold Open countdown and records the user opening a bottle and taking a sip.

Local university communities extend the same relay with short, repeatable Cold Open posts between classes or study sessions. Students use the app to create their version, then share it on Instagram and TikTok with the Cold Open cue. The five-city events, billboards, cinematic film, AR application, and university participation all use the same bottle-opening action, countdown, and lightly sweetened chilled-tea message, keeping TinyTea at the center of every required execution while building first-month awareness among local university students.

===== 创意评估 =====

弱项: executability
评估理由: The synchronized Cold Open relay is a differentiated, product-connected device: the bottle-opening sound, chilled serving cue, countdown, and repeatable sip ritual give TinyTea a recognizable role rather than treating it as incidental placement. Strategic fit is mixed. The concept consistently communicates chilled, lightly sweetened tea and provides university students with a simple social format, but the five-city international emphasis is disproportionate to the objective of awareness among local university students. Audience impact benefits from a clear action, celebrity visibility, short-form video adaptability, and a study-break context, although requiring students to use a dedicated app adds substantial friction to participation. Executability is critically weak. A globally famous celebrity making five in-person appearances, five international live events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days cannot realistically be delivered for a total production budget below $1,000. The concept includes every mandatory element, but scripting them tightly does not resolve their cost, travel, development, permitting, coordination, or production requirements.
优化建议: Preserve the Cold Open name, bottle-opening ritual, chilled-tea cue, countdown, consistent visual structure, and between-class student context. Improve strategic fit by making local university participation the narrative destination of the five-city relay, with every celebrity appearance, billboard interaction, cinematic edit, and AR response explicitly handing the countdown toward the local campus launch. Reduce participation friction by ensuring the AR application produces an immediately shareable vertical post through one focused interaction. However, the brief is internally infeasible: no honest refinement can make all mandatory international, celebrity, billboard, application, cinematic, and seven-day requirements executable below $1,000. The budget, timeline, or mandatory execution list must change for the concept to reach the quality threshold without inventing donated resources, partnerships, or existing technology.
