==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

===== 最终创意概念 =====
## TinyTea: Cold Across the World, Sipped Here

TinyTea turns one cold sip into a synchronized five-city launch created specifically for local university students. A globally famous celebrity appears in person at live public events in five major international cities, holding a cold bottle of lightly sweetened TinyTea and leading each crowd in the same three-second “cold sip” countdown. The countdown ends with a direct invitation to local students: **“Five cities. One cold sip. Your campus next.”**

Professional cinematic production captures the five appearances as one launch film, then reformats the footage into short Instagram and TikTok edits tailored for distribution through local university communities during TinyTea’s first month. Every edit opens immediately on the celebrity raising the bottle and the three-second countdown, making TinyTea, its cold serving ritual, and its lightly sweetened tea benefit clear even without sound.

Custom interactive digital billboards in all five cities extend the same countdown. Each billboard prompts people to raise a TinyTea bottle, then responds with the campaign’s cold-sip visual effect and the line **“Five cities. One cold sip. TinyTea.”** The resulting moments become additional short-form footage for campus-facing social posts, connecting the international spectacle back to the students TinyTea needs to reach locally.

A dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application keeps participation to one minimal journey: open, scan a billboard or TinyTea bottle, and record the three-second cold-sip countdown. The scan reveals the celebrity countdown footage and applies the same recognizable cold-sip effect used on the billboards and in the launch film. Students can then publish the recording to Instagram or TikTok, giving university communities a simple, repeatable version of the global activation.

TinyTea remains the link across every execution: the celebrity raises it, the billboards respond to it, the application recognizes it, and students repeat its cold-sip ritual. The campaign line is **“Five cities. One cold sip. TinyTea.”**

Activation is contingent on formal approval to change at least one conflicting production condition. A globally famous celebrity appearing in five international cities, five live events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated augmented-reality application, and professional cinematic production cannot all be delivered within seven days for a total production budget below $1,000. Until the budget, timeline, or mandatory scope is formally revised, this concept remains a production-ready creative direction rather than a credibly executable launch plan.

评估理由: The synchronized three-second cold-sip ritual is a coherent, memorable device that connects the celebrity, events, billboards, application, social content, and product, although the global countdown and participation-challenge format are recognizable approaches rather than exceptionally original ones. Strategic fit is strengthened by TinyTea's central visual role, clear communication of cold serving and light sweetness, short-form social adaptations, and distribution through university communities. However, the international spectacle is weakly connected to awareness among local university students, and the concept does not explain why that audience would download a dedicated application. Audience impact benefits from an immediate visual action, celebrity attention, and a repeatable social format, but the message focuses more on campaign scale than on a compelling student-relevant reason to notice TinyTea. Executability is critically low: the concept correctly acknowledges that five international live events, an in-person globally famous celebrity, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated augmented-reality application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days cannot credibly be delivered for less than $1,000. Labeling the idea contingent or production-ready does not satisfy the mandatory launch requirements.
优化建议: Preserve the three-second cold-sip ritual, consistent campaign line, immediate bottle visibility, and unified visual system. Strengthen audience relevance by making every celebrity prompt and social edit explicitly address the local university community and by giving students a clearer reason to repeat the ritual without inventing a new product benefit. Simplify the application's stated user journey and specify how the first-month Instagram, TikTok, and university-community content flows from the required executions. Executability cannot be raised to a credible level while every mandatory element, the sub-$1,000 budget, and the seven-day deadline remain unchanged. A compliant refinement therefore requires formal revision of at least the budget, timeline, or mandatory scope; no creative rewording can resolve that production conflict.
