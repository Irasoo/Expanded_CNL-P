==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

===== 最终创意概念 =====
**The Coffee You Don’t Throw Away**

FoldBrew turns an everyday office habit into a visual surprise: instead of removing and discarding the coffee packaging, the user drops the entire sleeve into hot water and watches it become the drink.

The principal execution is a series of tight, ordinary product videos built around a familiar takeaway-coffee reflex. An office worker holds a FoldBrew sleeve over a waste bin as if about to discard it. The action pauses, then reverses: the sleeve moves to a clear cup of hot water. In close-up, it visibly dissolves as the water transforms into coffee. On-screen copy follows the action: **“Don’t bin the sleeve. Brew it.”** The film ends on the finished coffee beside a clean desk and the FoldBrew name, followed by: **“The package becomes the coffee. Try FoldBrew.”**

Short-form variations begin with direct prompts such as **“Where does the coffee end and the package begin?”** or **“Watch this sleeve become your coffee.”** Each version keeps the dissolve uninterrupted and central, using clear vessels, close product framing, and quick cuts between common office moments such as arriving at a desk, returning from a meeting, or taking an afternoon break.

For Instagram, a product-photo carousel shows the three-step transformation: FoldBrew sleeve, sleeve dissolving in hot water, finished coffee. TikTok and Reels use the waste-bin fake-out as the opening hook before revealing the preparation behavior. FoldBrew is always the active mechanism, not a background prop: its packaging eliminates the need for a machine, pod, or disposable sachet by becoming the serving itself.

The idea speaks to urban office workers through a recognizable workday ritual while making FoldBrew’s faster, lower-waste alternative concrete and easy to remember: with this coffee, the part people expect to throw away is the part they brew.
