==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.

===== 最终创意概念 =====
**Concept: “Your Coffee Is Wearing It”**

A fast visual reveal built around a surprising question: “Where’s the coffee?” An office worker holds what appears to be an empty cup wrapped in a FoldBrew sleeve. They remove the sleeve, place it directly into hot water, and the camera stays close as it visibly dissolves and transforms the water into coffee.

Each short-form video follows the same memorable sequence: **unwrap, drop, dissolve, drink.** Tight product photography and overhead macro shots make the sleeve’s transformation the focal point, while quick cuts place the action in familiar office moments such as arriving at a desk, stepping into the break room, or preparing for a meeting. On-screen text lands the reveal: **“The sleeve is the coffee.”**

FoldBrew is positioned as the faster, lower-waste alternative to the audience’s usual takeaway routine: one serving made at work without a coffee machine, pod, or disposable sachet. Instagram and TikTok executions invite first-time trial by turning the unusual preparation into a repeatable product ritual, ending with a clear prompt: **“Drop your first FoldBrew.”**
