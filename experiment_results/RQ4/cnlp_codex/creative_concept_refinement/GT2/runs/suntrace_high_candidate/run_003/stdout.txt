==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
SunTrace

Product:
A facial sunscreen that is transparent when applied normally. The package includes reusable UV-sensitive stickers. When a sticker is placed on the skin before sun exposure and removed later, the protected area remains unchanged while the surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker shape.

Target audience:
Young adults aged 18-30 who understand that sunscreen is important but frequently forget to reapply it during outdoor activities.

Objective:
Make the invisible protective effect of sunscreen visually understandable and motivate users to remember sunscreen application and reapplication.

Key product benefit:
The sunscreen provides SPF 50 protection, while the included UV-sensitive demonstration stickers make the effect of sun exposure easier to visualize.

Channels:
TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social media communities.

Budget:
Low to medium.

Constraints:
Use only the real sunscreen and included demonstration stickers. Do not claim that the temporary visual demonstration measures exact UV damage. Do not use celebrities or expensive locations. The idea must be understandable within a short video.

===== 最终创意概念 =====
**Concept: “The Reveal Before the Reason”**

Each short video opens on an unexplained sticker-shaped reveal on a young adult’s skin in a familiar university setting. On-screen text asks: **“Why is that shape still unchanged?”** The action then snaps backward to show the simple setup: a reusable SunTrace UV-sensitive sticker placed on the skin, followed by transparent SunTrace SPF 50 applied normally before studying on the campus lawn, walking between classes, or eating outside.

The video returns to the opening moment as the sticker is removed, making the protected shape the visual reminder that prompts the creator to reach for SunTrace and reapply. A concise disclaimer appears with the reveal: **“A visual reminder, not a UV measurement.”**

Different included sticker shapes create a recognizable series across university social feeds. Each episode uses the same repeatable rhythm: surprising shape first, quick rewind to sticker placement and transparent sunscreen application, then reveal and reapplication. The recurring close is: **“Reveal. Remember. Reapply SunTrace.”**

SunTrace remains essential to both the setup and payoff: its SPF 50 protection is applied before outdoor activity, while its included reusable demonstration stickers turn an otherwise invisible benefit into a distinctive visual cue for young adults who know sunscreen matters but need a memorable prompt to reapply.

The repeated reveal-rewind-reapply structure directly addresses young adults who understand sunscreen but forget reapplication, and SunTrace is essential to both the visual proof cue and the SPF 50 application. The hook is understandable in a short social video and the campus situations fit the stated audience and channels. Ensure each video clearly shows sunscreen applied before outdoor activity, then the later sticker removal and reapplication, so the sequence remains easy to understand without implying the reveal measures UV damage.
