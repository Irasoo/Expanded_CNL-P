==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
SunTrace

Product:
A facial sunscreen that is transparent when applied normally. The package includes reusable UV-sensitive stickers. When a sticker is placed on the skin before sun exposure and removed later, the protected area remains unchanged while the surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker shape.

Target audience:
Young adults aged 18-30 who understand that sunscreen is important but frequently forget to reapply it during outdoor activities.

Objective:
Make the invisible protective effect of sunscreen visually understandable and motivate users to remember sunscreen application and reapplication.

Key product benefit:
The sunscreen provides SPF 50 protection, while the included UV-sensitive demonstration stickers make the effect of sun exposure easier to visualize.

Channels:
TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social media communities.

Budget:
Low to medium.

Constraints:
Use only the real sunscreen and included demonstration stickers. Do not claim that the temporary visual demonstration measures exact UV damage. Do not use celebrities or expensive locations. The idea must be understandable within a short video.

===== 最终创意概念 =====
**SunTrace: Reveal. Reapply. Pass It On.**

The video opens tightly on a young adult removing a SunTrace UV-sensitive demonstration sticker after time outdoors. As the temporary sticker shape appears, they hold the removed sticker toward the camera, creating a quick visual handoff to another student in a new campus setting.

That student catches the same sticker-shaped cue in frame, takes out SunTrace, and reapplies the transparent SPF 50 to their face before continuing outdoors. They then remove their own demonstration sticker and pass the cue into the next clip. Each short handoff links the product’s distinctive reveal directly to the intended behavior: see the cue, reapply, pass the reminder on.

Minimal on-screen text keeps the roles accurate and immediate: “SunTrace SPF 50 protects.” “The sticker helps visualize sun exposure.” “It does not measure exact UV damage.” The sequence ends with a final participant revealing the temporary shape, reapplying SunTrace, and covering the lens with the sticker for a seamless loop back to the opening reveal.

Shot vertically in ordinary campus locations using only SunTrace and its included reusable stickers, the repeatable reveal-to-handoff ritual is designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social communities. SunTrace remains essential to the idea: its sticker supplies the recognizable visual cue, while its transparent SPF 50 is the action young adults reach for whenever that cue appears. The closing line reinforces the habit: **“Reveal. Reapply. Pass it on.”**

评估理由: Originality is strong because the reveal-to-handoff loop turns the included sticker demonstration into a distinctive, product-specific social ritual rather than using a generic sunscreen reminder. Strategic fit is strong: SunTrace SPF 50 and the stickers both have essential, accurate roles, the sequence directly connects visualization with reapplication, and the language avoids presenting the demonstration as an exact measure of UV damage. Audience impact benefits from an immediate visual reveal, quick transitions, campus settings, and a repeatable closing line suited to young adults and short-form social video. Executability is strong because the concept uses ordinary locations, non-celebrity participants, simple match cuts, and only the real product and included stickers. The main risks are that three explanatory text statements may compete with the fast visual sequence and that filming a clear, consistent temporary reveal across multiple participants will require careful timing and shot planning.
优化建议: Preserve the reveal, handoff, reapplication behavior, product prominence, and looping structure. Improve clarity by integrating the required qualification into one concise, legible caption that remains on screen long enough to read, while separating the SPF 50 benefit into the product-action shot. Keep the number of handoffs limited so viewers can immediately understand the sequence within a short video. Plan each reveal as a close-up under consistent lighting and frame every reapplication clearly enough that the reminder behavior cannot be mistaken for the sticker itself providing protection.
