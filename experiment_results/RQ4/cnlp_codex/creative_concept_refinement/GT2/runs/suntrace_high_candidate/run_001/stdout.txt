===== 最终创意概念 =====
**Concept: “Still Out? The SunTrace Check”**

Each short video opens on a close-up of the temporary sticker-shaped reveal with the on-screen question: **“Still out?”** The creator removes an included SunTrace demonstration sticker after time outdoors and immediately reaches for SunTrace: **“Still out? Reapply SunTrace.”**

The video then jumps back to the start of an ordinary outdoor activity, such as studying on campus, walking with friends, or playing outside. The creator places the reusable sticker on their skin for the visual sun-exposure demonstration and applies transparent SunTrace SPF 50 normally to their face. Later, the sticker reveal acts as a visible reminder that they have spent more time outside, prompting them to reapply SunTrace before continuing their activity.

The sticker is presented only as a visual demonstration of sun exposure, not as proof of sunscreen performance, a measure of UV damage, or a timer for when to reapply. A concise caption states: **“Visual sun-exposure demonstration only. Not an exact measure of UV damage or reapplication timing.”** SunTrace remains central to the behavior: the sticker makes an otherwise easy-to-ignore outdoor moment visible, while the sunscreen provides the stated SPF 50 protection.

Designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social communities, each phone-filmed video follows the same repeatable sequence: **reveal, “Still out?” prompt, SunTrace reapplication, continue outside.** Ordinary campus and neighborhood settings keep the execution practical while turning **“Still out? Reapply SunTrace.”** into a clear, memorable cue.

评估理由: Originality is strong because the sticker-shaped reveal creates a memorable, product-specific visual device rather than relying on generic sunscreen education. Strategic fit is strong: the concept directly addresses forgetful young adults, makes an invisible sun-exposure context visible, keeps SunTrace SPF 50 central to the reapplication behavior, uses the specified short-form and university channels, and explicitly avoids presenting the sticker as a damage measurement or reapplication timer. Audience impact is solid but not exceptional because the reverse reveal and repeated "Still out?" cue are immediately understandable, while the emotional or participatory payoff remains somewhat limited and the reveal could still be misread as evidence that the sunscreen itself stopped working. Executability is strong because the repeatable phone-filmed format uses only the sunscreen, included stickers, ordinary outdoor settings, and a simple sequence suitable for a low-to-medium budget.
优化建议: Preserve the distinctive reveal-first structure, ordinary outdoor settings, concise "Still out? Reapply SunTrace" cue, and explicit limitation language. Improve audience impact and clarity by making the opening seconds visibly connect the sticker to a demonstration conducted alongside normal sunscreen use, then show the reapplication as a general habit cue rather than a response triggered by a diagnostic result. Keep the disclaimer legible within the short video, not only in the caption. Add a simple repeatable transition, such as matching the revealed sticker shape to the sticker shown at the activity's start, so viewers immediately understand what produced the visual without adding locations, props, claims, or production complexity.
