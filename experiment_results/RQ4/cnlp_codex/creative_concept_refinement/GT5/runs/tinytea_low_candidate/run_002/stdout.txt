=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The Cold Sip Check-In**

TinyTea makes one cold sip feel like a shared moment between local students and five cities around the world. During the first month after launch, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live public event in each of five major international cities and performs the same short action every time: opening a chilled bottle of lightly sweetened TinyTea, taking the first sip, and holding the bottle up for a synchronized “check-in.”

Each event uses one tightly repeatable format: a five-second countdown, a close-up of the TinyTea bottle opening, the celebrity’s cold sip, and a clear invitation for students to join from wherever they are. Custom interactive digital billboards in each city display the countdown and trigger the same cold-wave response when the sip begins. The dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality application gives students one simple participation action: place the TinyTea bottle and check-in marker in their surroundings, record their own cold sip, and share it.

Local university communities turn the international event into a campus-specific participation moment. Students post their AR check-ins to Instagram and TikTok, tag their university community, and show where they are taking their TinyTea sip. The campaign prompt is: **“Check in for the cold sip.”** Posts from each campus can be edited into short, repeatable launch assets using the same framing, countdown, bottle-opening sound, and sip gesture.

TinyTea is the essential reason for the action. Its lightly sweetened bottled tea, served cold, gives the celebrity’s gesture and each student’s check-in a simple product truth: this is a drink made for a refreshing cold sip. The repeated bottle reveal keeps the brand visible across the live events, billboards, AR application, university communities, Instagram, and TikTok, while the shared gesture makes the launch easy to recognize and remember.

To make the mandatory production as controlled as possible, every city uses the same short event script, shot list, billboard interaction, AR function, and visual treatment. The professional cinematic production is limited to capturing that one hero action and its immediate audience response, creating a compact master film and platform-specific edits within the seven-day schedule. The concept remains dependent on the required celebrity access, five live-city events, five custom interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, and professional production being supplied or separately secured, because the stated sub-$1,000 total budget cannot by itself cover those mandatory elements.

Evaluation reasons:
The synchronized cold-sip gesture is clear, repeatable, and meaningfully tied to TinyTea's lightly sweetened bottled tea served cold. The check-in framing gives the launch a recognizable social hook, and the bottle-opening and sipping actions could translate well across Instagram, TikTok, university communities, live events, billboards, and AR. However, the core idea relies on a globally famous celebrity appearing in person across five major international cities, five custom interactive digital billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days. Those mandatory requirements conflict directly with the very low budget of below $1,000. The concept acknowledges this conflict but does not resolve it, so strategic fit and executability must score very low despite the concept's clarity and potential audience appeal.

Refinement suggestions:
Preserve the simple cold-sip check-in, synchronized countdown, bottle-opening sound, and campus participation structure because these are the strongest and most reusable elements. The primary refinement needed is operational: the concept would require a revised brief that either provides separate resources for the celebrity, international events, billboards, AR application, and professional production, or removes or changes those mandatory requirements. If the constraints remain fixed, explicitly position the idea as a high-level creative platform or production-dependent concept rather than a feasible launch campaign, and define which elements are supplied externally and which are covered by the stated budget. To improve distinctiveness within the existing idea, make the check-in behavior more specific to local university life while keeping TinyTea's cold bottle and sip as the unmistakable central action.
