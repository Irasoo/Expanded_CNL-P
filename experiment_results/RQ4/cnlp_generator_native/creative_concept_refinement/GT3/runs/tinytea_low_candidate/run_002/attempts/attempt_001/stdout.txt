==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
======================
**TinyTea Cold Open: The Seven-Day Campus Break**

TinyTea turns the first sip of a cold bottle into a shared student ritual: at one announced “between-classes” moment, students in five major international cities open TinyTea together, while local university communities join through Instagram and TikTok. The idea is built around one clear behavior that belongs to the product: chill the lightly sweetened tea, open it, take a refreshing first sip, and show the moment.

Within the seven-day production window, TinyTea creates one repeatable Cold Open format for every city. Each live public event uses the same simple setup: a visible TinyTea-branded refreshment point, a countdown, students opening bottles together, and a globally famous celebrity appearing in person to lead the first opening. The celebrity does not introduce a separate performance or message; they demonstrate the exact student break TinyTea is inviting people to share. Local student hosts then repeat the ritual at nearby university communities, connecting the international launch to the everyday pause between lectures, study sessions, and social plans.

Custom interactive digital billboards in each city run the same countdown and display a live feed of participant Cold Open clips. A dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application uses the chilled bottle as the trigger for one simple effect: when students scan or frame their bottle, the screen reveals a shared “Cold Open” marker linking their campus to the five launch cities. The experience ends with a direct invitation to record the opening, first sip, and campus location for Instagram or TikTok.

Professional cinematic production captures one efficient master shoot across the five events, prioritizing repeatable product details: condensation, the cap opening, the first sip, and the crowd joining in. The resulting master footage is cut into vertical launch assets for the first month, while the billboards, AR experience, celebrity appearance, live events, and student-host content all use the same countdown, bottle-opening action, and “take your break cold” message. This keeps every mandatory element focused on one recognizable product moment and makes TinyTea the reason to participate, not merely the sponsor of a large event.

CREATIVE EVALUATION
===================
Originality: 7.0
Strategic fit: 5.0
Audience impact: 7.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 5.2
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The synchronized bottle-opening ritual is a differentiated, memorable idea that turns TinyTea's cold serving benefit into a simple social behavior, although countdown events and first-sip videos are familiar launch devices. The concept keeps the bottle, chilled tea, and student break occasion central, uses vertical social content appropriately, and gives all mandatory elements a coherent role. However, five international events dilute the focus on local university awareness, while the globally famous celebrity and international scale are disproportionate to a small local beverage launch. The action is immediately understandable and offers students an accessible participation format, but requiring a dedicated app creates friction and the international spectacle may feel remote from their daily campus lives. Most critically, the concept cannot credibly be produced for less than $1,000 within seven days: celebrity travel and attendance, five-city live events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, product distribution, staffing, permits, and simultaneous content capture would each create substantial cost and scheduling demands. Reusing one format improves conceptual efficiency but does not make these requirements operationally feasible.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the cold-bottle opening ritual, synchronized countdown, product close-ups, and student-generated vertical content, since these are the clearest and most product-specific strengths. Because every mandatory element must remain, radically minimize each element's scope: define the celebrity appearance as a brief coordinated opening at tightly scheduled events, make each event a compact branded refreshment point rather than a larger program, restrict billboards to one simple interaction, and limit the AR application to a single bottle-triggered effect with no secondary features. Create one production template, shot list, asset package, and remote operating plan for all cities, and connect each international event directly to the local target campus through named student hosts and a prominent local participation moment. Even with those reductions, the concept remains incompatible with the stated budget and seven-day deadline; the brief's mandatory requirements or available resources would need to change before the campaign could be responsibly approved for production.
