==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
======================
**TinyTea: The Cold Handoff**

TinyTea launches with one clear, repeatable student ritual: **open it cold, sip it, hand the moment on.** The globally famous celebrity begins the ritual in person at each of five live public events, opening a chilled bottle of TinyTea, taking one sip, and handing the next bottle to a university student. Each student continues the handoff to another student, making the product benefit visible in every frame: TinyTea is a lightly sweetened bottled tea made to open and enjoy cold between classes, on campus, and with friends.

At every event, the custom interactive billboard follows the live handoff. Students activate it by raising or scanning their TinyTea bottle, which adds their name or campus to a live “Cold Handoff” chain on screen. The dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application lets students record the same handoff sequence with a chilled-bottle visual effect and a simple final prompt: **“Who gets the next cold one?”** They share their clips on Instagram and TikTok with **#TinyTeaColdLaunch**, extending the live ritual into local university communities.

To keep the required production focused, every city uses the same fixed setup: one celebrity action, one billboard interaction, one AR experience, one bottle-first camera sequence, and one short social edit format. Professional cinematic production captures the handoff in a consistent visual style, then produces short vertical clips built around the opening bottle, cold sip, and pass-forward moment. The campaign’s central image remains the product itself: a bottle of TinyTea moving through a visible chain of students.

The campaign requires the mandatory celebrity appearance, five international live events, custom interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, and cinematic production to be provided outside TinyTea’s stated sub-$1,000 production budget; those requirements cannot be credibly produced within that budget and seven-day window.

CREATIVE EVALUATION
===================
Originality: 6.0
Strategic fit: 2.0
Audience impact: 6.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 3.8
Weak criteria: executability

Evaluation reasons:
The Cold Handoff is a clear, repeatable ritual that makes the cold-serving benefit visible and gives TinyTea a product-centered social behavior. The bottle moving through a chain of students is easy to understand and provides usable short-form content, while the consistent format could help create recognition across channels. However, the handoff mechanic is a familiar participatory advertising device rather than an exceptionally distinctive idea. Strategic Fit is substantially weakened because the concept accepts mandatory requirements that directly conflict with the stated sub-$1,000 budget and seven-day production window. Its celebrity, five international events, interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, and cinematic production are not credible within the brief, even though the core student ritual is relevant. Audience Impact is moderate-to-strong because the pass-forward prompt and campus participation could attract attention, but the scale-dependent elements do not make the underlying idea more compelling for local university students. Executability is a 1 because the concept explicitly depends on multiple high-cost, high-complexity elements that cannot realistically be delivered within the stated budget and timeline.

Refinement suggestions:
Preserve the Cold Handoff ritual, bottle-first visual language, campus participation, and simple pass-forward prompt, but resolve the feasibility conflict by replacing the mandatory large-scale requirements with locally producible equivalents only if the brief constraints are revised. Within the current brief, the concept needs a fundamental scope or constraint change: for example, authorize a local creator or student ambassador instead of a globally famous celebrity, replace international events and custom billboards with campus pop-ups and social templates, use a lightweight web-based AR filter instead of a dedicated application, and produce phone-shot vertical content instead of professional cinematic production. If those mandatory requirements cannot be changed, TinyTea should not proceed with this concept under the stated budget and timeline; it would need external funding, partners, production resources, and a materially longer schedule.
