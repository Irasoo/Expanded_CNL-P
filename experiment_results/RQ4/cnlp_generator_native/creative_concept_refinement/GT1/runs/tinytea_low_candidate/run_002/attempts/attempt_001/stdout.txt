==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
======================
**TinyTea: “Pass the Chill Home”**

TinyTea turns one cold bottle into a five-city relay that always ends with local university students. At a live public appearance in each of five major international cities, a globally famous celebrity opens a chilled TinyTea, takes a sip, and says: “Pass the chill home to [local university].” The emphasis is the simple student reset: lightly sweetened tea served cold between lectures, study sessions, and campus plans.

Each city uses the same seven-minute run-of-show, one compact event footprint, and one reusable creative system. The celebrity’s bottle opening triggers that city’s custom interactive digital billboard, which displays the named home university and invites passersby to open the dedicated TinyTea AR app. The app applies a cold condensation effect to a TinyTea bottle and delivers the same “Chill passed home” animation, creating a short vertical video designed for Instagram and TikTok.

The local university is the clear destination in every asset. Students encounter the handoff through university community channels and a small campus TinyTea serving point, where they film their own cold-bottle reset using the same AR effect. Their posts use a single campaign prompt: “Where do you pass your chill?” TinyTea reposts selected responses alongside the five celebrity appearances, making the international spectacle resolve in the daily routines of the target audience rather than remain a distant celebrity moment.

Professional cinematic production captures the identical bottle-opening, billboard-trigger, and student-response sequence in every city. A single remote crew, shared shot list, standardized event layout, one AR build, and one billboard interaction specification keep the work repeatable and limit on-site production to the essential action. The five appearances are scheduled within the seven-day window using the same prepared materials and edit structure.

Because the brief requires five in-person international appearances, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, and professional production for less than $1,000, the concept is executable only after written confirmation that the celebrity access, travel, venues, billboard placements, app development, event staffing, and production resources are supplied or waived before the seven-day production period begins. TinyTea’s budget covers the standardized creative package, local serving materials, and final social edits; no mandatory resource is assumed without that prior commitment.

CREATIVE EVALUATION
===================
Originality: 7.0
Strategic fit: 6.0
Audience impact: 7.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 5.5
Weak criteria: executability

Evaluation reasons:
The five-city handoff resolving into students' everyday campus routines is a differentiated relay structure, and the repeated bottle-opening, condensation, and 'pass the chill' devices connect meaningfully to cold TinyTea. The product remains visible and its lightly sweetened, served-cold benefit is placed in relevant moments between lectures and study sessions. The concept also uses Instagram, TikTok, and university communities coherently. However, the international celebrity spectacle is disproportionate to a local first-month awareness objective, while the lightly sweetened benefit receives less emphasis than the broader idea of chill. The handoff and student-response prompt are clear and potentially engaging, although participation depends on downloading a dedicated app, which creates substantial friction for a new local brand. Executability is critically deficient: five in-person international celebrity appearances, five live events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR app, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days cannot credibly be delivered for less than $1,000. The proposed written confirmation that major resources are supplied or waived assumes capabilities, access, and funding absent from the brief and does not resolve the mandatory budget constraint. A single remote crew also does not establish a workable production plan across five international cities in seven days.

Refinement suggestions:
Preserve the strong standardized relay, cold-bottle trigger, campus destination, and student response format, but make the product benefit more explicit in every handoff by consistently showing and naming the lightly sweetened cold tea as the study-break reset. Reduce audience friction by ensuring the billboard and social content communicate the idea without requiring an app download, while still including the mandatory dedicated AR app as an optional participation layer. Define the celebrity, event, billboard, AR, production, travel, staffing, permissions, and scheduling requirements transparently rather than treating unconfirmed supplied resources as an execution solution. Under the stated mandatory elements, seven-day deadline, and sub-$1,000 total production budget, there is no credible executable refinement; the brief owner must reconcile those constraints before production can proceed.
