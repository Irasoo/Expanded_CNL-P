==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
======================
**TinyTea: The Coldest Small Launch in the World**

TinyTea turns an impossibly large global launch into one focused challenge for local university students: **“Keep it tiny. Keep it cold.”** Across seven days, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at live public events in five major international cities and activates the same “Cold Moment” each time. The celebrity raises a chilled TinyTea and asks, “Can your campus keep it tiny?” before taking a sip and delivering the campaign line.

Each event uses the same compact format and production plan. A custom interactive digital billboard begins with an oversized TinyTea bottle shown warm and surrounded by exaggerated sweetness. Attendees open the dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality application and point their phones at the billboard. With one tap, the excess sweetness falls away, condensation and ice appear, and the bottle resolves into the lightly sweetened TinyTea served cold. The interaction ends with a city-specific frame: **“Big refreshment. Tiny sweetness. [City] made TinyTea cold.”**

Professional cinematic production captures only three repeatable shots in each city: the celebrity’s challenge, the warm-to-cold billboard transformation, and the chilled bottle sip. These are assembled into one short vertical edit per city for Instagram and TikTok. No separate storylines, sets, event programming, or city-specific application features are created.

The five international moments lead to a local university payoff. Each city edit tags the next city, while TinyTea’s local posts challenge students to recreate the Cold Moment on campus by filming a warm study-day reaction followed by the reveal and first sip of a cold TinyTea. The strongest student posts are reshared through TinyTea’s account and participating university communities, making local students the visible conclusion of the global sequence rather than passive spectators.

TinyTea remains central in every execution: the bottle triggers the transformation, cold service provides the visual payoff, and “Tiny sweetness” communicates its lightly sweetened taste. The contrast between five oversized global spectacles and one small local bottle makes the launch memorable while directing attention back to the students TinyTea needs to reach during its first month.

Production is governed by a mandatory pre-launch gate. Work begins only when written confirmation establishes no-cost access within the same seven-day window to the celebrity and international travel, all five public-event locations, five custom interactive billboards and their technical teams, a deployable dedicated AR application, and professional cinematic crews. The sub-$1,000 campaign budget is reserved for TinyTea’s unified creative files, local social publishing, and university-community distribution. If any mandated resource is not confirmed at no additional cost, the campaign cannot enter production within the brief’s budget and schedule.

EVALUATION
==========
Originality: 7.0
Strategic fit: 3.0
Audience impact: 6.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 4.4
Weak criteria: executability

Evaluation reasons:
The concept has a distinctive central contrast: an oversized global launch built around a small local bottled tea, with the warm-to-cold transformation making the product benefit visible. The repeated Cold Moment, bottle-triggered AR interaction, and student recreation mechanic give the idea more product specificity than generic launch advertising. However, strategic fit is severely weakened by the mandatory constraints. The concept targets local university students, but most attention is allocated to a celebrity and events in five major international cities, which are distant from the intended audience and do not clearly improve local first-month awareness enough to justify the complexity. The concept includes the required celebrity, live events, billboards, AR application, cinematic production, and seven-day schedule, but merely making them conditional does not make them workable. A globally famous celebrity, five international events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional crews, travel, and production cannot reasonably be delivered within a sub-$1,000 budget unless all resources are donated, and the brief provides no basis to assume those resources exist. The audience hook is understandable and potentially engaging, especially the visual reveal and student participation, but the international spectacle may overshadow TinyTea and local campus relevance.

Refinement suggestions:
Preserve the strong Keep it tiny. Keep it cold platform, the warm-to-cold product demonstration, and the student Cold Moment challenge. To improve strategic fit, make local university participation the primary experience and use the international sequence only as supporting content, with direct campus prompts, local student creators, and clear access to the product. To improve impact, sharpen the student tension behind the idea, such as the contrast between a warm study day and an immediately refreshing cold sip, rather than relying mainly on celebrity scale. The largest weakness is executability: under the stated constraints, the concept should be treated as nonviable unless written commitments already exist for every mandatory resource at no cost. The pre-launch gate is a useful acknowledgment of this issue but does not solve it. A practical refinement would be to present the idea as contingent on confirmed donated resources and prepare a separate low-cost activation using local student content and existing social channels if those confirmations are absent; if the brief truly forbids removing any mandatory element, the honest recommendation is not to produce the campaign until feasibility is established.
