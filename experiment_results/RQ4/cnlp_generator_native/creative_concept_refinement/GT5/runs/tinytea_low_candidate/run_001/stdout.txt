==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
======================
**TinyTea “Cold Front: The Seven-Second Chill Relay”**

TinyTea launches a seven-day, five-city relay led in person by one globally famous celebrity. At each live public event, the celebrity opens a cold TinyTea, takes one sip, and holds a “chill face” for seven seconds before challenging local university students to repeat the ritual. The distinctive seven-second action gives every appearance the same immediate hook while demonstrating the lightly sweetened tea as a simple cold drink between classes, study sessions, and social plans.

Each city uses one custom interactive digital billboard built around the same reusable countdown interface. The billboard captures completed chill faces and places them into an expanding mosaic that carries forward from city to city. The final mosaic reveals the TinyTea bottle and the line “Lightly Sweet. Best Served Cold,” making student participation, rather than event spectacle, the campaign’s memorable visual payoff.

A dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application offers only the essential relay experience: point the camera at a TinyTea bottle, start the seven-second countdown, record a chill face, and export the result vertically for Instagram or TikTok. Local university communities receive a daily city prompt and invite students to add their version to the relay, allowing the international activity to function as a concentrated awareness trigger for the local campus audience.

Professional cinematic production is limited to one modular master format: the celebrity’s opening ritual, seven-second countdown, product close-up, and direct invitation to students. The crew captures the five appearances using the same shot list, graphics package, and edit structure so footage can be released quickly throughout the first launch month without creating five separate campaigns.

TinyTea is indispensable to every interaction: the bottle starts the countdown, the cold sip motivates the chill face, and the closing frame consistently communicates “Lightly Sweet. Best Served Cold.” All five city events, celebrity appearances, custom billboards, application development, and cinematic assets must be contracted, produced, and completed within seven days. Because those mandatory requirements cannot be purchased or delivered credibly for less than $1,000, execution requires the brief owner to provide the celebrity, venues, billboards, application infrastructure, travel, and professional production as pre-secured resources; the campaign budget can then be reserved for the low-cost campus and social rollout.

Creative Evaluation
===================
Originality: 7.0
Strategic fit: 4.0
Audience impact: 6.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 4.7
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The seven-second chill face, relay structure, and student-generated mosaic create a distinctive and repeatable hook that is meaningfully tied to drinking TinyTea cold, supporting a 7 for originality. The concept places the product at the center of the ritual and provides usable formats for Instagram, TikTok, and university communities, but the globally famous celebrity, five major international cities, interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, and cinematic production are disproportionate to a local university awareness objective and conflict with the very low budget and seven-day timeline. The concept acknowledges this conflict by assuming all major resources are pre-secured, but those capabilities are not supplied in the brief, so that assumption cannot resolve the strategic and practical mismatch. The simple visual action is understandable and potentially participatory for students, earning a 6 for audience impact, although the celebrity-centered international spectacle could distract from the local product and the lightly sweetened benefit.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the seven-second chill ritual, countdown, student participation, and bottle-reveal payoff, since these are the strongest product-specific elements. Make the campaign genuinely executable by adapting the relay to local campuses, replacing the celebrity with student ambassadors or recognizable local participants, using social countdown templates instead of custom international billboards, and replacing the dedicated AR application with a lightweight camera filter or standard vertical video prompt. If the mandatory constraints cannot be changed, clearly position the concept as dependent on confirmed in-kind resources and reduce the score expectations accordingly; otherwise, the concept should be rewritten around the available sub-$1,000 production reality. Strengthen strategic fit by connecting the ritual to concrete student moments such as class changes, library breaks, or campus heat, and state a simple participation or sampling mechanic that drives awareness during the first launch month.
