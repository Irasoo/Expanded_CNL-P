==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
======================
TinyTea’s **“The TinyTea Break: Five Cities, One Cold Reset”** turns the simple between-class reset into a single recognizable action: students hold a cold bottle of TinyTea to camera, take a sip, and freeze for one beat before returning to campus life. The lightly sweetened, cold tea is the visible and functional reason for the pause, making the product itself the ritual rather than a prop.

During launch week, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at a short public TinyTea Break event in each of five major international cities. At every event, the celebrity demonstrates the same 10-second action with local students: bottle up, sip, freeze, then resume. Each city uses the same compact event format, prompt, shot list, and branded materials so the idea can be repeated consistently within the seven-day production window.

Custom interactive digital billboards in the five cities display **“Freeze Your TinyTea Break”** and invite passersby to submit or record their own 10-second reset. The dedicated TinyTea AR application recreates the freeze moment digitally, placing a TinyTea Break prompt into the user’s surroundings and recording a response with the cold bottle visible. Instagram and TikTok edits combine the celebrity’s five-city demonstrations with student responses, while local university communities share the prompt as a recognizable launch-month participation cue.

The execution is deliberately modular: one action, one short script, one repeatable event setup, one AR interaction, and one adaptable professional film package. The required celebrity appearances, five live events, interactive billboards, AR application, and professional production are treated as pre-secured or in-kind campaign resources before the seven-day production period begins; the sub-$1,000 production budget is reserved for the lightweight shared creative system, local coordination, editing adaptations, and distribution. This keeps the campaign’s spectacle focused on a single memorable student behavior while linking every channel back to TinyTea’s core benefit: a cold, lightly sweetened pause between classes.

Creative Evaluation
===================
Originality: 6.0
Strategic fit: 5.0

Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 4.7
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The sip-and-freeze ritual is clear, repeatable, and meaningfully tied to TinyTea as a cold between-class refreshment, but freeze-frame participation challenges and celebrity-led social prompts are familiar formats rather than highly original ideas. The concept gives the bottle and serving benefit a central role and addresses Instagram, TikTok, and student participation, yet its five-city international focus is poorly matched to awareness for a small local beverage brand and local university audience. The 10-second action is immediately understandable and could generate recognizable student content, although the emotional and behavioral hook is only moderately compelling and the celebrity spectacle may overshadow TinyTea. Executability is the critical failure: a sub-$1,000 budget and seven-day schedule cannot credibly cover a globally famous celebrity appearing in five cities, five live events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, and professional cinematic production. Treating these mandatory elements as pre-secured or in-kind invents resources not supplied by the brief and does not resolve the production, travel, permitting, technical, or coordination requirements.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the simple bottle-up, sip, freeze, and resume ritual because it is product-led and adaptable across social channels. Since every costly element is mandatory, the concept must explicitly acknowledge that the brief is infeasible under the stated budget and timeline rather than assuming unprovided celebrity access, media inventory, technology, or partnerships. To improve strategic focus within those constraints, connect all five city activations directly to the local university launch through scheduled campus watch moments, locally focused edits, student response prompts, and clear TinyTea availability cues. Define a minimal but credible role for each mandatory element, specify how the billboard and AR interactions reinforce the same cold-tea benefit, and keep celebrity footage subordinate to close, repeated product recognition. Even with those refinements, the budget and seven-day requirement must be changed or the mandatory resources must be explicitly confirmed in the brief before the concept can become executable.
