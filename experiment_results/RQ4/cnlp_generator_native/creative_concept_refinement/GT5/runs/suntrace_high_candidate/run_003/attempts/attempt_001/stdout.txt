==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
======================
**SunTrace: “The Shape of the Moment.”**

Each short video opens mid-activity with a tight shot of a surprising, temporary shape visible on a creator’s skin: a water drop after a refill, a camera icon during a campus photo walk, or a ball after a pickup game. A matching sound from that moment plays as on-screen copy asks: **“What made you remember?”**

The video jumps back to the start of the outing. The creator applies transparent SunTrace SPF 50, then places one included UV-sensitive sticker on their skin. Quick cuts follow one ordinary campus activity until a natural break after time outdoors, sweating, or activity. At that break, the creator removes the sticker in the same simple, consistently framed close-up from the opening. The protected area remains unchanged while the surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the shape. The shape connects the visual demonstration directly to the moment in the creator’s day, turning the sticker reveal into SunTrace’s recognizable reapplication cue.

The creator then reapplies SunTrace before continuing. On-screen copy reads: **“Time outdoors leaves a reminder. Reapply before you keep going.”** The sunscreen package remains visible beside the reveal and is clearly shown during both application moments, keeping SPF 50 protection and the included demonstration stickers central to the story.

Every TikTok or Reel uses the repeatable sequence: **shape first, rewind to application, live the moment, reveal, reapply.** University communities can adapt it to familiar outdoor routines using only the real product, included sticker shapes, ordinary campus locations, and one standardized reveal shot. The close is: **“SunTrace SPF 50. See the moment. Remember to reapply.”** A persistent caption during the reveal states: **“The sticker demonstrates exposure around the protected area. It does not measure UV damage or SPF performance.”**

===================

Weak criteria: originality, audience_impact
Evaluation reasons: The concept has a distinctive, repeatable format that makes an otherwise invisible sunscreen benefit visible through a recognizable reveal shot and links that reveal to a real outdoor moment. Its strongest strategic feature is turning the sticker removal into a reapplication cue, directly addressing the audience's tendency to forget reapplication while keeping SunTrace SPF 50 and the included stickers central. The rewind structure is well suited to TikTok, Reels, and university communities, and the ordinary campus activities and standardized framing support low-to-medium production needs. Audience impact should be solid because the opening shape creates curiosity and the before-and-after demonstration is easy to understand, though the emotional or behavioral hook may remain moderate if the reveal feels like a product demonstration rather than a personally meaningful moment. Originality is good but not exceptional: short-form rewind structures, creator routines, and visual transformation reveals are familiar formats. There is also some potential confusion in examples such as a water drop or camera icon, since the concept must use only the included sticker shapes and the revealed shape may not naturally correspond to the activity. The disclaimer is responsible and helps meet the constraint, but its length and placement should not overwhelm the short reveal.
Refinement suggestions: Make the product-specific mechanism even clearer in the first seconds by showing the sticker being placed before the activity or briefly pairing the opening reveal with a simple visual cue that the unchanged area was protected. Limit the campaign to the actual included sticker shapes and build the creative system around their meanings, rather than implying that creators can use arbitrary icons. Strengthen the behavioral payoff by making the reapplication moment more immediate and repeatable, such as a consistent on-screen reapply cue tied to elapsed activity milestones or common breaks. Keep the disclaimer concise, readable, and consistently placed without competing with the reveal. Test a few activity-specific versions in which the shape has a clear connection to the moment, while preserving the repeatable sequence and standardized close-up that make the concept efficient to produce.
