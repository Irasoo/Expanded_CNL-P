==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
======================
**“Drop the Coffee Run”**

FoldBrew turns the familiar office coffee run into one simple, memorable action: dropping the package directly into hot water. Each short-form video opens on an ordinary workday moment when someone wants coffee but has no time for a takeaway run. A close, uninterrupted product shot shows a FoldBrew sleeve held above a clear cup, dropped into hot water, and visibly dissolving until the water becomes coffee. On-screen copy follows the transformation: **“No machine. No pod. No sachet. The package is the coffee.”**

The principal execution is a repeatable Instagram and TikTok series built around the same visual ritual in recognizable office settings, such as arriving at a desk, preparing for a meeting, or taking a short afternoon break. Tight product photography and macro video make the dissolve the focal point, while real-time pacing keeps the unusual behavior credible and easy to understand. Each video closes on the finished drink beside the FoldBrew branding and the trial prompt: **“Drop it. Stir it. Try FoldBrew.”**

FoldBrew is not presented as an accessory to the demonstration; it performs the central action and proves the benefit on camera. The concept speaks directly to urban office workers by replacing the routine takeaway trip with a faster preparation moment at work, while making first trial desirable through a visually distinctive transformation designed for replay, sharing, and short-form video.
