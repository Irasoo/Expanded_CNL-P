Final Creative Concept

SET current_concept = "Drop the Sleeve. Make the Coffee. A short-form video series built around one surprising, repeatable office-desk ritual: an urban worker reaches for what appears to be a FoldBrew coffee cup sleeve, drops it into a clear cup of hot water, and watches it visibly dissolve into a serving of coffee. Tight product photography and macro shots capture the sleeve folding, dissolving, and transforming the water, followed by the worker taking their first sip. Each film opens with the visual hook, 'This is the coffee,' then reveals the full transformation and closes on the FoldBrew pack with: 'No machine. No pod. No sachet. Just add the whole sleeve to hot water.' Fast cuts and natural office settings make the execution native to Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video, while the repeated drop-dissolve-drink sequence makes FoldBrew's unusual behavior memorable and positions it as a fast, lower-waste alternative to the audience's usual takeaway coffee. The trial prompt is: 'Try the coffee that comes in its own disappearing sleeve.'"
