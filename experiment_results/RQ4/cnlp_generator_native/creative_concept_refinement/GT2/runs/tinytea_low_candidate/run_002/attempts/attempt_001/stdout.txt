==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**TinyTea: The Cold Moment Relay**

TinyTea turns one simple action into a shared launch signal for university students: open a cold bottle, take the first sip, and pass the moment forward. The campaign unfolds simultaneously through five live public events in major international cities, each built around the same compact ritual and message: **“Lightly sweet. Served cold.”**

A globally famous celebrity appears in person at the designated headline event and opens a bottle of TinyTea at the campaign’s agreed launch time. The four other city events mirror the action through local student hosts, creating one recognizable moment across all five locations without requiring separate celebrity appearances. Every event uses the same minimal setup: a visible TinyTea bottle station, a countdown, the synchronized opening, and student-shot vertical video for Instagram and TikTok.

The custom interactive digital billboards extend the ritual in each city. A passerby performs one simple interaction to make a digital TinyTea bottle chill and open, revealing the next city in the relay. The dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality app uses the same single action, placing a cold virtual bottle beside the user and producing a short relay clip that can be shared into university communities. The app, billboards, live events, and social edits all use one visual system and one call to action: **“Open cold. Pass it on.”**

TinyTea is the mechanism behind every interaction. Its bottle is the object students open, its cold serving benefit is the campaign’s only product message, and its lightly sweetened taste gives students a clear reason to try it. Local university communities make the idea relevant by recreating the opening moment in campus groups, residence halls, societies, and student feeds during the first month after launch.

To make the mandatory scope as executable as possible within seven days, the production is designed as a tightly templated package: one short cinematic master shoot, one repeatable event format, one billboard interaction adapted across five placements, one lightweight AR experience, and a shared library of vertical edits. No additional stunts, features, messages, or custom mechanics are introduced. The concept proceeds only when the required celebrity appearance, five-city live events, billboard placements and development, dedicated AR application, professional production, seven-day schedule, and sub-$1,000 total production budget are contractually secured; otherwise, the brief’s mandatory requirements make delivery impossible and the launch cannot be represented as producible.

Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The synchronized cold-bottle opening creates a coherent, product-specific ritual and gives TinyTea a recognizable social format, though relay mechanics and shareable opening moments are familiar campaign devices. The concept keeps the lightly sweetened, served-cold benefit visible and uses Instagram, TikTok, and university communities appropriately. However, the five international events, celebrity appearance, billboards, and AR app are far beyond what a small local beverage launch needs to build local student awareness, weakening strategic focus. The central action is easy for students to understand and potentially recreate, but the international spectacle risks feeling disconnected from a local product and may not translate into trial. Execution is not credible: globally famous celebrity talent, five live events, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, cinematic production, and a seven-day turnaround cannot be delivered for a total production budget below $1,000. The concept correctly identifies this conflict, but acknowledging impossibility does not make the mandatory scope executable.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the simple bottle-opening ritual, cold-serving cue, and student-created relay format because they are the most workable and relevant parts of the idea. To improve strategic fit and execution, the brief itself must be revised: replace or sponsor-provide the celebrity, international events, billboard inventory and development, AR-app development, and cinematic production, or substantially increase the budget and schedule. If every requirement truly remains mandatory without donated resources, no refinement of this concept can make it producible. Within any revised scope, focus the relay on local campuses, use native Instagram and TikTok effects instead of a dedicated app, and make sampling or a local purchase prompt the direct call to action.
