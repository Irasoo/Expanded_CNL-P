==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**TinyTea: The Seven-Minute Break**

At the same local class-change time during launch week, TinyTea turns seven minutes between lectures into one cold, shared break across five major international cities. The recognizable image is a cold TinyTea bottle held upright beside a phone, forming a “tiny window” that connects students taking the same pause elsewhere. The opening hook is immediate: **“Your next class can wait seven minutes.”**

Each city hosts one compact public event within a university community. A custom interactive digital billboard displays a live seven-minute countdown and a growing mosaic of participating students. Scanning its QR code opens the dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application, where pointing the camera at a TinyTea bottle or campaign mark reveals a single lightweight overlay: a window into another participating city, the synchronized countdown, and a seven-second recording prompt. Students film the moment they open a cold bottle, take a sip, and reset, then share it to Instagram or TikTok. Selected clips appear in the billboard mosaic and are reposted by the university communities, making recognition within the student community the participation reward.

A globally famous celebrity appears in person at the host-city event for one tightly defined role: placing a cold TinyTea bottle beside a phone, opening the first “tiny window,” and starting the countdown. The other four live events join through the same feed. The celebrity initiates the moment, but the visual quickly shifts to students across all five cities, keeping TinyTea and the shared campus ritual central.

Professional cinematic coverage is deliberately limited to one operator at the host event, while fixed phones at the other four events supply matching vertical footage. One editor combines the countdown, celebrity opening, live city feeds, billboard mosaics, and student clips into a short launch film plus reusable TikTok, Reel, and campus-channel edits. Every execution shows TinyTea cold and being opened during a real student transition, making its lightly sweetened bottled-tea benefit part of the ritual rather than an added claim.

To keep the seven-day production window and sub-$1,000 total budget credible, production begins only after written, no-fee commitments have been secured for the mandatory celebrity appearance, five public locations, five interactive billboard placements, AR application build, international event staffing, and professional production services. The campaign uses the budget only for TinyTea product, simple event identifiers, connectivity, and essential local transport. If those documented contributions are unavailable before day one, the campaign does not enter production because the mandatory brief cannot otherwise be delivered within its stated budget and timeline.

EVALUATION
Originality: 7.0

Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: Originality is strong because the seven-minute class-change ritual, synchronized countdown, and bottle-and-phone 'tiny window' create a coherent, recognizable idea tied to the TinyTea name and student behavior. Strategic fit is mixed: the concept addresses launch awareness, university communities, social channels, and cold consumption, while keeping the product visually central. However, the lightly sweetened benefit is barely communicated, and the campaign's international scale is disproportionate to a small local brand seeking local student awareness. Audience impact is promising because the short break, countdown, peer mosaic, and vertical student clips provide an understandable participation hook, although the many components may obscure the simple product message. Executability is extremely weak. The concept includes the mandatory elements, but it does not establish a credible way to obtain a globally famous celebrity, five international events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, international staffing, and professional production within seven days for less than $1,000. Requiring written no-fee commitments merely makes production conditional on extensive unprovided resources; it does not make the plan executable under the supplied brief.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the seven-minute campus-break ritual, synchronized countdown, bottle-and-phone visual, and student-generated vertical clips. Make the product benefit more explicit through a repeatable action or line connecting a cold, lightly sweetened tea with a quick reset between classes. Reduce conceptual complexity by giving the celebrity, AR view, billboards, live events, and social posts one identical seven-minute interaction rather than several overlapping mechanics. Since every mandatory element must remain, provide a concrete seven-day production schedule, named ownership for each workstream, technical scope for the AR application and billboard feeds, permissions and moderation procedures, and verified sources for all no-fee contributions. Without documented access to those resources, the honest refinement is to classify the concept as contingent and not production-ready, because creative simplification alone cannot reconcile the mandatory requirements with the budget and deadline.
