==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**TinyTea: The Five-City Cold Break**

TinyTea turns the first month of launch into a single student ritual: “Small break. Big chill.” At a scheduled time, university students take a cold TinyTea break together, led by a globally famous celebrity appearing in person at the main live event. The celebrity opens a TinyTea, takes a sip, and delivers the product message: lightly sweetened tea, served cold, for the moment between classes.

Five live public events in major international cities use custom interactive digital billboards showing a TinyTea bottle filling with cold tea. At each event, students use the dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application to tap the bottle, release a cold-break animation, and add their city to the shared countdown. The local university community is the campaign’s active center: campus posts and student community channels direct students to join the local launch, share their timed sip, and make TinyTea recognizable as the cold, lightly sweetened break for campus days.

Professional cinematic footage captures the celebrity kickoff, students joining the break, and the bottle-and-ice visual. Short vertical edits for TikTok and Instagram repeat one clear format: the countdown, an opened cold bottle, a sip, and the line “Small break. Big chill.” Every execution keeps the bottle and its lightly sweetened, cold-served benefit central.

This concept cannot credibly be produced within seven days and a total production budget below $1,000 while also requiring an in-person globally famous celebrity, five international public events, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, and professional cinematic production.

Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The synchronized cold-break ritual is coherent and gives TinyTea, the bottle, and the cold, lightly sweetened benefit a visible role, but countdowns, celebrity kickoffs, AR interactions, and shared social moments are familiar campaign devices rather than a highly original product-specific idea. The concept speaks appropriately to university students through between-class occasions, vertical social content, and campus communities. However, its five-city international scale is poorly aligned with awareness for a small local beverage launch, and the concept knowingly cannot satisfy the brief's simultaneous budget and seven-day production constraints. The timed sip, concise slogan, and repeatable bottle-opening format could be clear and engaging for the audience, although participation depends on an unnecessarily complex event and application ecosystem. Executability is critically weak: an in-person globally famous celebrity, five international events, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days cannot credibly be delivered for less than $1,000.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the strongest core idea: a scheduled between-class cold break, a visible TinyTea bottle, and short social edits built around opening, sipping, and the line "Small break. Big chill." Make the local student role and first-month awareness objective more explicit in the event and content flow. Within the stated brief, define the five-city, celebrity, billboard, AR, and cinematic components much more precisely so responsibilities, production requirements, and dependencies are transparent. However, no refinement can make all mandatory elements credible within the stated budget and seven-day deadline. The brief must change by materially increasing budget and production time or by removing mandatory production requirements before this can become an executable concept.
