**Checkout Button Guardrail Test**

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion.

Over 14 days, Control recorded 1,000 conversions from 10,000 users, a **10.0% conversion rate**. Variant recorded 1,200 conversions from 10,000 users, a **12.0% conversion rate**. This is a **+2.0 percentage point absolute difference** and a **20.0% relative lift**.

The result is **statistically significant**: two-sided **p = 0.00000619**, with a **95% confidence interval of +1.13 to +2.87 percentage points** for the Variant-Control difference. No sample ratio mismatch was detected, so the observed allocation is consistent with the expected 50/50 split. The test is marked **underpowered** against the 1 percentage point minimum detectable effect at 80% power, but this is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result.

**Guardrail assessment:** Customer support contact rate increased from **2% to 5%**, which the product team explicitly considers a meaningful negative effect and guardrail concern.

**Recommendation: Investigate before shipping.** The variant produced a statistically reliable conversion improvement, but the reported guardrail degradation prevents an unconditional rollout. Investigate why “Buy Now” increased support contacts, validate the support-rate measurement and affected user journeys, and determine whether the underlying confusion or issue can be resolved. After mitigation, rerun or extend the experiment while monitoring both purchase conversion and support contact rate before making a shipping decision.
