==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
**Checkout Button Guardrail Test**

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion.

Over 14 days, Control recorded 1,000 conversions from 10,000 users, a **10.0% conversion rate**. Variant recorded 1,200 conversions from 10,000 users, a **12.0% conversion rate**. This is a **2.0 percentage-point absolute increase** and a **20.0% relative lift**.

The result is **statistically significant**: two-sided **p = 0.00000619**, with a **95% confidence interval for the absolute difference of +1.13 to +2.87 percentage points**. The experiment is marked **underpowered** against the 1-percentage-point minimum detectable effect at 80% power, but this is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result. No sample ratio mismatch was detected, so the observed 50/50 allocation does not raise an SRM validity concern.

**Guardrail assessment:** Customer support contact rate increased from **2% to 5%**, which the product team explicitly considers a meaningful negative effect and guardrail concern.

**Recommendation: Investigate; do not ship broadly yet.** The variant produced a statistically reliable conversion improvement, but the reported support-contact increase represents an important adverse effect that prevents a straightforward rollout.

**Next steps:** Investigate why “Buy Now” increases support contacts, including whether it creates confusion about checkout commitment or expectations. Validate the support-contact measurement and its relationship to the variant, then test a revised label or experience designed to retain the conversion gain without the guardrail degradation. Reassess shipping only after the guardrail concern is resolved.
