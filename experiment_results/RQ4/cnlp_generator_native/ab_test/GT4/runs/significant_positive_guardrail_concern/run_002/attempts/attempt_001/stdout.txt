==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Checkout Button Guardrail Test ran for 14 days to test the hypothesis that changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” would increase purchase conversion rate.

Control: 10,000 users, 1,000 conversions, 10.0% conversion rate.  
Variant: 10,000 users, 1,200 conversions, 12.0% conversion rate.

The variant showed an absolute improvement of 2.0 percentage points and a relative lift of 20.0%. The supplied two-sided p-value was 0.00000619, with a 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference of 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points. The result is statistically significant. The supplied power assessment was underpowered relative to the 1 percentage point minimum detectable effect and 80% power target; this is a diagnostic limitation and does not override the significant result. No sample ratio mismatch was detected.

The guardrail is concerning: customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%, and the product team explicitly considers this a meaningful negative effect. The recommendation is to investigate rather than ship the variant.

Next steps: investigate why “Buy Now” may be increasing support contacts, review support-contact drivers and relevant user segments, and determine whether the button wording or surrounding checkout experience can be adjusted. Re-test the revised experience with the purchase conversion rate and customer support contact rate monitored together, using a sample size designed to meet the stated minimum detectable effect and power target.
