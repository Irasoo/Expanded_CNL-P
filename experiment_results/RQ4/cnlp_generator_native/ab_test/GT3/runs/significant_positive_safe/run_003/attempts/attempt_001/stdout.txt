**Checkout Button Positive Test**

The hypothesis was that changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” would increase purchase conversion. The experiment ran for 14 days.

Control recorded a 10.0% conversion rate (1,000 conversions from 10,000 users), while Variant recorded 12.0% (1,200 from 10,000). This is a 2.0 percentage point absolute increase and a 20.0% relative lift.

The result is statistically significant (two-sided p-value: 0.00000619), with a 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference of 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points. The test was classified as underpowered for the supplied 1 percentage point minimum detectable effect, but this is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result. No sample ratio mismatch was detected.

Guardrails were clear: revenue per user, page load time, and customer support contact rate remained stable, with no meaningful negative effects reported.
