==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Checkout Button Guardrail Test evaluated whether changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” would increase purchase conversion over 14 days.

Control recorded a 10.0% conversion rate (1,000/10,000), while Variant recorded 12.0% (1,200/10,000). This is a statistically significant increase of 2.0 percentage points, or 20.0% relative lift (two-sided p-value: 0.00000619; 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference: 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points). The test was underpowered for the supplied 1-point minimum detectable effect, but this diagnostic does not override the statistically significant result. No sample ratio mismatch was detected.

The guardrail raised a meaningful concern: customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%, and the product team explicitly considers this a negative effect. Recommendation: **investigate**, rather than ship immediately. Determine why “Buy Now” increased support contacts, review the nature and operational impact of those contacts, and test mitigations or revised button wording while continuing to monitor purchase conversion and the support-contact guardrail.
