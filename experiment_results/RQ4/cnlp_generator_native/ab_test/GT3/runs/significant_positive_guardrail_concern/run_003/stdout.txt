==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
The 14-day Checkout Button Guardrail Test evaluated whether changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” would increase purchase conversion.

Control recorded a 10.0% conversion rate (1,000/10,000), while Variant recorded 12.0% (1,200/10,000). The Variant improved conversion by 2.0 percentage points, a 20.0% relative lift. This result is statistically significant (two-sided p-value = 0.00000619), with a 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference of 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points.

The test was classified as underpowered for the supplied 1-percentage-point minimum detectable effect, but this is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result. No sample ratio mismatch was detected.

However, the customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%, which the product team explicitly considers a meaningful negative effect and guardrail concern. Therefore, the recommendation is to **investigate**, not ship immediately. Determine why “Buy Now” increased support contacts, assess the severity and source of those contacts, and consider testing revised button copy or supporting checkout messaging that preserves the conversion improvement without the guardrail degradation.
