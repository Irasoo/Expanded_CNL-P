**Checkout Button Guardrail Test**

Over 14 days, the test evaluated the hypothesis that changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” would increase purchase conversion.

Control recorded a 10.0% conversion rate with 10,000 users, while Variant recorded 12.0% with 10,000 users. This is a statistically significant absolute increase of 2.0 percentage points and a relative lift of 20.0% (two-sided p-value: 0.00000619; 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference: 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points).

The test was classified as underpowered for the supplied 1-percentage-point minimum detectable effect. This is a diagnostic limitation, but it does not override the statistically significant result. No sample ratio mismatch was detected.

**Recommendation: Investigate.** Although Variant improved purchase conversion, the customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%, which the product team considers a meaningful negative effect and guardrail concern. Do not ship broadly until the cause and business impact of the support increase are understood. Analyze the reasons for support contacts, determine whether “Buy Now” creates confusion or unexpected purchase behavior, assess the tradeoff between conversion gains and support impact, and test a revised button label or experience that preserves conversion improvement without the guardrail degradation.
