FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**Concept: “The Next Hand”**

FreshDrop turns the familiar handoff into a recognizable visual rule for moving through the day: every story begins with one person completing a small everyday action, then extends directly into the next person’s action through the same bottle. The bottle is never simply passed for its own sake; it is the single continuous link between one useful moment and the next.

The principal Instagram and Facebook execution is a series of short vertical videos for adults aged 18–45. Each film follows a simple three-part pattern:

1. **Ready:** FreshDrop appears in the opening frame as someone prepares for an ordinary next step, such as leaving home, starting a commute, heading to exercise, or meeting friends.
2. **The next hand:** The person moves the bottle in one consistent direction, such as from the lower-left to the upper-right of the frame. A match cut continues that exact movement in a new setting, where another person receives it and begins their own next step.
3. **Keep going:** The new action lands in a small human payoff: a friend arrives, a walk begins, a workout starts, or an afternoon plan comes together. The bottle remains visible, followed by the brief sign-off: **“FreshDrop. Ready for what’s next.”**

For example, someone closes a laptop and lifts FreshDrop toward the camera. The motion continues in the next shot as a friend takes the bottle while stepping outside, then passes it across frame to someone joining the walk. Another film begins with a person leaving an apartment, hands the bottle through a doorway, and cuts to a colleague arriving at a shared destination. Each transition follows the same direction, framing, and end movement, creating a repeatable campaign signature that viewers can recognize before the sign-off.

Static Instagram and Facebook posts use two or three sequential images rather than generic lifestyle scenes: one person reaches for FreshDrop, the bottle occupies the same position in the next image, and a new person continues the action. Short copy completes the sequence with lines such as **“Ready here. Ready for what’s next.”** and **“One day. The next hand.”**

FreshDrop’s role is meaningful but straightforward: its portable bottle is the visible object that makes the movement between everyday situations possible. The concept builds broad awareness by placing the brand in familiar, positive moments while giving every execution a distinct, disciplined visual pattern: the same directional handoff, the same continuous action, and a clear human next step. The format is practical for a medium budget, using ordinary people, accessible locations, short shoots, and modular edits that can be repeated across Instagram and Facebook.
