==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**Creative Concept: “The Sleeve Becomes the Coffee”**

FoldBrew turns a familiar office object into an unexpected coffee-making moment. Each short-form video begins with a FoldBrew sleeve clearly held beside an empty cup of hot water. On-screen text asks:

**“Sleeve or coffee?”**

The hand moves the sleeve toward the outside of the cup as expected, pauses, then drops it into the water. A tight overhead shot shows the intact branded sleeve breaking down as the water becomes coffee. Simple jump cuts mark three clear stages without requiring a difficult single-take dissolve:

**SLEEVE. DROP. COFFEE.**

The first two stages stay tightly framed on the cup for a legible product demonstration. The final stage widens to reveal the workday context: a meeting is about to begin, an unfinished task remains on screen, or a colleague’s **“Coffee run?”** message is waiting. The worker picks up the finished drink and continues working as the reply appears:

**“Already made.”**

The campaign uses three repeatable office executions:

- **Meeting in Two:** A calendar alert appears before the sleeve drops. The coffee is ready at the desk for the meeting.
- **Coffee Run?:** A takeaway invitation arrives. The worker makes FoldBrew with hot water and replies with the finished cup.
- **Coat Fake-Out:** A hand reaches for a coat, stops at the FoldBrew sleeve, and makes coffee without leaving the desk.

Every film uses the same transparent cup, overhead angle, sleeve-drop shot, and three-beat edit. This creates a recognizable visual mnemonic while keeping production practical across Instagram, TikTok, and other short-form placements. Natural dissolve footage can be captured once in a controlled close-up setup and reused with different office openings and endings.

Still and carousel assets preserve the same sequence in three ordinary product photographs: the intact FoldBrew sleeve, the sleeve visibly dissolving in hot water, and the finished coffee at the desk.

Every execution closes on the product beside the finished cup with a direct trial prompt:

**The sleeve becomes the coffee.**  
**Just add hot water. No machine, pod, or separate sachet.**  
**Try FoldBrew.**
