==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
## TinyTea: Pass the Chill, Five Cities to One Campus

TinyTea turns an outsized global relay into a short, repeatable countdown to one local university moment. A cold TinyTea bottle passes through five major international cities in seven days, linked by one globally famous celebrity appearing in person at every stop. Each handoff brings the bottle closer to its real destination: the local students TinyTea was made to reach.

Every live public event is a tightly controlled ten-minute appearance at a custom interactive digital billboard. The celebrity arrives, joins one local participant, and places a TinyTea bottle against a marked interaction point. The billboard responds by cooling an on-screen bottle from warm to iced, then reveals the next city. The celebrity says, “Pass the chill,” opens the real bottle, and hands it to the participant. A single professional filmmaker captures the same six-shot sequence at every stop, avoiding separate crews, stages, presenters, or event formats.

The dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application performs one function. Pointing a phone at a TinyTea bottle or campaign image triggers the same warm-to-cold transformation, reveals the latest relay clip, and displays the next stop. The billboard interaction, AR effect, social transition, and cinematic film therefore reuse one visual asset and one cooling animation.

The fifth handoff ends on the local university campus. Instead of passing the bottle to another international participant, the celebrity passes it to a TinyTea student representative. Students then recreate the simple “Pass the chill” handoff with a cold bottle and post it to Instagram or TikTok through their university communities. TinyTea reposts the clearest clips during launch month, keeping the global spectacle focused on local awareness and the product’s lightly sweetened, cold-serving experience.

Execution is built around a single seven-day route and one traveling production unit. Day one completes the AR build, billboard package, route rehearsal, and master shot template. Days two through six each cover one city event, billboard interaction, celebrity handoff, and same-day vertical edit. Day seven delivers the university finale, cinematic master film, and social cutdowns. All city events use identical signage, timing, technical files, camera positions, and participation rules.

Because the total production budget is below $1,000, the concept can proceed only after the celebrity’s five-city in-person availability, travel, public-event permissions, billboard access, and required professional services are confirmed at no charge to the production budget before day one. The cash budget is reserved for the reusable bottle-cooling asset, minimal single-feature AR build, identical event materials, and final editing. If those mandatory no-cost dependencies are not secured, the campaign cannot truthfully satisfy the brief’s budget and seven-day requirements.
