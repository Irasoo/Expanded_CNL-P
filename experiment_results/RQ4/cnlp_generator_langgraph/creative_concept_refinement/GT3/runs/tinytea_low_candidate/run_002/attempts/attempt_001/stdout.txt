==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**TinyTea: The Cold Break Relay**

TinyTea turns its launch into one recognizable seven-second ritual: open a cold bottle, take one sip, and describe the lightly sweetened taste in three words. The ritual travels from one globally famous celebrity to university students around the world, then lands directly in local campus communities.

The celebrity appears in person at the launch Cold Break in one major international city. In a single professionally filmed vertical take, they lift a visibly cold TinyTea bottle, sip, give three concise sensory words such as “lightly sweet, refreshing,” and pass the bottle toward camera. The shot becomes the campaign’s master asset and establishes the simple behavior students can copy.

The same Cold Break is performed as a short live public event in five major international cities. Each event uses the identical low-complexity format: a TinyTea table, bottled iced tea, a host, a seven-second countdown, and participants recording their three-word reactions. The celebrity’s master shot opens every event on screen or social feed, creating a relay without requiring a celebrity appearance in every city, additional scripts, or separate creative production.

Each city receives one custom interactive digital billboard using the same technical template. The only changes are the city name and the local reaction feed. When a participant raises a TinyTea bottle toward the billboard, the screen reveals the city’s Cold Break prompt and a three-word reaction. It then displays a QR code to the dedicated TinyTea AR application. The billboard experience has one purpose: turn the physical cold-bottle gesture into a recognizable campaign signature and direct people into the relay.

The AR application is deliberately limited to one recording function. It places a virtual cold TinyTea bottle in the user’s camera view, adds a condensation effect, and records a seven-second Cold Break clip with space for three words. Students can participate in the Instagram and TikTok relay without downloading the app; the application is the required extended experience for billboard participants, while native social posting keeps campus participation immediate.

During TinyTea’s first month, local university communities become the relay’s final and most important chapter. Campus students record Cold Breaks between lectures, study sessions, and activities, using sensory descriptions grounded in the product’s lightly sweetened, cold-serving benefit. Each participant tags another student or campus community to continue the sequence. TinyTea publishes weekly edits pairing the international city reactions with local student clips, keeping the bottle, the sip, and the three-word taste response visible in every asset.

The execution is designed as one repeatable production system: one celebrity master take, one shared event run-of-show, one billboard interaction adapted only by city, one single-purpose AR effect, and vertical footage captured for Instagram and TikTok from the start. All five events use the same materials and instructions, and the professional shoot produces the celebrity opener, event templates, billboard assets, AR launch footage, and social cutdowns within the seven-day window.

Because the brief requires high-cost access and infrastructure within a budget below $1,000, this concept is deliverable only through confirmed donated or pre-existing celebrity participation, event access, billboard inventory, AR development, and production support. Those resources must be secured before the seven-day production period begins; the creative system itself minimizes new production by making every mandatory element a variation of the same Cold Break action.

EVALUATION
Originality: 7.0
Strategic fit: 6.0
Audience impact: 6.0
Executability: 2.0
Weighted overall score: 5.5
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The Cold Break Relay is a clear, product-specific organizing idea: the cold bottle, sip, and three-word taste reaction create a repeatable behavior that can work in vertical social content and campus communities. The relay structure gives the launch a recognizable signature and makes the lightly sweetened, cold-serving benefit visible in every participation moment. However, the core action is relatively simple and familiar, so its distinctiveness depends heavily on the scale and celebrity rather than on an unusually ownable creative device. Strategic fit is mixed. The concept addresses awareness, university students, Instagram, TikTok, local communities, and the product benefit, while incorporating every mandatory element in the brief. Its product role remains understandable, but the international celebrity, five-city events, interactive billboards, AR application, and cinematic production substantially outweigh the needs of a small local beverage launch. Executability is very weak under the supplied conditions. A globally famous celebrity appearing in person, five international public events, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day completion are not credible within a total budget below $1,000 unless all major resources are already donated or available. The concept acknowledges this dependency, but making delivery conditional does not resolve the explicit budget, timing, and resource constraints. Audience impact is reasonably strong because the seven-second format is easy to understand, visually demonstrable, and naturally suited to student participation and social tagging. It may earn attention through celebrity association and repeated reactions, but the sensory-response mechanic could become repetitive, and the billboard-to-AR path introduces friction for an audience that can already participate through native social platforms.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the Cold Break ritual, the visible cold bottle, and the three-word taste response as the campaign's simplest and strongest assets. Make the product benefit more specific and memorable by defining a sharper, consistent three-word response format tied to lightly sweetened taste and cold refreshment rather than allowing generic sensory descriptions. Clarify the relay's participation rule so each clip has an obvious reason to continue, such as passing the challenge to a named campus group or location. Reduce friction by treating Instagram and TikTok as the primary participation path and making the AR experience an optional enhancement rather than a required step wherever the brief permits. For the mandatory high-cost elements, secure written commitments for donated celebrity time, billboard inventory, event access, AR development, and production support before presenting the concept as executable; otherwise, the concept should be rated as a compliance-minded creative direction rather than a deliverable launch plan. If the constraints are negotiable, concentrate the live celebrity moment in the local university market and replace international infrastructure with lower-cost campus adaptations, which would materially improve strategic fit and executability while retaining the relay idea.
