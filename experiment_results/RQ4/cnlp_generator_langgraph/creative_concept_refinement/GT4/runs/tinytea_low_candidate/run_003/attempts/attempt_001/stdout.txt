==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
**TinyTea: “The Seven-Second Tiny Break”**

TinyTea turns the existing bottle-opening ritual into one highly recognizable seven-second pause between classes. The local university is the campaign’s lead location: students hold up a cold TinyTea, open it together, take one sip, and return to their day. The consistent prompt across every channel is: **“Open cold. Take a Tiny Break.”**

During one synchronized live public event, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at the lead location and starts the seven-second ritual. Small public gatherings in four other major international cities join live, creating the required five-city event without duplicating the celebrity appearance. The international locations provide scale, but the camera, social coverage, and call to action remain focused on students at TinyTea’s local university.

Each city’s custom interactive digital billboard has one interaction: a seven-second countdown activated by a participant. At zero, the screen shows the celebrity opening a cold TinyTea and prompts everyone nearby to do the same. No additional games, data collection, or content variations are required.

A dedicated TinyTea AR mobile app performs one function. Pointing the camera at the bottle label or event marker adds a seven-second countdown, the city name, and the line “Tiny Break taken.” It then saves a vertical clip ready for Instagram or TikTok. The bottle is essential to the interaction, keeping TinyTea and its cold-serving benefit at the center.

Professional cinematic production is limited to one short master film assembled from the synchronized event: students between classes, five countdowns, five bottle openings, and the celebrity beginning the shared pause. The same footage becomes the campaign’s vertical social edits. Local university communities post the event time and reshare student clips throughout the first launch month, extending awareness beyond the single event.

All production is completed within seven days by using one script, one seven-second action, one AR effect, one billboard interaction, and one shared visual package. TinyTea’s sub-$1,000 production spend is reserved for the repeatable local-campus materials and social assets. Execution of the mandatory celebrity, international events, billboards, app, and cinematic production is conditional on those resources being secured within that same cap; without no-cost access to them, the brief’s mandatory scope cannot be produced as specified.

Weighted overall score: 3.8
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The seven-second ritual is a clear, memorable, and product-specific organizing idea: it turns opening a cold bottle into a repeatable pause that can work across Instagram, TikTok, campus communities, billboards, and live events. The bottle remains central, and the lightly sweetened cold-serving benefit is expressed through the opening and sipping behavior. The synchronized five-city structure and celebrity participation could create strong attention if fully resourced. However, Strategic Fit is severely reduced because the concept accepts rather than solves the brief's mandatory requirements within a very low budget and seven-day production window. The statement that execution is conditional on securing no-cost celebrity access, international events, interactive billboards, an AR app, and cinematic production does not make those requirements realistic or compliant. Audience impact is moderately strong because the action is immediately understandable and socially shareable, but the international scale may distract from the local university objective and the ritual itself offers limited emotional or functional depth beyond visibility.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the single seven-second action, the cold-bottle focus, and the simple social prompt, but make the concept operationally credible by defining how every mandatory element is secured within the stated constraints, including confirmed no-cost access, locations, technical production, and celebrity participation. If those resources cannot be guaranteed, the concept needs a clear feasibility gate rather than presenting the campaign as executable. Strengthen local relevance by making the lead university the primary story and giving students a meaningful reason to participate, such as a campus-specific shared moment or social challenge, while keeping the international cities as a concise supporting layer. Clarify the AR and billboard interaction specifications so they can genuinely be built, tested, and deployed in seven days, and ensure the production plan accounts for approvals, app distribution, live synchronization, and content moderation.
