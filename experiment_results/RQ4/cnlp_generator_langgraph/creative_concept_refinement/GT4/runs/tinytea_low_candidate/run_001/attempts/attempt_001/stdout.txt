==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
**TinyTea, Big World: One Cold Moment**

TinyTea turns the first cold sip between classes into a global student ritual. A participant opens a chilled bottle, takes a lightly sweetened sip, and answers one prompt: “What’s your tiny win today?” Each response is passed from one city to the next before returning to local university students, making the contrast between a small everyday refreshment and an oversized worldwide launch the campaign’s memorable hook.

The campaign begins with a globally famous celebrity appearing in person at the first of five linked live public events in five major international cities. In a professionally produced cinematic opening, the celebrity takes a TinyTea from a visible chilled display, opens it, tastes it, shares a tiny win, and sends the prompt to the next city. Every location follows the same compact run sheet: one presenter, one chilled-product station, one fixed camera, and one custom interactive digital billboard. Participants submit a short tiny win through the billboard interface; an approved response appears beside a prominent cold TinyTea bottle, then triggers the handoff to the next city.

A dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application performs only the functions required for the ritual. Pointing it at a TinyTea bottle reveals the current five-city handoff, accepts one short tiny win, and creates a branded vertical clip for Instagram or TikTok. Local university community accounts publish the returning global handoff and invite students to respond with TinyTea during real campus moments such as finishing a lecture, submitting an assignment, completing a club task, or finding a brief break between classes. Every asset demonstrates the benefit in the same sequence: bottle retrieved cold, cap opened, lightly sweetened tea tasted, tiny win shared.

Execution uses a locked, single-template production system. The same script, cinematic shot list, event footprint, billboard layout, response rules, AR interface, social end card, and technical cue sequence are duplicated across all five cities. One central producer owns the seven-day schedule; each city has one local event owner and one technical owner. Celebrity attendance, travel, venues, permits, chilled TinyTea supply, professional film crew, billboard hardware and operators, connectivity, and the existing code base used to package the dedicated AR application must be contractually confirmed as no-cost contributions before the seven-day production period begins. The production budget of less than $1,000 is used only for template adaptation, app and billboard asset configuration, lightweight social editing, and contingency supplies.

A written go/no-go gate occurs before production starts. It requires signed confirmation of the celebrity’s in-person appearance, all five venues and permits, travel, professional production services, compatible interactive billboards, local operators, connectivity tests, and an app foundation capable of being branded and released within the deadline. If any mandatory resource is missing, TinyTea rejects the campaign as infeasible under the brief rather than spending the launch budget on a partial execution. Once the gate is passed, no new features, locations, messages, or production formats may be added during the seven-day build.

The cinematic film launches the story, while standardized vertical edits document each city-to-city handoff and drive attention back to the local campus audience: “The world shared its tiny wins. What’s yours?” TinyTea remains the physical and visual trigger throughout, positioning its cold, lightly sweetened tea as a simple refreshment for the small wins that shape student life.

Weighted overall score: 5.7
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The tiny-win prompt and the handoff from one city to the next create a differentiated, memorable structure, and the repeated cold sip, lightly sweetened tea, and student-life moments give the idea a meaningful product role. The concept clearly targets university students and provides social content suited to Instagram, TikTok, and university communities. However, the global celebrity, five-city live events, interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day deadline make the execution extremely difficult under a total budget below $1,000. The concept acknowledges this conflict through a go/no-go gate, but it assumes that celebrity participation, travel, venues, permits, production, technology, operators, and an app foundation can all be secured as no-cost contributions; those resources are not established in the brief. As a result, the idea is strategically relevant and potentially engaging, but its central promise is operationally high-risk.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the tiny-win ritual, city-to-city handoff, and visible cold product demonstration, but make resource validation the first deliverable rather than treating in-kind support as an assumption. Obtain signed commitments and technical proofs for the celebrity, all five events, permits, billboard interactivity, connectivity, professional production, and an adaptable AR foundation before approving any production spend. Define a tightly limited interaction for both the billboards and app so the required technology can be configured quickly without feature creep, and use one locked visual and editorial template across every location. Make the local university audience the primary endpoint of every handoff by routing each international response into campus-community posts and student participation prompts. If any mandatory commitment cannot be verified before the seven-day window, reject the concept under the stated constraints rather than producing a partial campaign that would lose its central idea.
