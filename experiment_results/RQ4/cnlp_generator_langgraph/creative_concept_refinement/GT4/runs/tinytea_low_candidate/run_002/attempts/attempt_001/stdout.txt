==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
**TinyTea: “The Cold Pass — Five Cities, One Campus Moment”**

TinyTea launches with one unmistakable student ritual: show a chilled bottle, take a sip, and pass the frame to someone else. The simple handoff makes TinyTea’s core benefit visible and gives local university students a repeatable way to join the launch between classes, study sessions, and campus plans.

During the seven-day launch window, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at one live public TinyTea event in each of five major international cities. At every event, the celebrity performs the same ten-second Cold Pass with a chilled TinyTea bottle, handing the moment to students in the crowd. Each city continues the same filmed handoff, creating one continuous relay that ends with local university students joining from their campuses.

A custom interactive digital billboard at each event displays the live relay. Students point the dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application at the billboard or a TinyTea bottle to activate one simple pass effect. The app immediately prompts them to record the same action: show the chilled bottle, take a sip, and pass the frame toward a friend, with direct sharing to Instagram or TikTok. There are no additional challenges, filters, formats, or participation rules.

The celebrity and five-city appearances function as the launch trigger; the campaign’s sustained audience is local university students. After the seven-day relay, TinyTea publishes short vertical edits from the same footage throughout the first month, while university communities receive a single clear invitation: “Catch the Cold Pass with TinyTea.” Students can participate from campus with their own chilled bottle, extending the city-to-city relay into local student networks and making TinyTea visible in the everyday moments when a cold drink is relevant.

To meet the very-low-budget and seven-day requirement without adding production complexity, the concept is approved only as a compact execution using pre-existing, confirmed access to the globally famous celebrity, event locations, billboard systems, AR application capability, permissions, and professional production resources. TinyTea funds no new travel, venue construction, billboard hardware, app build, celebrity fee, or separate content system within the stated sub-$1,000 production budget. One ten-second action, one AR effect, one billboard interaction, one cinematic relay film, and short vertical edits provide the complete campaign system. Without those resources already confirmed and available at no additional production cost, the mandatory execution cannot proceed within the brief’s budget and seven-day schedule.

Creative Evaluation

Weighted overall score: 5.2
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The Cold Pass is a familiar social-video handoff mechanic, but connecting the gesture to a chilled TinyTea bottle, a sip, and student participation gives it a clear product role and some meaningful distinction. Strategically, the concept supports first-month awareness, visibly communicates cold consumption, includes the required channels, and gives university communities a simple participation format. However, the five-city international activation is disproportionate to the local student audience, and the lightly sweetened benefit is not communicated. The celebrity, continuous relay, and repeatable ten-second action could earn attention and generate memorable short-form content, although requiring access to a bottle or AR app adds participation friction. Executability is critically weak: the concept satisfies the mandatory elements only by assuming free, pre-existing access to celebrity appearances, five international events, interactive billboards, an AR app, permissions, travel, and professional cinematic production. Those resources are not supplied by the brief and cannot realistically be secured and delivered within seven days for less than $1,000.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the single Cold Pass action, visible chilled bottle, and campus-friendly vertical format, but turn the execution caveat into a concrete feasibility gate: do not approve production unless every mandatory resource is documented as already contracted, funded outside the stated production budget, scheduled, and technically ready. Define one tightly standardized capture and publishing workflow for all five cities so the seven-day schedule has a credible operational path. Make local university students more central in the resulting edits and calls to action, clarify how they can participate without unnecessary app friction, and add a concise visual or spoken cue for 'lightly sweetened' alongside the cold-serving behavior. Because the mandatory scope cannot otherwise fit the budget, the concept should explicitly report the brief as infeasible rather than treating unconfirmed donated capabilities as an execution solution.
