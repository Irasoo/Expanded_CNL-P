==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**TinyTea: The Cold Open Relay**

TinyTea turns a cold tea break into one recognizable student signal: **open the bottle, pour over ice, and pass the moment forward**. The relay begins with a globally famous celebrity appearing in person at a live public event in each of five major international cities. At every stop, the celebrity performs the same brief handoff with local students, creating a single repeatable action that makes TinyTea’s benefit immediately visible: lightly sweetened tea, served cold, ready for the next break.

The campaign’s main film follows one continuous Cold Open Relay across the five cities. Each location contributes one short handoff shot, using the same camera position, timing, script, and bottle movement. The film ends with the relay arriving at a university student, who opens a TinyTea during an ordinary break between classes. The message is: **“Open cold. Pass the moment.”**

In each city, a custom interactive digital billboard displays the relay paused just before the handoff. A student activates the next movement through the dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application. The app uses one fixed experience across all five cities: it places a virtual TinyTea bottle into the user’s environment, prompts the user to perform the same open-and-pass action, and creates a short vertical clip for Instagram or TikTok. City names and event footage change; the interaction, artwork, technical specification, and user prompt do not.

Local university communities are the campaign’s primary audience and participation engine. During the first month, TinyTea publishes short clips from the international relay alongside student submissions showing their own cold tea break: outside a lecture hall, in a library, at rehearsal, or on the way home. Campus community pages and student groups receive a simple posting package built from the same master assets. The international events provide attention, while the familiar student break gives the campaign local relevance and a behavior that is easy to repeat.

Executability is built into the concept through strict production control. Before production begins, the campaign must have written confirmation of the celebrity appearance, all five event permissions, billboard access and interactivity specifications, the dedicated app’s technical readiness, image and music rights, and the seven-day delivery schedule. These are mandatory launch inputs, not assumed support or additional creative ideas. If any required input is unconfirmed, production does not proceed under this concept.

Once confirmed, the campaign uses one locked seven-day schedule: pre-approved master assets and scripts are finalized first; the same compact event format is captured in each city; one professional cinematic shot list covers every location; one AR interaction is built for all markets; one billboard template is adapted with city-specific footage; and the master film is cut into vertical social versions. No city receives a separate mechanic, app experience, script, or visual system. The sub-$1,000 production budget covers only the reusable creative toolkit, editing, social cutdowns, and asset preparation; the mandatory celebrity, event, billboard, application, access, and production resources must be confirmed as available within the brief before approval.

TinyTea remains central in every execution. The bottle is opened and served cold at the live events, drives the relay in the film, forms the visual pause on the billboards, anchors the AR interaction, and gives university students a concrete action to copy. The result is one disciplined launch device that connects a global celebrity moment to a locally repeatable student ritual throughout TinyTea’s first month.

EVALUATION
Originality: 6.0
Strategic fit: 4.0
Audience impact: 6.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 4.4
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The repeatable open-and-pass relay is a clear product-specific behavior and makes serving the tea cold visible, giving the idea more distinction than generic launch content. Its global celebrity, five-city structure, interactive billboards, and AR layer add attention value, but the underlying relay mechanic is relatively familiar, supporting a 6 for originality. The concept addresses launch awareness, uses the requested Instagram, TikTok, and university-community channels, and connects TinyTea to a simple student break ritual. However, the international spectacle is disproportionate to a small local beverage company and does not strongly prioritize local university students. The concept also treats the explicit requirements as possible only if extensive resources are already confirmed, while the supplied brief provides no such confirmations. This weakens strategic fit to 4 because the low budget and seven-day constraint are central parts of the brief. The relay is easy to understand, visually demonstrable, and potentially engaging for students, especially through user-generated open-and-pass clips. Its emotional and behavioral hook is clear but not unusually powerful, and the celebrity-led international framing may feel remote from local campus life, resulting in a 6 for audience impact. Executability is 1 because the mandatory combination of a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, five international live events, interactive billboards in five cities, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and full completion within seven days is not credibly achievable within the stated production budget below $1,000 based on the supplied information. The concept's confirmation checklist identifies risks but does not solve them or establish that the required resources exist.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the strong open-and-pass ritual, consistent visual language, and student-generated clips, but make the concept approval-dependent on documented commitments for every mandatory resource before any production or public promise is made. Add a detailed seven-day dependency schedule that assigns each required celebrity, city event, billboard, AR, rights, and production input an owner, deadline, technical acceptance criterion, and cancellation decision; without those confirmations, the concept should be rejected as unworkable rather than treated as launch-ready. Tighten the creative role of the celebrity and international events so each directly hands the relay to local university communities, with campus participation as the primary story rather than a follow-on. Define one minimal AR interaction, one billboard interaction, and one cinematic master setup that can genuinely be reused across all cities, and validate their technical and rights requirements before approval. The remaining major weakness cannot be solved through creative simplification alone: the brief must provide donated, sponsored, or otherwise confirmed access to the mandatory celebrity, events, billboards, application development, and professional production while keeping the stated cash budget constraint.
