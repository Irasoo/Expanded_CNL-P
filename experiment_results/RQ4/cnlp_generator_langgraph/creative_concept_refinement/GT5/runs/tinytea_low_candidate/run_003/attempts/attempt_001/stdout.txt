==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
# TinyTea: “Pass the Cold Home”

## Core Idea

TinyTea turns a global relay into a local student ritual. A globally famous celebrity starts the same simple action in five major international cities: open a cold bottle of lightly sweetened TinyTea, take a sip, and pass the “cold break” onward. Every city sends its moment back to one local university community, making the small local launch the campaign’s home base rather than a footnote.

The central thought is: **the cold break can travel the world, then come back to your campus.**

## Main Execution

Over seven days, TinyTea stages five compact, repeatable public events using the same event format: one visible TinyTea bottle station, one celebrity cold-sip handoff, one student recording point, and one short call to action:

**“Take a cold break. Pass it home.”**

The celebrity appears in person at each event and performs the campaign’s signature action: taking a sip of chilled TinyTea and handing the bottle to a student. Each student records a short vertical handoff video showing the bottle, the cold sip, and the phrase “passing it home.”

The five events are filmed with one consistent, concise shot structure for rapid editing into Instagram Reels, TikToks, and local university-community posts. No additional storylines, sets, or complex event programming are required. The same bottle station, handoff action, framing, and copy are repeated in every city.

Each city has one custom interactive digital billboard showing a giant TinyTea bottle. When a student activates the billboard interaction, a visible cold stream travels from that city toward the local university hub. The billboard then displays the student’s recorded handoff and the message:

**“Your cold break is heading home.”**

The dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application provides one fast scan-to-record experience. Students scan the TinyTea bottle or event marker, see the animated cold stream leave the bottle, record their handoff, and share the finished vertical clip directly to Instagram or TikTok. The experience does not require multiple game modes, profiles, or extended participation.

The local university community is the campaign’s primary destination. Its social channels publish a daily compilation of the five-city relay, alongside student handoff clips made locally. The final launch-day edit shows the celebrity beginning the relay, the five cities passing it onward, and local students receiving it with cold bottles of TinyTea.

## Role of TinyTea

TinyTea is the object that makes the relay possible. The bottle is the AR trigger, the celebrity’s handoff object, the billboard’s central visual, and the required prop in every student video. Its chilled appearance and the repeated sip demonstrate the key benefit directly: a lightly sweetened bottled tea ready to enjoy cold during a short student break.

The campaign keeps the spectacle consistent and small in format while directing its meaning back to local university students. Every touchpoint repeats one memorable behavior:

**Open TinyTea cold, take a sip, and pass the cold break home.**

EVALUATION
Originality: 6.0
Strategic fit: 4.0
Audience impact: 6.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 4.4
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The relay mechanic is clear, repeatable, and meaningfully tied to TinyTea: the bottle, cold sip, handoff, and return-to-campus idea give the product a visible role. The phrase "Pass the Cold Home" provides a usable behavioral hook, and the student-created vertical videos fit Instagram, TikTok, and university communities. However, the global celebrity relay, five international live events, interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, cinematic production, and seven-day timeline make the concept highly distinctive mainly through scale rather than through a uniquely ownable idea. Strategic fit is weakened because the concept is built around an international spectacle that does not align credibly with a very low budget or a small local beverage launch; the product benefit is simple and local, while most of the attention goes to the celebrity and global infrastructure. The concept could attract attention from students, but the complexity may obscure the basic reason to try TinyTea. Executability is critically weak: the mandatory requirements conflict with the stated budget and production timeline, and the concept does not provide a credible way to deliver all required elements without assuming unavailable resources, partnerships, or capabilities.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the strongest assets: the cold-break ritual, the simple handoff, the bottle-centered visual identity, and the return-to-campus message. To improve strategic fit, make the university community the primary story rather than treating it as the destination of a global campaign, and connect participation to a concrete local student behavior such as sharing a study break or passing a bottle across campus. To improve audience impact, sharpen the demonstration of lightly sweetened cold tea and use a short, instantly recognizable participation prompt that works without explaining the wider relay. To address executability, the concept would need a fundamental production redesign: replace the international celebrity appearances, five-city events, custom billboards, dedicated AR app, and cinematic production with lightweight student content and existing social or campus channels. Under the stated constraints, those mandatory elements cannot be credibly delivered on the available budget and timeline, so refinement is only possible if the brief's requirements are revised or independently funded and supplied.
