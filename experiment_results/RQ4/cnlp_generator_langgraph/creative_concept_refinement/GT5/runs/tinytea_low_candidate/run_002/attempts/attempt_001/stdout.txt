==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT
**TinyTea: The Five-City Cold Start Relay**

TinyTea turns the first cold sip into a shared signal that a student’s day has begun. The campaign opens with a globally famous celebrity appearing in person at one public launch event, taking a chilled TinyTea bottle from a student, saying “Start cold,” and taking the first sip. That action triggers a synchronized five-city relay: public launch moments take place in five major international cities, each using the same short sequence, bottle reveal, countdown, and “TinyTea. Start cold.” sign-off.

The events are deliberately compact and repeatable. Each uses an existing public setting, a TinyTea bottle, a small branded marker, and the same five-minute first-sip action. Students are invited to show the moment before the sip, the cold bottle opening, and the first taste of lightly sweetened tea, then identify what they are starting: a lecture, commute, study session, break, or night out. This keeps the international spectacle connected to the everyday university moments TinyTea is made for.

A custom interactive digital billboard in each city carries the live relay countdown and displays the shared “Cold Start” prompt. Students point the dedicated TinyTea AR application at a TinyTea bottle to activate one simple effect: a visible cold wave passes through the screen, the words “STARTED” appear, and the user receives the same first-sip recording frame used at the live events. The application ends with a direct prompt to post the clip on Instagram or TikTok.

The celebrity’s in-person first sip is the campaign’s opening trigger and appears in the professionally produced launch film. The five-city relay, billboard countdowns, AR activation, and student clips are edited into short vertical videos using one consistent shot list: before the sip, bottle opened cold, first taste, and “started” reaction. The production system uses one script, one visual treatment, one AR interaction, one billboard template, and one repeatable event format across all locations, allowing the mandatory elements to function as a single campaign rather than separate experiences.

During TinyTea’s first month, local university communities repeat the format with their own cold-start clips. TinyTea is not just shown in the campaign: its chilled bottle is the trigger, its lightly sweetened taste is the payoff, and its simple first-sip ritual gives students a memorable behavior to copy. Every execution closes with: **“TinyTea. Start cold.”**

EVALUATION
Originality: 7.0
Strategic fit: 3.0
Audience impact: 6.0
Executability: 1.0
Weighted overall score: 4.4
Weak criteria: executability
Evaluation reasons: The concept has a differentiated central behavior: the first cold sip becomes a repeatable Cold Start signal, with the bottled tea acting as the trigger and the lightly sweetened chilled taste as the payoff. The consistent before-sip, bottle-opening, first-taste, and started-reaction format is well suited to Instagram, TikTok, and university communities. This supports a 7 for originality and a 6 for audience impact because the ritual is clear, easy to imitate, and potentially engaging for students. Strategic fit is substantially weakened by the explicit brief constraints. A very low-budget local launch aimed at university students is paired with a globally famous celebrity, five live events in major international cities, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and a seven-day production deadline. The concept repeats these requirements effectively, but it does not solve the conflict between them and TinyTea's audience, budget, and launch objective, so the product risks becoming a small prop inside an unrealistic global spectacle. Executability is a 1 because the mandatory combination of celebrity participation, international events, custom billboards, an AR app, professional production, and seven-day completion is not credibly achievable within the supplied budget and timeline without assuming unprovided resources or partnerships.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the Cold Start ritual, the short repeatable shot list, and the 'TinyTea. Start cold.' sign-off as the creative core. Make the product benefit more explicit at the moment of payoff by showing the chilled bottle and describing the lightly sweetened taste in the content rather than relying mainly on the celebrity or spectacle. To improve audience relevance, frame every relay moment around recognizable student starts and give university communities a clear, low-effort participation mechanic. To address the weakest aspect, the brief must provide a credible production path for the mandatory elements, such as confirmed celebrity access, existing international event or billboard partnerships, an already-built AR platform, and production resources available within seven days. If those resources cannot be confirmed, the concept should be presented as a creative platform with a feasibility warning rather than as an executable first-month campaign; no refinement can make all mandatory requirements workable within the stated budget and timeline without changing the constraints or adding verified resources.
