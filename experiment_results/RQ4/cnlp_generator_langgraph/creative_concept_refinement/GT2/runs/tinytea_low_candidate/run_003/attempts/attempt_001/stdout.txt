==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
**TinyTea’s Seven-Second Cold Break**

TinyTea turns a short student break into a synchronized global signal for a local launch. At a fixed time, students in five major international cities and participating local university communities raise a chilled TinyTea, pause for seven seconds, and say: “Tiny tea. Big break.” The repeated action makes the bottle, its cold serving benefit, and TinyTea’s name the campaign’s memory trigger.

On day one, the globally famous celebrity appears in person at the flagship public event and performs the first seven-second break. Four student hosts lead the identical live event format in the other cities. Each event uses the same minimal setup: a marked standing spot, chilled TinyTea bottles, a phone camera, and one instruction. No stages, performers, or separate event concepts are required. Local university communities make the campaign relevant by organizing campus participation, recording their own seven-second vertical clips, and reposting them through Instagram, TikTok, and university channels during the first month after launch.

In each city, one custom interactive digital billboard shows the TinyTea bottle, a seven-second countdown, and the line “Tiny tea. Big break.” Its interaction opens TinyTea’s dedicated AR mobile application, which contains one focused camera experience: a cold-mist countdown and TinyTea bottle frame that lets students recreate the same seven-second clip on campus, at an event, or beside the billboard. Every public and digital touchpoint repeats the same action, visual cue, and brand line.

Professional cinematic production is limited to one master vertical film captured across the five synchronized events, with close shots of cold bottles, the celebrity’s opening break, student participation, and local campus clips. All Instagram and TikTok edits come from that single film and the community submissions. The campaign therefore uses one ritual, one AR experience, one billboard template, one event format, and one master edit across every required element.

The seven-day production is organized as a locked production package: day one confirms the celebrity, public permissions, billboard access, app publishing support, city hosts, and local university contacts; days two and three finalize the shared creative assets and AR build; days four and five capture the five live events; day six completes the master film and social cutdowns; day seven publishes the film, AR experience, billboard content, and campus posting kit. The sub-$1,000 cash budget covers TinyTea, ice, simple event markers, app publishing, and the centralized edit. The mandatory celebrity participation, five-city access, billboard inventory, app development, event permissions, and local capture support must be supplied or confirmed as existing in-kind resources before day one; without those confirmed resources, the concept does not proceed.

Weakest criteria: executability
Evaluation: The seven-second cold-break ritual is a clear, memorable device that connects TinyTea's name, chilled serving benefit, and student break occasions. Repeating one action and line across short-form video, campus communities, events, billboards, and AR gives the idea coherence and makes it suitable for Instagram and TikTok. The celebrity launch moment and synchronized participation could attract attention, but the international scale is disproportionate to awareness for a small local beverage launch, and requiring users to install a dedicated app creates friction. Most importantly, the concept is not executable within the supplied sub-$1,000 budget and seven-day schedule. It assumes that celebrity participation, five-city logistics, permissions, interactive billboard inventory, application development and publishing, and production support will be provided in kind, but the brief supplies none of those resources. Making those assumptions prerequisites does not resolve the budget or timing conflict. The schedule also leaves no credible margin for contracting, travel, permits, app-store review, technical testing, or a professional five-city cinematic production.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the seven-second ritual, repeated brand line, cold-bottle imagery, and student-generated vertical clips because they provide the concept's strongest product and audience connection. Since every mandatory element must remain, replace unsupported claims of feasibility with a concrete minimum execution specification for each one: define the celebrity's exact appearance, a simultaneous low-footprint event format, the billboard interaction, the smallest viable dedicated AR app, and the footage required for the master film. Explicitly identify all celebrity, travel, venue, billboard, development, publishing, permitting, and production costs and dependencies. The concept can proceed under the stated constraints only if documented third-party support covers them before the seven-day production period begins; without that confirmation, it should be presented as infeasible rather than as a sub-$1,000 plan. Reduce audience friction by making the core seven-second participation work directly through Instagram and TikTok, while retaining the mandatory app as an optional enhanced version of the same effect.
