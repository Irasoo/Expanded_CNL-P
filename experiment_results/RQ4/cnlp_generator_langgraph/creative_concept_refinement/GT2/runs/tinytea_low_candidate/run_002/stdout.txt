==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
**TinyTea: “The Five-City Cold Check”**

For seven days, one chilled bottle of TinyTea becomes the focus of a tightly structured global-to-local relay. A globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live public event in each of five major international cities and performs the same five-second ritual: raise the bottle, take the first cold sip, and describe the lightly sweetened taste in three words. Each appearance ends with the celebrity naming TinyTea and passing the Cold Check directly to students at the local university.

Custom interactive digital billboards in all five cities show the celebrity’s latest three-word reaction and a live sequence of student responses. Attendees use a dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality app to scan the billboard or bottle, see the five-city relay, record their own three-word Cold Check, and export it immediately as a vertical Instagram or TikTok post. The interaction requires no account creation or additional steps beyond scanning, recording, and sharing.

Professional cinematic production follows one repeatable shot list across every city: cold bottle close-up, first sip, three-word reaction, and campus challenge. This creates five concise launch films and reusable edits without introducing additional storylines or formats. During the remainder of TinyTea’s first month, local university communities continue the same ritual between classes, using the prompt: “Cold TinyTea. Three words. Go.”

TinyTea remains the reason for every interaction: the cold bottle starts the ritual, its lightly sweetened taste generates the three-word response, and its name is spoken and shown in every video. The global celebrity relay supplies the opening attention, while the direct campus handoff turns that attention toward the local students the launch needs to reach.

Execution begins only when the brief owner has secured and supplied the mandatory celebrity participation, five international event locations, billboard inventory and technical access, AR application resources, professional production crew, travel, permissions, and logistics. The campaign’s below-$1,000 production budget is reserved for the local university rollout and cannot fund those mandatory global requirements. All supplied elements must be confirmed before the seven-day production window starts; without that confirmation, the concept cannot be produced within the stated constraints.

Weakest criteria: executability
Evaluation: The three-word Cold Check is a clear, repeatable participation mechanic that connects directly to the cold serving occasion and lightly sweetened taste, giving the concept some product-specific distinction. The global relay, celebrity endorsement, live events, billboards, AR app, and cinematic films create a potentially attention-grabbing launch, and the campus handoff provides a logical bridge to university students. However, the concept depends on mandatory celebrity participation, five international events, international billboard access, a dedicated AR application, professional production, travel, and extensive permissions, while treating those requirements as already secured outside the stated total budget. The brief provides no basis for assuming those resources exist. This makes the concept strategically noncompliant with the very-low-budget constraint and operationally infeasible within seven days. Audience impact is potentially strong because the ritual is immediately understandable and easy to share, but the global spectacle may be distant from local students and the campaign's reach depends on major unsupported infrastructure.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the Cold Check mechanic, the three-word response, the cold bottle close-up, and the direct campus handoff. To improve strategic fit and executability, redesign the mandatory elements so they are genuinely deliverable within the stated constraints rather than assuming they are supplied: define a credible form of celebrity in-person participation, limit the live relay to resources actually available, replace custom international billboards and a dedicated AR app with low-cost university screens, QR-linked mobile web effects, and existing social platforms, and use phone-shot or locally produced vertical video instead of professional cinematic production. If the brief's mandatory global requirements cannot be changed, the concept should be rejected as non-producible rather than presented as a viable campaign. The student ritual should remain the main expression of TinyTea, with the celebrity serving only as an attention trigger.
