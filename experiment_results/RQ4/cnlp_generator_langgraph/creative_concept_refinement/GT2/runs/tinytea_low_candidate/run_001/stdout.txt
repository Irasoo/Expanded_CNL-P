==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Final Creative Concept
TinyTea’s **“The Cold Relay”** turns one cold sip into a synchronized relay from five major international cities back to local university students. At a single scheduled moment, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at the launch event, raises a chilled TinyTea bottle, takes one sip, and passes the cue to students gathered at live public events in the other four cities. Every event follows the same compact format: a countdown, the bottle raised to camera, one sip, and a genuine reaction to TinyTea’s lightly sweet taste served cold. The action is deliberately simple so students can recognize it instantly and recreate it throughout the first month after launch.

Custom interactive digital billboards in all five cities run the same countdown and display the relay prompt: **“Raise cold. Sip light. Pass it on.”** When students scan a TinyTea bottle with the dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, a frost effect appears over the bottle, followed by the city-to-city relay animation and a recording screen for their own sip-and-reaction video. The app has one purpose: recognize TinyTea, visualize its cold serving benefit, and make the repeatable behavior easy to share on Instagram and TikTok. University communities receive the synchronized time, event details, and a simple invitation to post their own relay reaction using the campaign tag.

TinyTea is the essential trigger and proof of the idea: its bottle activates the AR experience, its chilled appearance creates the visual cue, and its lightly sweet taste supplies the reaction students share. To make the concept executable within seven days and a production budget below $1,000, every city uses the same pre-approved event layout, billboard artwork, AR flow, camera setup, shot list, countdown file, and edit template. Professional cinematic production captures the celebrity’s in-person launch, the five live events, bottle close-ups, and student reactions as one modular package for rapid Instagram and TikTok edits. The campaign begins only after written commitments are secured for the celebrity appearance, five event sites, billboard inventory and integration, dedicated AR app development, and professional production; TinyTea’s budget then covers only product, ice, and essential event materials, with no new creative, technical, or production requirements added during the seven-day window.

Weakest criteria: executability
Evaluation: The synchronized cold-sip relay is a differentiated, memorable device, and the bottle, chilled serving cue, lightly sweet taste reaction, and AR frost effect give TinyTea a meaningful role rather than treating it as incidental branding. The simple raise-sip-pass behavior is clear and suitable for Instagram and TikTok, although celebrity-led challenges, synchronized events, reaction videos, and AR scans are familiar techniques. Strategic fit is mixed: the concept supports launch awareness, features the product benefit, and gives students a repeatable social action, but its five-city international scale is poorly focused on a small local brand and local university audience. Audience impact could be strong because the action is immediately understandable and visually recognizable, but requiring a dedicated app adds friction and the international spectacle may feel disconnected from students' local context. Executability is fundamentally untenable. Reusing templates does not make it possible to secure and deliver a globally famous celebrity in person, five international public events, five custom interactive billboard integrations, a dedicated AR application, and professional cinematic production within seven days and a total budget below $1,000. The proposed written commitments and externally covered resources are not supplied by the brief and cannot be assumed; shifting most costs outside TinyTea's stated budget does not resolve the constraint.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the strong raise-sip-pass behavior, consistent cold visual cue, and central bottle role, but specify an execution model that does not depend on unsupported donated inventory, celebrity access, development, venues, travel, or production. Because every mandatory element must remain, radically reduce complexity within each one: use the shortest viable celebrity appearance, tightly controlled event footprints, one reusable billboard interaction adapted across locations, a minimal AR app with only bottle recognition and recording, and a single standardized cinematic capture plan. Refocus all resulting content on local university students by making their reactions and campus participation the campaign's main story rather than the international cities. Even with those reductions, the brief's budget and seven-day deadline remain incompatible with the mandatory scope, so the concept should explicitly identify feasibility as unresolved instead of claiming that pre-approved templates or unspecified commitments make it executable.
