==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Checkout Button Guardrail Test evaluated the hypothesis that changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” would increase purchase conversion over 14 days. Control had 10,000 users and a 10.0% conversion rate; Variant had 10,000 users and a 12.0% conversion rate. Variant improved conversion by 2.0 percentage points, a 20.0% relative lift. The difference is statistically significant (two-sided p = 6.19×10⁻⁶; 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference: 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points).

The result is marked underpowered against the specified 1 percentage point minimum detectable effect at 80% power, but this is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result. No sample ratio mismatch was detected, so the observed 50/50 allocation does not raise an experiment-validity concern.

Recommendation: Investigate before shipping. Although the primary metric improved significantly, customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%, and the product team explicitly considers this a meaningful guardrail concern. Determine why “Buy Now” is generating additional support contacts, review the nature and product impact of those contacts, and test mitigations or revised button wording. Do not broadly ship the variant until the guardrail concern is understood and resolved.
