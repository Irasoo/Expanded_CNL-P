==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
Checkout Button Guardrail Test

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

**Results:** Over 14 days, Control had 10,000 users and a 10.0% conversion rate; Variant had 10,000 users and a 12.0% conversion rate. The Variant improved conversion by 2.0 percentage points, a 20.0% relative lift. This result is statistically significant (two-sided p-value: 6.19 × 10⁻⁶; 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference: +1.13 to +2.87 percentage points).

**Validity:** No sample ratio mismatch was detected, consistent with the expected 50/50 traffic split. Sample-size adequacy was classified as underpowered for the specified 1-percentage-point minimum detectable effect at 80% power. This is a diagnostic limitation and does not override the statistically significant observed result.

**Guardrail:** The customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%, which the product team explicitly considers a meaningful negative effect and guardrail concern.

**Recommendation: Investigate.** The Variant produced a statistically significant conversion improvement, but the meaningful increase in support contacts prevents a ship decision based on the primary metric alone.

**Next steps:** Investigate why “Buy Now” increases support contacts, confirm the guardrail measurement and affected customer journeys, and assess whether the conversion gain justifies the support impact. Consider testing revised button copy that preserves purchase clarity while reducing confusion, with purchase conversion and support contact rate monitored together before making a rollout decision.
