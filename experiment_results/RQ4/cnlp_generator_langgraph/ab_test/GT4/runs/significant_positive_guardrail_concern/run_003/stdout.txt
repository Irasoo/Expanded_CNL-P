==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
**Checkout Button Guardrail Test**

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

**Observed results:** The experiment ran for 14 days with 10,000 users in Control and 10,000 in Variant. Purchase conversion increased from 10.0% in Control to 12.0% in Variant, an absolute increase of 2.0 percentage points and a relative lift of 20.0%. The two-sided p-value was 0.00000619, and the 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference was 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points. The result is statistically significant.

**Validity:** No sample ratio mismatch was detected, so the traffic allocation does not invalidate the experiment. The test was classified as underpowered for the specified 1 percentage point minimum detectable effect at 80% power; however, this diagnostic does not override the statistically significant result.

**Guardrail assessment:** Customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%. The product team explicitly considers this a meaningful negative effect, so there is a guardrail concern.

**Recommendation: Ship, with guardrail follow-up.** The variant produced a clear, statistically significant conversion improvement, and the experiment shows no sample ratio mismatch. However, the reported support-contact increase is an important negative effect. Ship the variant in a controlled manner, investigate why “Buy Now” increases support contacts, closely monitor both purchase conversion and support contact rate during rollout, and be prepared to pause or reverse the rollout if the guardrail degradation persists.
