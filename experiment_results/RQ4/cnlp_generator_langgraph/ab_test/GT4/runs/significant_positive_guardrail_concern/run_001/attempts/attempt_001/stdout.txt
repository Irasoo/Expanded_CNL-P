**Checkout Button Guardrail Test**

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

**Experiment results**
- Duration: 14 days
- Control: 10,000 users; 10.0% purchase conversion rate
- Variant: 10,000 users; 12.0% purchase conversion rate
- Absolute difference: +2.0 percentage points
- Relative lift: +20.0%
- Two-sided p-value: 6.19 × 10⁻⁶
- 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference: +1.13 to +2.87 percentage points
- Statistical significance: Significant at the 0.05 threshold
- Power status: Underpowered for the specified 1 percentage point minimum detectable effect at 80% power. This is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result.
- Sample ratio mismatch: Not detected

**Guardrail assessment:** The customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%. The product team explicitly considers this a meaningful negative effect, so there is a guardrail concern.

**Recommendation: Ship.** The variant produced a statistically significant purchase conversion increase, with no sample ratio mismatch invalidating the experiment. However, the substantial support-contact increase represents a meaningful downside and makes an unrestricted rollout risky.

**Next steps:** Ship with active monitoring and a controlled rollout where possible. Immediately investigate why “Buy Now” increases support contacts, identify and mitigate the underlying customer confusion or issue, and define rollback criteria. Continue tracking purchase conversion and support contact rate after release; pause or roll back the variant if the guardrail degradation persists or worsens.
