==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================
**Checkout Button Guardrail Test**

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

**Observed results:** The experiment ran for 14 days with 10,000 users in Control and 10,000 in Variant. Control conversion was 10.0%, while Variant conversion was 12.0%. This is an absolute increase of 2.0 percentage points and a relative lift of 20.0%. The two-sided p-value was 0.00000619, and the 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference was 1.13 to 2.87 percentage points. The result is statistically significant at the 0.05 threshold.

**Validity:** No sample ratio mismatch was detected, so the traffic allocation does not make the experiment inconclusive. The test was classified as underpowered for the specified 1-percentage-point minimum detectable effect at 80% power. This is a diagnostic limitation, but it does not override the statistically significant primary-metric result.

**Guardrail assessment:** Customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%. The product team explicitly considers this a meaningful negative effect, so a guardrail concern is present.

**Recommendation: Ship.** The Variant produced a statistically significant purchase-conversion improvement, but shipping carries a documented customer-support cost. Use a controlled rollout with close monitoring rather than an unmonitored full release.

**Next steps:** Investigate why “Buy Now” increases support contacts, confirm that the increase is attributable to the change, and define acceptable guardrail criteria. During rollout, monitor purchase conversion and support contact rate, and pause or roll back if the reported negative effect persists at an unacceptable level.
