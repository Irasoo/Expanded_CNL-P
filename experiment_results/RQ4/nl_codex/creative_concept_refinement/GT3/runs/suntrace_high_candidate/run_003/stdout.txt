Brand:
SunTrace

Product:
A facial sunscreen that is transparent when applied normally. The package includes reusable UV-sensitive stickers. When a sticker is placed on the skin before sun exposure and removed later, the protected area remains unchanged while the surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker shape.

Target audience:
Young adults aged 18-30 who understand that sunscreen is important but frequently forget to reapply it during outdoor activities.

Objective:
Make the invisible protective effect of sunscreen visually understandable and motivate users to remember sunscreen application and reapplication.

Key product benefit:
The sunscreen provides SPF 50 protection, while the included UV-sensitive demonstration stickers make the effect of sun exposure easier to visualize.

Channels:
TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social media communities.

Budget:
Low to medium.

Constraints:
Use only the real sunscreen and included demonstration stickers. Do not claim that the temporary visual demonstration measures exact UV damage. Do not use celebrities or expensive locations. The idea must be understandable within a short video.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**The Part You Protected**

SunTrace makes an invisible act visible through the shape of its reusable UV-sensitive sticker.

Each short video follows one young adult through a normal outdoor day: before leaving, they apply SunTrace facial sunscreen and place one included sticker on their skin. The video then jumps through simple, relatable moments such as walking across campus, sitting outside, or spending time outdoors. Later, they remove the sticker. The area beneath it remains visually unchanged while the surrounding exposed skin temporarily reveals the sticker’s shape, creating a clear visual contrast between the area protected by SunTrace and the area exposed to the sun.

The reveal is presented as a demonstration of SunTrace’s SPF 50 protection, not as a measurement of exact UV damage. The on-screen message is concise: **“You can’t see sunscreen working. Until you see what you protected.”**

The final beat turns the reveal into a repeatable reminder. The person looks at the visible sticker shape, reapplies SunTrace, and returns to their activity. The caption and end card read: **“Protect it before you go. Remember to reapply during your day outdoors.”** No specific reapplication interval is stated.

The format is a series of phone-shot TikTok and Instagram Reels videos, adapted for sharing in university social media communities. Each film uses the same simple structure: apply, place the sticker, spend time outdoors, remove, reveal, reapply. Different participants and everyday university settings can keep the series relatable without requiring celebrities, expensive locations, or additional production resources.

The core idea serves the audience by turning a benefit they normally cannot see into a brief, memorable visual moment. The sticker reveal helps them understand why sunscreen matters, while the repeated sequence links the product to the moment they are most likely to forget it: continuing an outdoor day after the initial application.
