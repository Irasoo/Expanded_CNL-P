CreativeDirectorAssistant: provide one complete creative brief including brand, product, target audience, objective, key product benefit, channels, budget, and constraints.


==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
TinyTea

Product:
A new bottled iced tea sold by a small local beverage company.

Target audience:
Local university students aged 18-25.

Objective:
Increase awareness of TinyTea during the first month after launch.

Key product benefit:
A lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and local university communities.

Budget:
Very low. The total campaign production budget is below $1,000.

Constraints:
The central campaign concept must include a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, a live public event in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, professional cinematic production, and completion of all production within seven days. Every element is mandatory and may not be removed.
================== END USER INPUT 1/1 ==================

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
TinyTea’s launch becomes **“The World’s Smallest Global Tour.”**

A globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live TinyTea launch event in each of five major international cities, delivering the same simple challenge: take a **Tiny Break** with a cold TinyTea. In every city, a custom interactive digital billboard displays a giant world map made from student-submitted campus moments. When people interact with the billboard, it reveals a small “Tiny Break” from a university student somewhere else in the world: between lectures, in the library, on a sunny bench, or during a late study session.

Students in local university communities join through a dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application. By pointing their phone at a TinyTea bottle, campus poster, or social post, they unlock the same miniature world map and add a short video of their own cold-tea moment. The app places each contribution on the map as a tiny virtual tour stop, connecting local student routines to the five-city events.

A professionally produced cinematic launch film follows the celebrity moving between the five live events, billboards, and AR map moments, while incorporating student footage from participating universities. The film and shorter edits run on Instagram and TikTok, with local university communities sharing their own stops and nominating the next campus location. The repeated visual is a TinyTea bottle acting as the landmark for every stop, from a student desk to an international city billboard.

TinyTea remains the reason for the ritual: it is the lightly sweetened bottled tea served cold when students need an easy, refreshing pause during a busy day. The campaign line is **“Big world. Tiny break.”** The local campus videos preserve the original audience connection and participation mechanic, while the celebrity, five-city events, interactive billboards, AR application, and cinematic film make the “smallest global tour” the organizing idea rather than separate campaign elements.
