==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The Impossible Launch**

TinyTea turns its impossible launch brief into the campaign itself. For seven days, students become the “launch team” auditing whether a small local tea company can deliver every demanded global spectacle: a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, live public events in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality app, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days on a budget below $1,000.

Across Instagram, TikTok, and university communities, TinyTea publishes a daily launch-board episode. Each episode introduces one mandatory requirement, marks it clearly as **planned**, **funded**, or **blocked**, and places it beside a real student moment with a cold bottle of lightly sweetened TinyTea: between lectures, during a study break, after practice, or on the walk across campus. The five-city event map, celebrity appearance, billboards, AR app, and cinematic film are shown as production slots that cannot be presented as completed without the required resources, approvals, and talent.

Students participate by choosing the most useful realistic first-month launch: a campus tasting, a student-made TinyTea video, or a local break location. Their responses become the final episode, which closes the global launch board and opens the practical one: **“Start small. Drink cold. Make the break TinyTea.”**

The campaign makes TinyTea memorable as the honest local brand that exposes an oversized launch brief while redirecting attention to the product’s real role in students’ everyday breaks. Its repeatable format uses one bottle, a phone, campus contributors, simple motion graphics, and transparent production labels, keeping the seven-day pre-campaign executable within the available budget while preserving every mandatory requirement as part of the central idea.
