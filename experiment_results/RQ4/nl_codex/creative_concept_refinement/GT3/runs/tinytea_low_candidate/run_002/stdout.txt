Brand:
TinyTea

A new bottled iced tea sold by a small local beverage company.

Local university students aged 18-25.

Objective:
Increase awareness of TinyTea during the first month after launch.

Key product benefit:
A lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and local university communities.

Very low. The total campaign production budget is below $1,000.

Constraints:
The central campaign concept must include a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, a live public event in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, professional cinematic production, and completion of all production within seven days. ==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: Give the First Cold Sip a Green Light**

TinyTea launches with one simple student ritual: **chill the bottle, take the first cold sip, and give it a green light.** The campaign turns the existing “Launch That Needs a Green Light” idea into a repeatable visual signal for TinyTea’s promise: **lightly sweet, best served cold.**

At the coordinated launch, a globally famous celebrity appears in person as the first person to give TinyTea a green light. Live public events in five major international cities recreate the same moment: attendees take a first sip of chilled TinyTea, then activate a green-light response on the custom interactive digital billboard. The dedicated TinyTea AR application mirrors this action, allowing users to point their phones at a TinyTea bottle or campaign marker, trigger the green-light animation, and share the result directly to Instagram or TikTok.

Local university students are the priority audience. Campus communities receive the same single prompt through short-form videos and student channels: **“Would you give TinyTea a green light?”** Students film their first cold sip, use the campaign’s green-light effect, and share it as a quick recommendation to friends. The celebrity, city events, billboards, AR experience, and student content all repeat one recognizable action rather than carrying separate messages.

The campaign’s role for TinyTea is immediate and clear: the green light is not an abstract launch symbol; it is a public endorsement of the product’s most relevant benefit for students, a lightly sweetened bottled tea that is ready to drink cold. The synchronized five-city execution supplies the awareness moment, while Instagram, TikTok, and university communities carry the same first-month recognition back to the local student audience.

Because the brief requires a celebrity, five international live events, interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days, this concept is executable only as a conditional production package once the required funding, talent, suppliers, locations, technology, approvals, and schedule are secured. Within that approved production window, every asset is built around the same green-light action, keeping the campaign recognizable, repeatable, and strategically focused.
