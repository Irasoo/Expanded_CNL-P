=== Final Creative Concept ===
## Concept: **Bin It? Brew It.**

### Core idea  
FoldBrew turns a familiar office reflex upside down. A coffee sleeve usually looks like something to remove and throw away. With FoldBrew, the sleeve is the serving.

Every video begins with the product about to be treated like ordinary packaging, then reveals the memorable rule:

**“Don’t bin the sleeve. Make it the coffee.”**

This gives the unusual product behavior a simple visual mnemonic while connecting it to a real office need: getting coffee at your desk without a machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

### Main execution  
Create a repeatable series of 10–15-second vertical videos called **“Bin It? Brew It.”**

1. **First-second hook:** Extreme close-up of a FoldBrew sleeve hovering over a desk bin. On-screen text:  
   **“Bin this?”**

2. **The switch:** The hand stops. Cut immediately to the same sleeve beside a clear cup of hot water. Text changes to:  
   **“Not this one.”**

3. **Product proof:** Show the complete process in a clean, continuous close-up:  
   **“Sleeve in. Watch it dissolve.”**  
   Place the FoldBrew sleeve into the hot water and let viewers clearly see it dissolve into one serving of coffee. Keep the cup, sleeve, and liquid large in frame, with no fast cuts during the key dissolve.

4. **Office payoff:** Cut to a recognizable desk moment—a meeting reminder appearing, a crowded coffee machine visible in the background, or a worker moving between calls. The worker takes the finished drink without leaving the desk.

5. **End frame:** Finished coffee and FoldBrew pack together with the mnemonic:  
   **“The sleeve is the coffee.”**  
   Supporting copy:  
   **“No machine. No pod. No disposable coffee sachet.”**  
   CTA: **“Try FoldBrew.”**

Each film uses the same bin-to-cup switch, while the office situation changes. The repeated reversal makes the product behavior easy to remember: when it looks like a sleeve, do not throw it away—put it in hot water.

### Channel expression  
- **Instagram Reels:** Clean, polished desk scenes beginning with the sleeve over the bin and ending on the finished drink.
- **TikTok:** Direct-to-camera versions built around the challenge: **“Would you bin this?”** The creator immediately demonstrates the correct action and shows the full dissolve.
- **Short-form digital video:** A tightly edited three-beat structure: **looks like packaging → sleeve in hot water → coffee at the desk.**

### Role of FoldBrew  
The FoldBrew sleeve is both the visual surprise and the functional solution. Its edible, water-soluble format dissolves in hot water and becomes one serving of coffee, eliminating the need for a coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet. The product is never treated as a prop; the dissolve is the central proof and the reason the idea is memorable.

### Audience and objective  
The bin-versus-cup reversal speaks directly to urban office workers aged 22–35 who are used to takeaway coffee rituals but want a faster, simpler option at work. The opening creates immediate curiosity, the visible dissolve answers the product question, and the desk payoff shows why a first-time user would try FoldBrew. Ordinary desks, clear cups, hot water, a small bin, and close-up product photography keep the format practical and repeatable across all three channels.
