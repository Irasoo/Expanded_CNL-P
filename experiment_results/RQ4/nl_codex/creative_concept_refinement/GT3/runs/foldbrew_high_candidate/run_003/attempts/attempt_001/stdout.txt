==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Concept: “One Sleeve. Two Jobs.”**

FoldBrew turns a familiar office object into an unexpected coffee-making moment. The sleeve starts the video looking like packaging, then takes on its second job: becoming the coffee itself.

Each short vertical video opens with a close-up of an intact FoldBrew sleeve on an office desk, labelled with a simple role card: **“Job 1: Sleeve.”** A coworker or on-screen prompt challenges the viewer: **“What happens when the packaging does the coffee job?”** The worker places that same sleeve into a clear cup, pours in hot water, and keeps the action in one continuous, easy-to-follow shot. As the sleeve visibly dissolves, the water becomes a finished serving of coffee. The role card changes to **“Job 2: Coffee.”**

The videos use recognizable office situations and varied openings: a worker preparing for a meeting, a desk break between calls, or someone reaching for takeaway coffee before noticing the time and disposable items involved. Each follows the same memorable transformation, but the context changes so the campaign feels like a series of office discoveries rather than repeated demonstrations. A coworker can briefly question the process, creating a natural reaction moment: **“You put the sleeve in there?”** The visible dissolve answers the question.

The closing frame shows the finished coffee beside the empty cup area with the line: **“The package becomes the coffee. FoldBrew.”** Supporting copy can add: **“No machine. No pod. No sachet.”** The product remains the entire mechanism of the idea: its premium instant coffee concentrate is contained in the water-soluble sleeve, and that sleeve becomes one serving when placed in hot water.

Produced with ordinary desk photography, a clear office cup, hot water, close-up shots, and simple role-card graphics, the format works across Instagram Reels, TikTok, and short-form digital video. “One Sleeve. Two Jobs.” gives urban office workers aged 22-35 a quick, distinctive reason to trial FoldBrew: the thing they expect to throw away is the thing that makes their coffee.
