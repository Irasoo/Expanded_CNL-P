==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**FoldBrew: Put the Package In**

FoldBrew turns the most familiar coffee instruction on its head: instead of removing the packaging before making the drink, you put the package directly into the cup.

Every short-form execution opens on a close-up of a FoldBrew sleeve hovering over a clear mug of hot water. A hand begins to peel it back or move toward the bin, then stops. On-screen text lands immediately:

**“Don’t unwrap it.”**

The sleeve is placed into the mug. It visibly dissolves in real time, the water becomes coffee, and the finished serving is shown clearly before the worker takes a sip. The closing line is:

**“FoldBrew. Put the package in.”**

The principal 10- to 15-second video takes place during an ordinary office coffee moment. A worker reaches for the FoldBrew sleeve at their desk. The familiar instinct is to unwrap and discard it, but the video interrupts that action with the direct instruction. The sleeve goes into hot water, dissolves on camera, and becomes the coffee. The worker drinks from the mug and returns to work.

The recurring creative format is the **Put-It-In Test**: each film presents the same small moment of hesitation between throwing the sleeve away and placing it in the cup. The repeated reversal makes the unusual behavior easy to recognize and gives the campaign an ownable structure without adding production complexity. Variations can show a desk drawer coffee break, a rushed start before a meeting, or a worker returning from a takeaway run, but each follows the same visual rule: sleeve, hesitation, cup, dissolve, finished coffee.

Product photography uses the same distinctive setup: a FoldBrew sleeve positioned above a clear mug, caught at the moment it begins to disappear into the drink. Still posts and thumbnails carry concise copy such as **“This package goes in the cup”** and **“No unwrapping required.”** The sleeve, hot water, dissolve, and completed coffee remain visible so the image communicates the behavior without relying on explanation.

FoldBrew is the central action, not merely the subject of the message. Its sleeve is the coffee preparation itself, allowing urban office workers to make one serving at their desk without a coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet. The concept turns the product’s surprising use instruction into the campaign’s memorable idea: the package that normally gets removed is the thing that makes the coffee.

The campaign is designed for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and short-form digital video using ordinary office settings, clear drinkware, controlled close-ups, and repeatable product demonstrations. The direct call to action is included in captions and end frames:

**“Try FoldBrew. Put the package in.”**
