=== Final Creative Concept ===
## SunTrace: The Sun Kept Your Place

Young adults often leave campus or home for a short errand and end up staying outdoors for hours. SunTrace turns that familiar change of plans into a visual “bookmark” for sunscreen: the included UV-sensitive sticker marks where protection began, while the SPF 50 routine continues when the day runs longer.

### Main execution: “Still Out?”

Every TikTok, Reel, or university-community post follows the same simple vertical format and close-up framing:

1. **The plan changes.** A creator starts with a realistic message or caption such as: **“Quick walk to class”** or **“Still out three hours later…”**
2. **Before going outside,** the creator shows the actual SunTrace bottle, applies SPF 50 to one clearly framed area of the forearm, and places one included UV-sensitive sticker on that area. On-screen text says: **“SPF 50 before going out.”**
3. **The day continues.** A single text-card checkpoint appears over ordinary student footage: **“Still out?”** The creator reapplies SunTrace, keeping the behavior visible and making clear that sunscreen is not only a before-leaving step.
4. **The sun leaves a bookmark.** Later, in the same close-up framing, the creator removes the sticker. The surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker’s shape while the protected area remains visually unchanged. The caption reads: **“A visual reminder of sun exposure—not an exact UV-damage measurement.”**
5. **The behavior closes the story:** **“Before you go: apply SPF 50. Still out: reapply SunTrace.”**

The sticker is not presented as a score or scientific measurement. It is the memorable visual cue that shows where protection was established, while the repeated **“Still out?”** checkpoint connects the reveal to the audience’s real problem: forgetting to reapply when a short outing becomes a long one.

Each video uses one person, one SunTrace bottle, one reusable sticker, a forearm close-up, natural daylight, and ordinary campus or outdoor activity. The fixed framing and captions make the demonstration easier to reproduce and reduce confusion from changing camera angles or application areas. If the temporary sticker shape appears faint, the edit uses the same locked-off **before / after-sticker-removal** shots with the result clearly captioned, rather than adding new production elements.

SunTrace remains central because the visual bookmark begins with its SPF 50 application and ends with a practical reapplication reminder. The repeatable format gives young adults a quick, recognizable way to see the invisible routine behind sunscreen: **apply before going out, then reapply while still out.**
