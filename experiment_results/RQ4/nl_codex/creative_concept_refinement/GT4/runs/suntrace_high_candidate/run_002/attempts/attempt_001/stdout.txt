Brand:
SunTrace

A facial sunscreen that is transparent when applied normally. The package includes reusable UV-sensitive stickers. ==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Past You Left a Bookmark**

SunTrace turns the included UV-sensitive sticker into a physical bookmark between two outdoor moments: applying sunscreen and remembering to reapply.

Each short video opens on a close-up of a young adult removing the sticker and revealing its temporary outline. They look at the camera and say:

**“Past me bookmarked this.”**

A match cut shows the earlier moment: the same person applying SunTrace SPF 50, placing the reusable sticker on their skin, and heading into an ordinary campus or neighborhood activity. The video cuts back to the present. They recognize that they are still spending time outdoors, pick up the SunTrace bottle, and reapply before returning to the activity.

The sticker is the visual handoff from “I applied” to “I need to remember protection again.” It does not measure UV damage or determine when protection expires; it simply makes the invisible idea of sun exposure memorable enough to prompt a real action.

The central line is:

**“Past you bookmarked the sun. Present you reapplies.”**

A concise on-screen caption accompanies the reveal:

**“A visual reminder during continued outdoor time. Not a measurement of UV damage.”**

The format is a repeatable 15-second TikTok, Instagram Reel, or university social video. Every execution begins with the unexpected “bookmark” reveal, match-cuts to the earlier application, shows one recognizable young-adult outdoor situation, and ends with a clear SunTrace SPF 50 reapplication. Situations can vary between crossing campus, sitting outside, walking to class, or playing sport, while the locked camera angle and sticker reveal make the series recognizable.

SunTrace remains central at both behavior moments: it provides SPF 50 protection, and its included reusable sticker creates the memorable visual link that helps young adults remember to reapply while they continue being outdoors.
