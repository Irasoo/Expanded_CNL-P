==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**SunTrace: Don’t Leave Your SunTab Open**

Young adults remember sunscreen when they leave home, then forget to reapply once the day gets busy. SunTrace turns the included UV-sensitive sticker into a visible checkpoint for the moments when an outdoor activity continues.

A TikTok or Reel opens with a fast campus scenario: a student applies SunTrace SPF 50 before heading outside. On-screen text immediately says:

**“You remembered sunscreen. You forgot the next coat.”**

They place a reusable SunTrace sticker on the protected area, and a small caption appears:

**“SunTab opened.”**

The person moves through an ordinary outdoor activity: playing sport, walking between classes, sitting on the lawn, or swimming. At a natural interruption, such as a water break, halftime, changing clothes, or stopping for lunch, they remove the sticker. The protected area remains unchanged while the surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker shape.

The reveal is treated as a visual reminder that the person has been in the sun, not as a measurement of UV exposure, damage, or exact protection. The person immediately reapplies SunTrace before returning outdoors, then places the reusable sticker again to open the next SunTab.

The repeatable video structure is:

**Open the SunTab. Outdoor activity. Checkpoint reveal. Reapply. Continue.**

The end frame reads:

**“Don’t leave your SunTab open.”**  
**“Reapply SunTrace SPF 50 when your outdoor activity continues.”**

The sticker gives SunTrace’s invisible protection a memorable role in the user’s real routine: SPF 50 provides the protection, while the temporary reveal creates a physical reminder at a moment when reapplication can be forgotten. The checkpoint format is designed for students and young adults because it fits existing activity breaks rather than requiring a special location, device, or production setup.

Each creator can film the same short sequence in a campus setting with one participant, natural light, the real sunscreen, and the included reusable sticker. Different activities provide fresh content, while the recognizable **open, check, reapply, continue** pattern builds recall across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social media communities.
