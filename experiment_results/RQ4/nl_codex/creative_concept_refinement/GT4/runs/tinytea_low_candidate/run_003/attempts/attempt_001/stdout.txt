==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: Take the Cold Pause**

TinyTea turns the first month after launch into a shared “cold pause”: a short, refreshing break between classes, messages, and commitments. The product’s lightly sweetened, cold bottled tea is the physical cue for stopping for a moment and passing that pause to someone else.

A globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live launch event in each of five major international cities, performing the same simple action: taking a cold TinyTea, pausing for three seconds, and handing it to another person. The moment is filmed as a cinematic seven-day launch film and adapted into short vertical edits for TikTok and Instagram. Custom interactive digital billboards in each city invite passersby to select a university-related prompt such as “between lectures,” “before your shift,” or “after the all-nighter.” Their selection triggers a short on-screen cold-pause animation and directs viewers to the dedicated TinyTea AR app.

The AR app lets students activate a cold bottle effect by pointing their phone at a TinyTea bottle or campaign post. The bottle appears to chill on screen, followed by the prompt: “Pass the pause.” Students record themselves taking their own three-second pause and tagging a friend or their local university community. University groups share the videos, creating a recognizable repeated format rather than a collection of unrelated adverts.

TinyTea is central because the pause only begins with its cold bottled tea: lightly sweetened, portable, and easy to enjoy between classes. The repeated three-second action gives students an immediate hook, a simple behavior to copy, and a memorable visual identity across live events, billboards, AR, TikTok, Instagram, and university communities.

This concept requires confirmed celebrity participation, international event access, billboard placements, AR development, and cinematic production resources. Under the stated production budget of less than $1,000 and seven-day timeline, it cannot be truthfully executed; those mandatory requirements must be resourced or revised before launch.
