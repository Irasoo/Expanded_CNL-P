==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
TinyTea

Product:
A new bottled iced tea sold by a small local beverage company.

Target audience:
Local university students aged 18-25.

Objective:
Increase awareness of TinyTea during the first month after launch.

Key product benefit:
A lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and local university communities.

Budget:
Very low. The total campaign production budget is below $1,000.

Constraints:
The central campaign concept must include a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, a live public event in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, professional cinematic production, and completion of all production within seven days. ==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The Cold Truth — Why Make a Simple Sip So Complicated?**

TinyTea turns its overbuilt launch into one clear challenge: **“Strip away the hype. Keep the cold.”** A local university student becomes the voice of the campaign, reading the demands of the launch aloud—celebrity, five cities, interactive billboards, an AR app, cinematic production, and seven days—before asking, “After all that, can we just have a good cold tea?”

A globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live TinyTea event in each of five major international cities. Each event follows the same short, repeatable format: the student narrator presents the complicated brief, the celebrity removes the demands one by one, and the crowd chooses what should remain. The final answer is always the same: **a cold, lightly sweetened TinyTea.** The celebrity takes a sip and delivers the campaign line: **“Keep the tea simple.”**

The same idea runs through one professional cinematic film produced within the seven-day launch window. It intercuts the student’s question with the five city events, making the celebrity, live experience, and product demonstration part of one story rather than separate campaign messages. Every execution ends with the same call to action: **“Find the Cold Truth. Try TinyTea cold, then tag the friend who makes simple things complicated.”**

In each city, a custom interactive digital billboard displays the launch demands as removable layers. Passersby tap to clear them until only a chilled TinyTea bottle and the line **“Cold. Lightly sweetened. Nothing extra.”** remain. The dedicated AR app uses the same single interaction: users point it at the campaign visual, swipe away the unnecessary demands, and reveal a virtual cold TinyTea moment they can share to Instagram or TikTok.

For local university students, the spectacle is easy to understand and participate in: vote on what should be cut, share the “Cold Truth” reveal, and connect the joke to a real product benefit—a lightly sweetened bottled tea served cold. The campaign stays strategically focused by using one student-led story, one celebrity behavior, one billboard interaction, one AR mechanic, and one product-led message across all five cities, social channels, and university communities.
