==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Concept: “The Coffee Supply That Disappears”**

**Core Idea**

At work, FoldBrew is the entire coffee setup: one sleeve goes in, and the sleeve becomes the coffee.

The campaign uses a repeatable “desk supply check” format. Each film begins with a clean overhead view of an ordinary office desk: an empty mug or glass, hot water, and exactly one FoldBrew sleeve. The sleeve is the only coffee item needed. By the end, the sleeve has disappeared into the finished drink.

The visual rule is consistent and memorable:

**One sleeve in. One coffee out.**

**Main Execution**

Create short-form videos in a fixed overhead or close-up desk view, using one continuous take wherever possible.

The film opens on the FoldBrew sleeve beside a clear heat-safe vessel of hot water. A simple on-screen label reads:

**“Desk coffee supply: 1 FoldBrew.”**

The person places the sleeve into the hot water. The camera remains locked on the vessel while the actual sleeve visibly dissolves. There are no cutaways during the transformation. As the sleeve disappears and the liquid becomes coffee, the label changes to:

**“Supply used.”**

The finished drink is lifted back to the same desk. The final frame shows the empty space where the sleeve was, beside the completed coffee:

**“One sleeve in. One coffee out.”**  
**“FoldBrew.”**

The first seconds show the actual sleeve and hot water immediately, so the film cannot be mistaken for a conventional takeaway coffee demonstration. The dissolve is the central event, not an effect added around it.

A secondary edit can use a side-by-side before-and-after composition: the intact sleeve on one side, the finished coffee on the other, with the dissolve shown between them. Still posts can use the same visual system in three factual stages:

1. **The only coffee supply**
2. **The sleeve dissolving**
3. **The finished coffee**

**Product and Brand Role**

FoldBrew is both the coffee and the preparation. The sleeve is not presented as packaging that must be removed or thrown away; it is the ingredient that disappears into one serving of coffee.

The demonstration proves the product’s defining benefit in real time: users do not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet. The brand is identified at the start, during the dissolve, and in the final frame so the unusual behavior is linked directly to FoldBrew.

**Audience Connection**

The setting is the familiar office moment of needing coffee at a desk without setting up a full coffee station. The “desk supply check” makes the benefit concrete for urban office workers who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster option while working.

Its visual surprise comes from the object disappearing from the desk and reappearing as the drink. That gives first-time viewers a clear reason to watch through the dissolve and gives them a simple behavior to remember:

**Put the FoldBrew sleeve in hot water. Watch it disappear. Drink the coffee.**

**Practical Production**

The campaign requires only an ordinary desk, one clear vessel, hot water, FoldBrew sleeves, a phone or close-up camera, and simple office-worker casting. A fixed camera and continuous take make the demonstration easy to repeat across multiple videos while keeping the product transformation credible.

The strongest version prioritizes the real dissolve and uses minimal copy. There are no special effects, celebrities, installations, unsupported environmental claims, or extra preparation mechanics. Each execution follows the same efficient structure:

**Show the one-item supply. Dissolve the sleeve. Reveal the coffee.**
