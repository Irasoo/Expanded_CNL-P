==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Concept: “Watch the Sleeve Disappear”**

**Core Idea**  
FoldBrew turns the most important part of the product demonstration into the story: the coffee package disappears into the drink. Each execution begins with a familiar office coffee problem, then focuses attention on the unexpected solution: place the edible, water-soluble coffee sleeve into hot water and watch it dissolve into one serving of coffee.

The central line is:

**“No pod. No machine. No sachet. Just the sleeve.”**

This communicates the product benefit using only the capabilities stated in the brief.

**Principal Execution: Short-Form Transformation Films**  
Create a series of concise Instagram Reels, TikToks, and short-form digital videos built around one repeatable visual sequence:

1. An office worker places an empty mug on a desk.
2. The FoldBrew sleeve is shown clearly in close-up.
3. On-screen text identifies the ordinary coffee setup being skipped: **“No coffee machine.”**
4. The sleeve is placed into hot water.
5. A tight macro shot visibly captures the sleeve dissolving.
6. The water becomes one serving of coffee.
7. The finished drink is shown beside the now-absent package.
8. End frame: **“The package becomes the coffee. Try FoldBrew.”**

The dissolving moment is the visual signature of the campaign. It should be shown in real time or lightly slowed down, with the camera close enough for viewers to see the sleeve breaking down in the hot water. The transformation must remain the focus rather than being hidden behind a cut or explained only through text.

A typical 10- to 15-second film could use this structure:

- **0–2 seconds:** “Your coffee package usually gets thrown away.”
- **2–5 seconds:** FoldBrew sleeve enters the mug.
- **5–10 seconds:** The sleeve visibly dissolves as the coffee forms.
- **10–15 seconds:** “The package becomes the coffee. FoldBrew.”

The opening line refers only to the disposable packaging behavior addressed by the brief. The execution should avoid broader environmental claims or unsupported waste-reduction statistics.

**Brand and Product Role**  
FoldBrew is both the coffee and the demonstration. The package is not merely shown alongside the product; its unusual behavior is the product experience. The films therefore make the key benefit immediately understandable without requiring technical explanation:

- FoldBrew does not require a coffee machine.
- It does not require a coffee pod.
- It does not require a disposable coffee sachet.
- The sleeve itself dissolves into one serving of coffee.

The product should appear clearly before, during, and after the transformation so that viewers can connect the package with the finished drink.

**Audience Relevance**  
The setting is an ordinary urban office desk, reflecting the target audience of office workers aged 22–35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee during the workday. The format presents FoldBrew at the moment they need it: when they want a faster, lower-waste option at work without relying on a coffee machine, pod, or sachet.

The content should feel quick and easy to understand while scrolling. The satisfying visual of the sleeve disappearing gives the audience a memorable reason to stop, watch, and understand how the product works.

**Channel Adaptation**

- **Instagram:** Use polished 10- to 15-second Reels with clean desk compositions, close-up product photography, and a prominent final product shot.
- **TikTok:** Use faster, more direct versions beginning with the transformation or the question, **“What if the coffee package became the coffee?”**
- **Short-form digital video:** Use the clearest demonstration-led cut, with readable captions and the product benefit stated in the final seconds.

Still photography can support the videos with a simple three-image sequence: FoldBrew sleeve beside an empty mug, sleeve dissolving in hot water, and finished coffee with no package remaining. This keeps the campaign within ordinary product photography and short-form video production while making the unusual behavior easy to recognize.

**How It Serves the Objective**  
“Watch the Sleeve Disappear” drives first-time trial by showing exactly what a new user needs to understand: the package is placed in hot water and becomes the coffee. Repeating that physical transformation across every channel makes the behavior distinctive and memorable, while the office setting connects the demonstration to the audience’s regular takeaway-coffee routine.
