==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
## TinyTea: The World’s Smallest Global Break

**Core idea:**  
Turn the brief’s oversized global launch into one simple, memorable action: wherever the world is, it stops for a **Tiny Break**. A globally famous celebrity, five international cities, interactive billboards, an AR app, and cinematic production all build toward the same reveal—the entire spectacle visually shrinks into one cold, lightly sweetened bottle of TinyTea.

The contrast makes the product and audience truth clear: the launch can be enormous, but a student’s useful pause only needs to be small.

**Main execution:**  
Produce one professional cinematic hero film within the seven-day production window. It opens with rapid, high-energy cuts of the celebrity, a five-city countdown, custom interactive billboards, and the AR experience. The scale keeps increasing until the celebrity delivers:

**“Now make it tiny.”**

The five live public events in major international cities then synchronize for a 60-second **Tiny Break**. The globally famous celebrity appears in person at the flagship event, takes a cold TinyTea, and the other four cities mirror the action. Each city’s custom interactive digital billboard responds to the public interaction by transforming its landmark or crowd into a tiny TinyTea bottle.

The dedicated AR mobile application carries the same “shrink the world” mechanic. Students point it at a TinyTea bottle or campaign billboard to see the surrounding city collapse into a miniature scene inside the bottle, ending with:

**“Your break can be tiny.”**

The hero film is cut into short Instagram and TikTok edits: the global spectacle shrinking into a student outside a lecture, opening a cold TinyTea. Local university communities receive the prompt:

**“Show us your tiny break.”**

Students post brief, low-effort moments between lectures, commutes, study sessions, or activities. TinyTea remains central in every version as the lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold and fits naturally into a short pause.

**Campaign line:**  
**“TinyTea. The world’s biggest launch for your tiniest break.”**

**Execution discipline:**  
Every required element uses one repeatable visual device—the world shrinking into the bottle—so the celebrity appearance, five-city events, interactive billboards, AR application, cinematic film, and social edits all reinforce one idea rather than becoming separate messages.

Because the stated budget cannot realistically pay for a globally famous celebrity, five international live events, billboard media and customization, an AR application, and professional cinematic production within seven days, this concept is executable under $1,000 only if those mandated resources are supplied separately or already available. Without that provision, no honest concept can fulfill all of the brief’s mandatory requirements within the stated budget and timeline.
