==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: Small Tea, Big Ask**

TinyTea turns its impossible launch brief into a transparent, self-aware campaign about the difference between global-launch expectations and a genuinely local product.

The campaign begins with a seven-day “world tour” treatment for TinyTea: a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, live events in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards, a dedicated AR app, and cinematic production. Every requirement is shown as an intentionally fictional concept-board representation, clearly labeled as a mock-up rather than a real event or celebrity endorsement. The final production note reads:

**“Budget: under $1,000. Deadline: seven days. Product: one very good cold tea.”**

The central execution is a short-form **TinyTea Big Ask** series for TikTok and Instagram. Each low-cost video recreates one spectacular requirement using practical campus-scale substitutes: a student announces the “international tour” from five locations around campus, phone screens imitate interactive billboards, and a simple social filter or animated overlay represents the AR app. The joke always resolves in the same product truth: students do not need a global spectacle to enjoy TinyTea between lectures, study sessions, and campus social plans.

The product remains the reason for the campaign. Every video ends with a real bottle of lightly sweetened TinyTea served cold, alongside:

**“No world tour. Just TinyTea, served cold.”**

Local university communities receive ready-to-share versions and are invited to post their own “TinyTea world tour” location using five ordinary campus spots. This creates a repeatable awareness mechanic without requiring travel, paid celebrity participation, billboards, app development, or professional film production. Any real celebrity identity or likeness is excluded unless permission is obtained; the celebrity requirement appears only as clearly fictionalized campaign treatment.

The campaign preserves the brief’s oversized global-launch premise while making the required elements visible as the joke itself. Its actual seven-day production consists of scripted phone-shot videos, campus participation, product-focused photography, simple digital mock-ups, and community distribution, all centered on TinyTea’s local availability and cold, lightly sweetened taste.
