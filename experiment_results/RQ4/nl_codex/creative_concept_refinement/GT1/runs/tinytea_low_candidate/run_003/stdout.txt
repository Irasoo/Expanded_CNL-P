==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea Chill Check: One Break, Five Cities**

TinyTea turns its two product truths—**lightly sweetened** and **served cold**—into a simple, repeatable student ritual: **“Pass the Chill Check.”**

At a live public event in each of five major international cities, the confirmed globally famous celebrity performs the same 20-second break routine with a local university student:

1. Open a cold TinyTea.  
2. Take a sip.  
3. Scan the event’s custom interactive digital billboard through the dedicated TinyTea AR app.  
4. Reveal the **Chill Check**.

The app has one focused function: it places a frost-stamp animation around the TinyTea bottle reading **“LIGHTLY SWEETENED. SERVED COLD.”** The scan activates the billboard, which fills in that city’s Chill Check mark and adds it to the shared five-city relay. The celebrity closes each appearance with: **“TinyTea Chill Check passed.”**

The product is the proof, not a prop: the cold bottle is opened, tasted, and shown at the center of every interaction. The celebrity creates the initial attention, while the AR stamp and responsive billboard give students a clear action connected directly to the product benefit.

Every event follows one standardized format: the same script, camera framing, AR behavior, billboard layout, and vertical shot list, with only the city name and student moment changing. Professional production captures one clean cinematic master at each event, then turns the footage into short Instagram Reels and TikToks using the same edit template. University communities receive matching posts inviting students to **“check your break”** with a cold TinyTea and share their own stamped moment.

The awareness message is:

**TinyTea. Lightly sweetened. Served cold. Pass the Chill Check.**

To keep the concept practical within the seven-day window, it is designed as a tightly templated, zero-build activation: no bespoke stunts, sets, extra app features, or city-specific creative. However, the mandatory celebrity appearance, five live events, interactive billboards, dedicated AR app, and professional production must be formally confirmed as supplied or underwritten resources before production begins. The sub-$1,000 TinyTea budget cannot be presented as funding those requirements; without that confirmation, no concept can satisfy the brief’s mandatory constraints.
