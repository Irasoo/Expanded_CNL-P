TinyTea

Increase awareness of TinyTea during the first month after launch.

Key product benefit:
A lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and local university communities.

Budget:
Very low. The total campaign production budget is below $1,000.

Constraints:
The central campaign concept must include a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, a live public event in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, professional cinematic production, and completion of all production within seven days. ==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The Launch Requirements Reality Check**

TinyTea stages the impossible launch brief as the story. A student receives a seven-day assignment to launch a small local bottled tea with a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, live events in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards, a dedicated AR app, and professional cinematic production, all for a total budget below $1,000. Instead of pretending these elements can be delivered, the campaign makes the contradiction its central message: **“TinyTea is local. The brief is global.”**

The main execution is a professionally produced cinematic short film built around the student’s checklist. Each required element appears as an on-screen production demand, followed by a practical status stamp: “needs celebrity access,” “needs five-city event production,” “needs billboard installation,” “needs AR development,” or “cannot be completed in seven days at this budget.” The film ends with the student setting the checklist aside, opening a cold bottle of lightly sweetened TinyTea, and taking a real campus break. The closing line is: **“TinyTea. Small launch. Real refreshment.”**

The film is adapted into short Instagram and TikTok edits, with the full checklist shared through local university communities. Students are invited to comment with the most unrealistic requirement in their own study day, then show the realistic TinyTea break that replaces it. Every version keeps the bottle visible and makes the cold, lightly sweetened tea the tangible payoff.

The concept serves awareness by turning the conflict between TinyTea’s local student relevance and the brief’s global demands into a memorable, shareable story. It is executable as a low-cost feasibility and approval piece using one student setting, one bottle, simple graphic overlays, and campus-focused social distribution. It does not falsely claim that the mandatory celebrity appearance, five-city live events, interactive billboards, AR application, cinematic production, and seven-day delivery can all be completed within the stated budget. Those requirements remain a formal production gate: unless the brief is revised or additional resources are provided, TinyTea should release this reality-check concept as the campaign recommendation and not authorize production of the full mandatory activation.

=== Evaluation ===

Executability: 3/10
Overall score: 4.80/10
Weak criteria: strategic_fit, executability
Reasons: The concept has a distinctive, product-relevant premise: it turns the contradiction between a small local tea launch and an impossible global production brief into the story, with a memorable local-versus-global line. The student break, visible cold bottle, and lightly sweetened tea provide a clear and credible product payoff, while the short-form adaptations and comment prompt could engage university audiences. However, Strategic Fit is low because the brief makes the celebrity appearance, five-city events, interactive billboards, AR app, professional cinematic production, seven-day timeline, and sub-$1,000 budget mandatory; the concept explicitly declines to deliver them and recommends a feasibility piece instead. Executability is therefore also low for the required campaign, even though the proposed reality-check film and social edits would be relatively simple to produce. Audience impact is moderate rather than exceptional because the contradiction is understandable and potentially shareable, but the idea may feel like an internal production critique rather than an exciting product launch.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the reality-check framing, student relatability, visible bottle payoff, and local university distribution as a strong feasibility or approval asset. To make it a compliant campaign, first secure a formally revised brief or the additional budget, access, production time, and technical resources needed for every mandatory element; then connect each requirement to the same central narrative rather than presenting it as impossible. Build a single production map showing how the celebrity, five-city events, billboards, AR experience, cinematic film, and seven-day schedule each drive awareness and a cold TinyTea trial. If the constraints cannot change, label this clearly as a recommendation to revise the brief, not as the launch campaign, and strengthen the audience hook with a more explicit student participation mechanic and a direct local purchase or sampling action.
