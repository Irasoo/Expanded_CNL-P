==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Concept: “TinyTea: The Smallest Global Launch”**

TinyTea stages one deliberately oversized idea around the smallest useful pause in a student’s day: a cold, lightly sweetened tea between classes, study sessions, and social plans. The campaign’s central image is a globally famous celebrity taking a “tiny break” with TinyTea while the world treats that moment like an international premiere.

Within the seven-day production window, the celebrity appears in person at five live public events in five major international cities. Every event follows the same short, repeatable format: a countdown on the city’s custom interactive digital billboard, the celebrity opening and drinking a chilled TinyTea, and a local university student joining the celebrity for a one-minute break. The events are intentionally simple and identical, so the concept remains recognizable across cities and easy for university communities to share.

The custom billboards show a giant countdown shrinking into a tiny animated bottle. When people interact with the billboard, it prompts them to take and share their own “tiny break” with TinyTea. The dedicated AR mobile application uses one straightforward experience: scanning TinyTea packaging or campaign content places a miniature version of the five-city launch into the user’s surroundings, ending with a prompt to record a short Instagram or TikTok video of their own cold-tea pause. The same visual language, script, AR interaction, billboard treatment, and event setup are reused in every location. Professional cinematic production captures the celebrity’s five appearances, the student moments, the cold bottle, and the shared countdown in one tightly planned production package.

TinyTea is the reason the spectacle happens and the visible payoff in every piece of content. The celebrity demonstrates the ritual, but students own it: each local university community supplies the relatable “one-minute reset” moments that turn an international event into a familiar campus behavior. The campaign therefore uses the absurd contrast of a global launch for a tiny local tea brand to create awareness, while keeping the product benefit clear: TinyTea is a simple, cold, lightly sweetened drink for a small break that fits into a busy student day.

Because the brief makes the celebrity, five-city events, interactive billboards, AR application, cinematic production, seven-day schedule, and sub-$1,000 budget mandatory, this concept is executable only if all required access, rights, production capability, venues, billboard placements, application development, and celebrity participation are secured in advance or provided at no additional campaign cost. The execution is deliberately reduced to one repeatable event format, one AR behavior, one billboard system, and one production script, but those mandatory resources remain prerequisites rather than costs the concept can credibly absorb within the stated budget.

Weak criteria: executability
Reasons: The central contrast between a tiny local tea brand and an oversized international launch is distinctive, memorable, and meaningfully tied to the product's small, cold refreshment ritual. The repeated one-minute break gives the idea a clear student behavior and makes TinyTea visible in every event, billboard, AR interaction, and social post. The concept should attract attention through absurd scale, celebrity participation, countdowns, and shareable student moments. However, the strategic fit is weakened by the mismatch between a small local beverage company, a very low budget, and a campaign architecture requiring five international events, a globally famous celebrity, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR app, and professional cinematic production. The concept technically includes every mandatory requirement, but it does not provide a credible way to satisfy them. Executability is therefore extremely weak: the seven-day schedule and sub-$1,000 budget are not realistically compatible with celebrity contracting, international travel and event logistics, billboard development and placement, app creation, rights management, and cinematic production unless all of those resources are already secured or provided at no additional cost.
Refinement suggestions: Preserve the strong 'smallest global launch' contrast and the simple one-minute student break, but turn feasibility into an explicit go/no-go plan. Before creative production begins, require documented confirmation of the celebrity's in-person availability, rights, five venues, billboard inventory and interactivity, AR development capability, international logistics, and production support; do not treat these as assumptions. Build one master production package with a locked script, modular event kit, reusable billboard assets, and a minimal AR experience so the seven-day window is technically manageable if the mandatory resources are already available. Make the local student participation and TinyTea product demonstration the hero of every asset rather than relying only on celebrity spectacle. If any mandatory resource is not already secured or contributed outside the stated budget, flag the concept as non-viable rather than presenting it as executable, since those constraints cannot be solved through simplification alone.
