=== Final Creative Concept ===
## TinyTea: The Cold Reality Check

**Core idea**

TinyTea turns an everyday student reset into a recognizable social ritual: open the fridge, grab a cold TinyTea, take the first sip, and give a one-word “reality check” on how the day is going. Every post ends with: **“Keep it cold. Keep it light. TinyTea.”**

**Main execution**

The campaign cannot be commissioned under the current brief. A globally famous celebrity appearing in person, live events in five major international cities, custom interactive billboards, a dedicated augmented-reality application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days cannot credibly be delivered for a total production budget below $1,000. No celebrity, event, billboard, application, partner, or production resource will be announced or implied unless it has been secured. Formal approval to revise either the requirements or the available resources is the required first step.

Once the global requirements are removed through an approved brief revision, the first-month campaign uses one repeatable phone-shot format across TikTok and Instagram. Each video opens with a student stating their one-word reality check, cuts immediately to a visibly cold TinyTea bottle coming out of a refrigerator or cooler, and captures the first sip. The closing line and on-screen tag are **“Keep it cold. Keep it light. TinyTea.”** and **#TinyTeaRealityCheck**.

TinyTea supplies a small number of cold bottles for simple sampling through participating local university communities. Students and community accounts reproduce the same short format using their own phones, while the brand reposts the clearest reactions. Production remains limited to bottles, cold storage, sampling materials, and basic phone-shot editing so the format is practical within the stated budget and repeatable throughout launch month.

**Product and brand role**

The cold TinyTea bottle triggers the reality-check ritual. Its lightly sweetened taste supports the “keep it light” message, while serving it cold provides the consistent visual cue and first-sip payoff in every post.

**Audience and objective**

The one-word check-in reflects the changing moods, schedules, and pressures of local university life without requiring polished performances. Its short, consistent structure is designed for student participation, community reposting, and repeated recognition on Instagram and TikTok, building local awareness during TinyTea’s first month after launch.
