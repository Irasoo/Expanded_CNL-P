=== Final Creative Concept ===
TinyTea “The Cold Sip Signal” is a tightly controlled seven-day launch built around one repeatable student action: take a cold sip of TinyTea, make the campaign’s simple hand signal, and pass the signal to the next university community. The signal is the same in every channel, so the product benefit, the celebrity appearance, the live events, the billboards, the AR application, and the social content all become one recognizable awareness cue rather than separate campaign elements.

During the first month after launch, a globally famous celebrity appears in person at one live public event in each of five major international cities. Each event uses the same short sequence and standardized materials: the celebrity visibly takes a cold sip of lightly sweetened TinyTea, makes the Cold Sip Signal, and invites local university students to repeat it on camera. The events are run as compact public appearances with one core script, one bottle presentation, and one student participation prompt, limiting the number of moving parts that must be prepared in each city.

The five custom interactive digital billboards use a single approved design adapted by city. A student selects a university or city, and the billboard responds with the same Cold Sip Signal animation and the next short prompt: “Show your cold sip.” The dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application contains one narrowly scoped effect only. Scanning a TinyTea bottle places the Cold Sip Signal beside the user and records a short vertical clip using the same visual cue seen at the events and on the billboards. No additional app features, locations, or interactions are required.

Professional cinematic production captures the celebrity’s five event appearances and the student Cold Sip Signals. The production uses one master shot list, one script, one bottle setup, one visual identity, and reusable footage templates for all social edits, billboard content, and AR assets. The same standardized execution package is prepared for all five cities, while each city’s event, billboard version, and university prompt are limited to the mandatory local adaptation. Production is treated as a single controlled seven-day build: all mandatory access, permissions, celebrity availability, billboard placements, AR development, event operations, and production costs must be confirmed before the seven-day window begins; the concept does not assume any unconfirmed partner, technology, or resource.

For local university students, participation is immediate and easy to film: they chill or pick up a TinyTea, take the defining cold sip, make the signal, and identify their university or city. Every encounter keeps the bottle visible and demonstrates the product’s lightly sweetened, cold-serving benefit. The famous face and five-city scale create the opening attention, but the memorable takeaway is specific to TinyTea: wherever the student sees the Cold Sip Signal, TinyTea is the bottle that starts the next cold moment.
