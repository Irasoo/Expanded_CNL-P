=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The Smallest Global Launch**

TinyTea stages the world’s most overbuilt launch in the smallest possible format. In seven days, one tightly scripted cinematic film follows a student producer trying to launch a modest bottled iced tea with a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, five simultaneous live public events in major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, and a dedicated AR mobile app. Every requirement happens literally, but in TinyTea’s language: each event lasts one minute, each billboard is a compact interactive display, the AR experience turns a TinyTea bottle into a miniature global stage, and the production uses one repeatable visual system across every location.

The campaign opens with the celebrity appearing in person beside a TinyTea bottle and asking, “For a lightly sweetened iced tea?” The film cuts rapidly between the five cities, where student-led public events challenge passersby to find the “tiny launch” on the billboards and scan the bottle through the AR app. The billboards show a TinyTea bottle traveling between the five cities; touching or approaching the display triggers the bottle to open and reveal the message: “Big launch. Small tea.” The AR app recreates the same journey on a phone, placing a miniature TinyTea launch in the user’s surroundings and ending with a prompt to locate or share a real bottle on campus.

The celebrity’s role is intentionally brief and memorable: they deliver the launch line, take a sip, and conclude, “Keep the spectacle. Make the tea simple.” Professional cinematic production gives the seven-day campaign one polished hero film, while the same shot list, script, bottle animation, billboard interaction, and AR asset are reused across all five cities, social edits, and university-community posts. Local student crews operate the short events using the shared format, keeping the execution consistent and repeatable.

Instagram and TikTok edits turn each requirement into a hook: “Five cities. One celebrity. One tiny bottle.” Students use the AR experience, visit the live launch moment, and post the smallest version of a huge product launch with TinyTea visible in frame. The contrast remains the central joke, but TinyTea is never incidental: its cold, lightly sweetened bottled tea is the simple payoff beneath the extravagant global spectacle, giving local students a memorable reason to notice, try, and look for the new drink during launch month.
