==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
SunTrace

Product:
A facial sunscreen that is transparent when applied normally. The package includes reusable UV-sensitive stickers. When a sticker is placed on the skin before sun exposure and removed later, the protected area remains unchanged while the surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker shape.

Target audience:
Young adults aged 18-30 who understand that sunscreen is important but frequently forget to reapply it during outdoor activities.

Objective:
Make the invisible protective effect of sunscreen visually understandable and motivate users to remember sunscreen application and reapplication.

Key product benefit:
The sunscreen provides SPF 50 protection, while the included UV-sensitive demonstration stickers make the effect of sun exposure easier to visualize.

Channels:
TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social media communities.

Budget:
Low to medium.

Constraints:
Use only the real sunscreen and included demonstration stickers. Do not claim that the temporary visual demonstration measures exact UV damage. Do not use celebrities or expensive locations. The idea must be understandable within a short video.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**SunTrace: The Sun Bookmark**

The video opens on the payoff: an extreme close-up of a student beginning to peel away a SunTrace UV-sensitive sticker. As the temporary shape starts to appear, the action freezes and rewinds. On-screen text asks: **“How many outdoor chapters have you had today?”**

Back at the start of the day, the student applies transparent SunTrace SPF 50, places the included reusable sticker on their cheek, and heads across campus. Each time their day shifts from indoors to outdoors, the same quick visual cue appears: a freeze-frame labeled like a chapter marker, followed by **“New outdoor chapter? Sunscreen check.”** Before walking to class, leaving lunch, and joining friends outside, the student takes out SunTrace and reapplies rather than waiting to notice the package or remember spontaneously.

Fast, recognizable campus transitions keep the story compact: **Chapter 1: Walk. Chapter 2: Lunch. Chapter 3: Hangout.** The repeated chapter marker turns a common moment, stepping outside again, into a practical reapplication cue young adults can use throughout an active day.

The video returns to the opening close-up. The student fully removes the sticker, revealing the temporary contrast between the unchanged covered area and the surrounding area exposed during the demonstration. On-screen text clarifies: **“A visible illustration of sun exposure, not a UV-damage measurement or SPF test.”** The student returns the reusable sticker to the SunTrace package.

The end card reads: **“SunTrace SPF 50. New outdoor chapter? Reapply.”**

Each vertical video uses the same repeatable production setup: one student, one ordinary campus route, consistent sticker placement, a fixed close-up angle for the opening and final reveal, and three quick indoor-to-outdoor transitions. Different students can create their own “chapters” around walks, lunch breaks, sports, or outdoor social plans, making the format native to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university communities while keeping SunTrace and its included demonstration sticker central to both the visual hook and the reapplication habit.
