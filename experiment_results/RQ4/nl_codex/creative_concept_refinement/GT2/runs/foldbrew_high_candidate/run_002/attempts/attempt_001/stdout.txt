==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Cancel the Coffee Run: The Reverse Unboxing**

FoldBrew turns the familiar takeaway-coffee unboxing into its opposite: instead of opening a package to reveal a drink, the viewer watches the package disappear and become the drink.

Each short-form video opens like a routine office coffee order. A worker reaches for their coat, passes a takeaway cup across the desk, or starts opening a coffee sachet. A bold physical **CANCELLED** stamp interrupts each expected step. The worker stays at their desk, places a FoldBrew sleeve into a clear cup, and pours in hot water. The camera holds on the product transformation: the sleeve visibly dissolves into the coffee until the package is gone and one serving remains. The final beat shows the worker taking the first sip as the cancelled takeaway cup is moved out of frame.

The recurring structure is: **unbox the routine, cancel the detour, dissolve the package, drink what was inside it.** The reverse-unboxing format makes FoldBrew’s unusual behavior the story itself, giving the brand a distinctive visual mechanism rather than using the cancellation only as a convenience message.

Office moments can change from video to video—minutes before a meeting, during a deadline, or at the first desk slump—but the transformation remains identical and easy to recognize. The clear cup, readable packaging, close-up dissolve, and finished drink appear in every edit so first-time viewers immediately understand the behavior: FoldBrew, a cup, and hot water replace the usual coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

FoldBrew is central because its package is both the thing being “unboxed” and the coffee itself. The campaign uses concise copy such as **“The package is the coffee.”**, **“Coffee run cancelled. Cup activated.”**, and **“FoldBrew. Dissolve your way to coffee.”** Shot with real hands, ordinary office settings, product photography, and short edits for Instagram, TikTok, and digital video, the idea is practical to repeat while making the dissolve the memorable payoff that drives first-time trial.
