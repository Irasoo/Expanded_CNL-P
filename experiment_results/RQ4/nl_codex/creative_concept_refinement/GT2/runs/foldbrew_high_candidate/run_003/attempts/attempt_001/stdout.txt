=== Final Creative Concept ===
**FoldBrew: “Promoted in Public.”**

FoldBrew turns its unusual preparation into a tiny office promotion ceremony. In every short-form video, the complete coffee sleeve is introduced as the next thing on a worker’s schedule: a close-up of a desk calendar, chat message, or meeting note reads **“NEXT PROMOTION: PACKAGING TO COFFEE.”** The worker drops the entire FoldBrew sleeve into a clear cup of hot water, making the transformation impossible to miss.

The video then follows one consistent visual proof: the full sleeve goes in, the sleeve visibly dissolves, the water changes into a serving of coffee, and the finished drink is shown beside the empty cup. As the sleeve disappears, the on-screen status changes from **“PACKAGING: IN PROGRESS”** to **“COFFEE: READY.”** A crisp dissolve sound completes the promotion. The sequence is fast, repeatable, and built around the product action rather than a separate advertising claim.

Each execution places the same promotion in a recognizable office moment: before a meeting, during a packed afternoon, or when the coffee machine queue is too long. The worker does not need a machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet; the package itself becomes the coffee. The office language makes the behavior feel specific to the target audience while giving FoldBrew a distinctive, ownable ritual beyond a standard product demonstration.

The final frame reads: **“FoldBrew. The package becomes the coffee.”** followed by **“Try the promotion.”** The concept drives first-time trial by showing exactly what to do and making the complete sleeve’s transformation the memorable payoff in every Instagram, TikTok, and short-form video execution.
