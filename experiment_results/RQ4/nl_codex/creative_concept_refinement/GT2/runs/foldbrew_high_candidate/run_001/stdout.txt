==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Concept: “The Last-Inch Brew”**

FoldBrew turns the moment before disposal into a recognizable coffee-saving move. Every short-form execution begins with a familiar office situation: a worker tidying a desk before a meeting or during a rushed break, holding what appears to be an empty coffee sleeve over the bin.

Just before the sleeve crosses the rim, the video freezes on the line:

**“Wait. That’s the coffee.”**

The same hand movement then continues through a sharp match cut: the sleeve drops into a clear mug of hot water. The camera holds in a tight macro shot as the edible, water-soluble sleeve visibly dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee. The finished drink and the worker’s first sip follow immediately, returning them to work without a machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

The repeated “last-inch switch”—bin to mug, discard gesture to coffee—becomes FoldBrew’s distinctive visual signature. It creates an immediate product mystery, then resolves it through a satisfying demonstration that cannot be separated from the product itself.

On-screen copy stays brief:

**“Don’t bin your coffee.”**  
**“The sleeve dissolves in hot water.”**  
**“The package becomes one serving of coffee.”**

The closing frame reads:

**“FoldBrew. Coffee from the sleeve.”**

TikTok and Instagram videos use one worker, one desk, a bin, a clear mug, hot water, and close-up product photography. Variations place the same last-inch switch before a meeting, during an afternoon desk break, or between office tasks, keeping the audience context familiar while making the unusual preparation memorable. Instagram carousels show the same three beats: **“Wait” → sleeve dissolving → finished coffee.**
