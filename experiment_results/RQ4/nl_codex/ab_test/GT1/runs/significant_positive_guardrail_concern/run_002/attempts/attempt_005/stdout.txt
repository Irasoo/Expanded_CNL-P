==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

Hypothesis:
Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

Test duration and sample sizes:
14 days. Control: 10,000 users; Variant: 10,000 users. Traffic split was 50%/50%.

Primary metric:
Purchase conversion rate was 10.0% for Control and 12.0% for Variant.

Effect:
Absolute difference: +2.0 percentage points.
Relative lift: +20.0%.

Statistical result:
p-value: 6.19e-06.
95% confidence interval for the difference: +1.13 to +2.87 percentage points.
The result is statistically significant.

Power and sample-ratio status:
Power status: underpowered. This is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result.
Sample-ratio mismatch: none detected.

Guardrail:
Customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%. The product team considers this a meaningful negative effect and a guardrail concern.

Recommendation:
investigate

Reasoning:
The Variant significantly improved purchase conversion, but the increase in customer support contact rate is a meaningful guardrail concern. The positive primary-metric result should therefore not be treated as sufficient for rollout.

Next steps:
Investigate why support contacts increased, assess the relationship between the button-text change and the guardrail impact, and resolve the guardrail concern before making a rollout decision.
