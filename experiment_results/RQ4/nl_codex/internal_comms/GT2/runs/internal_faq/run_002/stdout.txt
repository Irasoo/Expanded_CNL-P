# Internal FAQ: Hybrid-Work Pilot

## When will the new hybrid-work arrangement begin, and how long will it last?
The pilot begins on October 7 and will last eight weeks. Employees participating in the pilot must work from the office at least two days per week.

The available materials contain no new decision about additional commuting support. Human Resources will provide separate guidance before the pilot begins.

## Will the arrangement become permanent after the pilot?
