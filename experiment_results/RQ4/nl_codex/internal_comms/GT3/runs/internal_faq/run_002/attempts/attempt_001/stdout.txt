# Internal FAQ: Hybrid-Work Pilot

### 1. When will the new hybrid-work arrangement begin, and how long will it last?
The pilot begins on **October 7** and will last **eight weeks**. Employees participating in the pilot must work from the office at least **two days per week**.

### 2. Which teams will participate in the first phase?
The first phase includes the **Product, Design, and Customer Success** teams.

### 3. Will additional commuting support be provided?
The available information does not include a new decision about commuting support. **Human Resources will provide separate guidance before the pilot begins.**

### 4. Will the hybrid-work arrangement become permanent after the pilot?
No decision has been made about permanent adoption. The company will evaluate **employee feedback and data from the eight-week pilot** before making a decision.
