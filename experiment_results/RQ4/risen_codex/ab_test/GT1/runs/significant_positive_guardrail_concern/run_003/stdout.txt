## Checkout Button Guardrail Test

- **Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.
- **Duration:** 14 days.
- **Sample sizes:** Control: 10,000; Variant: 10,000.
- **Conversion rates:** Control: 10.0%; Variant: 12.0%.
- **Effect:** Absolute difference of 2.0 percentage points; relative lift of 20.0%.
- **Statistical result:** p-value = 6.188458781738804e-06. The 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference is [1.133%, 2.867%]. The result is statistically significant.
- **Power:** The experiment is marked **underpowered**. This is diagnostic context and does not override the statistically significant result.
- **Sample ratio mismatch:** None detected; traffic was split 50%/50%.
- **Guardrail assessment:** The customer support contact rate increased from 2% to 5%. The product team considers this a meaningful negative effect and a guardrail concern.

**Recommendation: investigate.** The variant shows a statistically significant conversion improvement, but the meaningful increase in customer support contacts requires investigation before drawing an overall positive conclusion.

**Next steps:** Investigate the cause of the support-contact increase and evaluate it alongside the conversion improvement before making a rollout decision.
