==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

**Test setup:** The experiment ran for 14 days. Control had 10,000 users and a 10.0% conversion rate; the variant had 10,000 users and a 12.0% conversion rate.

**Primary result:** The absolute difference was **+2.0 percentage points**, with a **20.0% relative lift**. The 95% confidence interval for the difference was **1.13 to 2.87 percentage points**. The p-value was **6.19 × 10⁻⁶**, so the result was statistically significant.

**Power and allocation:** The analysis reports the test as **underpowered**. Power status is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result. Sample-ratio mismatch was **false**: traffic shares were 50%/50%, with equal sample sizes. If sample-ratio mismatch were true, the experiment would be inconclusive.

**Guardrail:** Customer support contact rate increased from **2% to 5%**. The product team considers this a meaningful negative effect and a guardrail concern.

**Recommendation:** **investigate**

**Reasoning and next steps:** The variant produced a statistically significant increase in purchase conversion, but the increase in customer support contact rate is a reported guardrail concern. Investigate the cause and implications of that guardrail change.
