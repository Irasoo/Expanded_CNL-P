==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

**Experiment:** 14 days; control sample size **10,000** and variant sample size **10,000**.

**Primary metric results:**
- Control conversion rate: **10.0%**
- Variant conversion rate: **12.0%**
- Absolute difference: **+2.0 percentage points**
- Relative lift: **+20.0%**
- p-value: **6.19 × 10⁻⁶**
- 95% confidence interval for the difference: **[1.13, 2.87] percentage points**
- Statistical significance: **Yes**
- Power status: **Underpowered**

Power status is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result.

**Sample-ratio mismatch:** **No**; traffic shares were 50% control and 50% variant. If a true sample-ratio mismatch were present, the experiment would be inconclusive.

**Guardrail:** Customer support contact rate increased from **2% to 5%**. The product team considers this a meaningful negative effect and a guardrail concern.

**Recommendation:** **investigate**

**Reasoning:** The variant produced a statistically significant positive change in purchase conversion, but the increase in customer support contact rate is a meaningful negative guardrail effect.

**Next steps:** Investigate the customer support contact-rate increase and assess it alongside the positive primary-metric result.
