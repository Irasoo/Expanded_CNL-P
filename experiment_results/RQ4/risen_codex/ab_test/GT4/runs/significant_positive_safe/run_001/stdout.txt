**Hypothesis:** Changing the checkout button text from “Continue” to “Buy Now” will increase purchase conversion rate.

**Experiment:** 14 days. Control: 10,000 users, 10.0% conversion. Variant: 10,000 users, 12.0% conversion.

**Result:** Absolute difference was **+2.0 percentage points**; relative lift was **+20.0%**. The p-value was **6.19 × 10⁻⁶**. The 95% confidence interval for the absolute difference was **[1.13, 2.87] percentage points**.

**Statistical assessment:** The result was statistically significant. Power status was **underpowered**; this is diagnostic and does not override the statistically significant result.

**Experiment quality and guardrails:** No sample-ratio mismatch was detected. If a sample-ratio mismatch were present, the experiment would be inconclusive. Revenue per user, page load time, and customer support contact rate were stable, with no meaningful negative effects observed. Guardrail concern: **false**.
