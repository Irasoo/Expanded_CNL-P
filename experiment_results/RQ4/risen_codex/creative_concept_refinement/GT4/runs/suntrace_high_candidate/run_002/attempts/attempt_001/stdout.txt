Brand:
SunTrace

A facial sunscreen that is transparent when applied normally. The package includes reusable UV-sensitive stickers. Young adults aged 18-30 who understand that sunscreen is important but frequently forget to reapply it during outdoor activities.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**SunTrace Checkpoint: The Mark You Get Before You Go Back Out**

SunTrace turns the moment between two outdoor activities into a visual checkpoint. Each short video begins with a tight, consistent close-up of a sticker being removed from the same area of the face or arm. The surrounding skin temporarily reveals the sticker shape from sun exposure, while the covered area remains unchanged.

The reveal is introduced as an **“OUTDOOR CHECKPOINT”**, followed immediately by one question:

**“Going back out?”**

A rapid rewind shows the earlier checkpoint: the person applies transparent SunTrace SPF 50, places one included UV-sensitive sticker on the same area, and heads outside for an ordinary campus activity such as lunch, practice, or walking between classes. A simple **“Later”** card returns to the reveal, with the concise caption:

**“Shows sun-exposure contrast, not UV damage.”**

The contrast is not treated as a measurement or score. It is the visible reminder that makes an invisible protection routine easier to remember. Immediately after the reveal, SunTrace appears in the person’s hand as they reapply before leaving the library, courtyard, cafeteria, or game.

Every video ends on the same unmistakable action cue:

**“Reapply before you step back out.”**

The repeatable series format is built around recognizable campus “checkpoints”: leaving one place, seeing the trace from the previous outing, and applying SunTrace before the next one. The sticker reveal gives each transition a distinctive visual signature, while the reapplication moment makes the product the solution rather than the demonstration itself.

Each 10–15-second vertical video uses four fixed beats: sticker reveal, rewind to SunTrace application and sticker placement, labeled “Later” return with the short disclaimer, and reapplication before heading outdoors again. The camera stays locked on the same close-up, skin area, background, and natural-light setup so the temporary contrast remains easy to recognize without implying precision. The execution requires only a phone, SunTrace SPF 50, the included reusable sticker, and an everyday campus setting, making the format practical for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social media communities.
