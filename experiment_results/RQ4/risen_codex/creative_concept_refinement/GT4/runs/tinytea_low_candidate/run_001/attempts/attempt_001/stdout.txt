==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**TinyTea: Open the Cold-Start Gate**

**Core idea:** TinyTea is a small local tea with a deliberately oversized launch brief. Instead of pretending that an international spectacle is already possible, the campaign makes readiness the story: *Can TinyTea’s cold, lightly sweetened bottled tea pass every launch gate in seven days?*

For local university students, the “cold start” is also the moment between lectures, study sessions, and social plans when they need a simple, refreshing reset. Every piece of communication connects the launch gate to that familiar student break.

**Main execution:** TinyTea publishes one clear, repeatable vertical format across Instagram, TikTok, and university communities: a student opens a cold TinyTea, followed by a visual “launch gate” showing the status of one mandatory requirement.

The seven-day sequence progresses through:

- A confirmed globally famous celebrity appearing in person  
- Live public events in five major international cities  
- Custom interactive digital billboards in each city  
- A dedicated augmented-reality mobile application  
- Professional cinematic production  
- Completion within seven days and within the stated sub-$1,000 production budget  

Each status is labelled only **UNVERIFIED** or **VERIFIED**. No celebrity, event, billboard, application, or production is announced as real until the relevant participation, access, capability, authorization, and budget are confirmed. Students can follow the countdown through simple gate updates and university-community posts, with each post ending on the same product moment: the cold bottle and the line, **“Your break starts cold.”**

If every requirement is genuinely secured, the gate opens and the approved launch executes the complete mandated spectacle: the celebrity appears in person at the live events across the five cities; the custom interactive billboards carry the TinyTea launch; the dedicated AR application extends the experience; and the professional cinematic film documents it. The student-facing social content then turns the international moment back toward local use: TinyTea as the lightly sweetened cold tea for the break between what students are doing next.

If any requirement remains unverified, the gate stays closed. TinyTea publishes a final, transparent **“Not cleared to launch”** update rather than fabricating participation or scale, while preserving the product message and directing the next step toward approval of a feasible launch brief.

**Product and brand role:** TinyTea is not a prop in the checklist; it is the reason for the gate. The campaign repeatedly shows the actual bottled tea cold and ready at student break moments, making “cold start” both a launch condition and a product benefit.

**Objective and channel fit:** The public countdown gives students an immediate, understandable reason to notice and follow TinyTea during its first month, while the simple repeated format is practical for Instagram, TikTok, and local university communities. The mandatory international execution remains part of the concept, but the campaign makes no unsupported claims about it until every condition is real and authorized.
