==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**TinyTea: The Seven-Day Tiny Break Relay**

TinyTea turns the ordinary gap between university classes into one shared, instantly recognizable ritual: **open a cold bottle, take one lightly sweetened sip, pause for ten seconds, and get back to the day**. A globally famous celebrity appearing in person starts the ritual at synchronized live public events in five major international cities, while local university students become the campaign’s home audience and visible participants.

Before launch, each participating local university community receives a simple schedule for its own “Tiny Break” moment, with students invited to gather between lectures, bring or buy a cold TinyTea, and recreate the ten-second pause. The five-city events happen at the same announced time. In every city, the celebrity opens a cold TinyTea and leads the crowd through the ritual. The moment is filmed as a single, repeatable sequence rather than a large program: **countdown, bottle opening, sip, pause, return to the day**.

Custom interactive digital billboards in each city display the countdown and prompt nearby students to join the Tiny Break. At zero, every billboard switches to the same branded visual cue and live event content. The dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application has one focused function: pointing a phone at a TinyTea bottle activates a ten-second countdown and a shareable overlay for an Instagram or TikTok post. Students who do not use the app can participate through the same countdown and ritual in person or through a short social video using **#TinyBreak**.

Professional cinematic production captures the celebrity, the five synchronized events, the billboards, and local university participation in the same compact visual format. Short edits for Instagram and TikTok connect the international spectacle directly to local student life by ending with the participating university community’s location, the TinyTea brand, and the product’s clear role as a cold, lightly sweetened tea for a quick break between classes. The celebrity supplies the launch attention; the student-led ritual makes the idea locally relevant and repeatable throughout TinyTea’s first month.

The concept is a **conditional production plan**: because the brief requires a globally famous celebrity, five international live events, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days despite a total budget below $1,000, execution may begin only after written confirmation that every mandatory resource is supplied at no campaign cost and is available within the seven-day window. Without those confirmed no-cost resources, the concept cannot truthfully be produced under the stated brief.
