==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.
================== END USER INPUT 1/1 ==================

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**The Missing Sleeve**

FoldBrew turns an everyday office mystery into a repeatable short-form series: **“Where did the coffee sleeve go?”** Each film opens like a tiny workplace investigation. A meeting notification appears, a desk drawer is opened, or the afternoon slump hits. On-screen text identifies the case—**“CASE 03: Coffee needed before the meeting.”** The worker places a FoldBrew sleeve in a clear cup of hot water and leaves the camera running in one continuous, satisfying shot.

The sleeve visibly dissolves into the drink. The video then returns to the simple “evidence” layout: the cup now contains coffee, the sleeve is gone, and the worker takes an immediate sip before opening the meeting or returning to work. The final text resolves the mystery: **“Missing sleeve. Coffee found.”** A FoldBrew pack shot follows with: **“Drop the sleeve. Make the coffee.”**

The product is the entire proof of the idea: it is not merely packaging that disappears—it becomes one serving of coffee. Each execution keeps the same recognizable investigation format while changing only the office trigger, such as a meeting starting, a late-afternoon desk break, or coffee running out at work. The sequence always shows the sleeve, hot water, the visible dissolve, the finished coffee, and the immediate workday payoff.

Designed for Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video, the concept uses ordinary desk setups, a cup, hot water, and close-up product footage. Real-time dissolution or clearly labelled speed changes keep the demonstration credible. The office-mystery framing gives FoldBrew a distinctive, memorable voice while making the first-trial reason instantly clear: **no machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet—just the sleeve that becomes the coffee.**
