==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**Don’t Sleeve the Cup. Brew the Sleeve.**

FoldBrew turns a familiar takeaway-coffee gesture inside out. In every short video, an urban office worker reaches for what looks like an ordinary coffee cup sleeve—but instead of sliding it around a finished drink, they place the sleeve into a clear cup of hot water.

The opening is built around a recognizable office moment: a calendar alert appears for an imminent meeting, a colleague messages “Coffee?”, or the worker realizes the coffee machine is occupied. The worker reaches for the FoldBrew sleeve as if preparing a normal takeaway coffee. A quick on-screen prompt interrupts the expected action: **“Don’t sleeve it.”** The sleeve is dropped into the cup. **“Brew it.”**

The camera stays tight and uninterrupted as the sleeve visibly dissolves. The packaging disappears while the water becomes one serving of coffee. The finished drink is lifted into the same frame, with the end line: **“The sleeve is the coffee. FoldBrew.”**

The creative device is the reversal of the sleeve’s familiar job. Viewers first recognize the object as something that belongs around a cup, then see FoldBrew use that same object as the preparation itself. This makes the unusual product behavior feel specific and memorable rather than presenting the dissolve as a generic disappearing-package trick.

The series, titled **“Don’t Sleeve It. Brew It.”**, uses repeatable desk-based situations: a meeting notification, an afternoon slump, a crowded office coffee machine, or a short break between tasks. Each film follows the same three-beat structure: show the expected sleeve behavior, reverse it, and reveal the coffee forming in the cup. Tight framing, a clear cup, strong contrast, and a short real-time dissolution keep the transformation legible on a phone screen.

FoldBrew is the hero throughout. Its water-soluble coffee sleeve is not just shown as packaging; its unexpected second role is the idea. The demonstration makes clear that the package itself becomes one serving of coffee, without a coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet. For urban office workers who want coffee without leaving their desk or assembling the usual setup, FoldBrew turns a familiar coffee object into a simple, surprising preparation ritual—one sleeve, one cup, one drink.
