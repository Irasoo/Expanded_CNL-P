=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**Concept: “The Everyday Relay”**

FreshDrop is the one thing that carries from one everyday moment into the next. Each social film follows one precise visual rule: the bottle enters from the left, travels across the frame, and exits to the right. The next scene begins with the bottle reappearing from the left in exactly the same position and movement.

The first frame shows the FreshDrop bottle and brand name immediately as someone picks it up. In the next beat, the bottle crosses the screen and the setting changes: leaving home becomes commuting, a work break becomes time outdoors, or a solo moment becomes meeting friends. Different adults aged 18–45 carry out familiar, positive routines, while FreshDrop remains the visual relay connecting them.

Each vertical video runs through three or four quick transitions. The bottle is always the source of the cut and stays clearly visible, making its portable role easy to understand without adding extra claims or effects. The final movement places it naturally beside the person in the new setting—ready for the next part of the day.

The consistent ending is:

**FreshDrop**  
**Bottled mineral water. Keep Water Close.**

The same relay structure works in Facebook and Instagram cutdowns, with one or two transitions followed by the end frame. Static posts use a simple three-panel layout: the bottle occupies the same position in each panel while the surroundings change from one daily context to another. The recurring left-to-right movement and fixed bottle position give the campaign a recognizable signature, while the everyday situations keep it broadly relatable and reusable for a conventional bottled-water brand.

FreshDrop is central as a portable bottle of mineral water that moves naturally with people through their day. By turning that practical benefit into a clear, repeatable relay, the campaign builds brand recognition and communicates portability quickly in-feed.
