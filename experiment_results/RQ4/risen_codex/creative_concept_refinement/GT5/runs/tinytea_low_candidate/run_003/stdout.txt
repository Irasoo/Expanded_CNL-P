=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
## TinyTea: **Cold Everywhere? Not Yet.**

### Core idea  
Turn TinyTea’s impossible launch brief into the story: a small local tea brand cannot honestly pretend it has a globally famous celebrity, live events in five international cities, interactive billboards, an AR app, cinematic production, and seven days to launch on less than $1,000. Instead of faking a spectacle, TinyTea makes the gap visible and asks students where a genuinely local cold-tea launch should begin.

### Main execution  
Across Instagram, TikTok, and university communities, TinyTea posts a short, phone-shot series titled **“The Launch We Can’t Afford Yet.”** Each video opens with an exaggerated global-launch checklist:

**Celebrity. Five cities. Interactive billboards. AR app. Cinematic film. Seven days. Under $1,000.**

The checklist stops at the reality check: **“We’re a small local tea company. So we’re starting with the part we can actually deliver: lightly sweetened tea, served cold.”**

The next shot is a real TinyTea bottle being chilled, opened, and shared by students in familiar campus settings. Students comment or post their own answer to **“Where should TinyTea be cold next?”**—a study spot, library steps, residence common room, or campus hangout. The strongest responses become simple follow-up posts and local community updates.

### Product and brand role  
TinyTea is the honest alternative to an overproduced global launch: a lightly sweetened bottled tea that is cold, accessible, and made for everyday student moments. The bottle and its cold-serving benefit appear in every piece of content rather than serving as a prop for an unrelated stunt.

### Audience and objective  
The opening checklist gives students an immediate, relatable hook—the absurdity of a global spectacle for a small local launch—while the reality check makes TinyTea memorable and credible. Student participation creates repeatable Instagram and TikTok content and introduces the product through university communities, increasing awareness during the first month without pretending that the mandatory international production requirements have been delivered.

This is an awareness concept and transparent launch hold, not a claim that the celebrity appearance, five-city events, interactive billboards, AR application, cinematic production, and seven-day production schedule have been completed.
