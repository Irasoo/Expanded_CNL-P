Brand:
TinyTea

Key product benefit:
A lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold.

Budget:
Very low. The total campaign production budget is below $1,000.

Constraints:
The central campaign concept must include a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, a live public event in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, professional cinematic production, and completion of all production within seven days. ==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**TinyTea Cold Pause — The Seven-Day Global Pause**

TinyTea turns a familiar student moment—being overwhelmed between classes, before an exam, or after a long study session—into one simple, recognizable ritual: **open a cold TinyTea, take one refreshing pause, and share it**. The ritual is always shown in the same order so the product remains the hero: the bottle opens, the student takes a sip, the surrounding pressure briefly stops, and the student posts the pause.

A globally famous celebrity launches the ritual in person at live events in five major international cities. Rather than adding separate activities, every event centers on one demonstration: the celebrity opens a cold, lightly sweetened TinyTea, takes the pause, and invites students everywhere to repeat it. The event footage becomes the campaign’s professional cinematic launch film and short-form edits for Instagram and TikTok.

At each event, a custom interactive digital billboard repeats the same cold-pause sequence. When people interact with it, the screen prompts them to take and share their own pause. The dedicated augmented-reality mobile application uses one simple effect: it places a virtual TinyTea and the pause gesture into the user’s surroundings so students can record the ritual wherever they are. No additional games or mechanics are introduced.

On local university communities, TinyTea posts a single prompt: **“Where do you need a cold pause today?”** Students share their pause between lectures, before studying, or after an exam using the campaign hashtag. Selected posts become short social edits that connect the five-city launch to everyday campus life.

TinyTea is essential to the idea: its cold, lightly sweetened refreshment creates the pause, while the repeated opening-and-sip gesture makes the new bottle easy to recognize and remember. The celebrity supplies the launch signal, the events and billboards make the ritual visible, the AR application lets students place it in their own surroundings, and campus posts make it locally relevant. Together, the executions build awareness of TinyTea during its first month without introducing separate campaign messages.

The concept is production-gated: the five in-person celebrity appearances, international events, interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day delivery must all be confirmed and funded before launch. The below-$1,000 budget cannot by itself support those mandatory requirements, so the campaign may be presented or produced only after the brief owner formally confirms additional funding or already-secured resources, access, permits, technical support, and talent availability. Until then, **TinyTea Cold Pause** remains the complete creative platform rather than an unverified production promise.
