Creative brief: 

==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.
================== END USER INPUT 1/1 ==================

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**The Desk Test**

FoldBrew turns the everyday office desk into a simple challenge: **“Can this desk make coffee?”** Each short video opens with a worker arriving at a desk where the usual coffee setup is missing. The camera quickly inventories the scene: a cup, hot water, and no machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet. Then the worker reveals what looks like a coffee cup sleeve, with its FoldBrew labeling clearly visible.

The sleeve is placed directly into the hot water in one continuous close-up. The camera stays on the cup as the edible, water-soluble sleeve dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee. The finished drink is shown immediately afterward, followed by the worker taking the first sip as the meeting begins, the inbox fills, or the workday gets moving. Every film ends with the recurring line: **“Desk passed. FoldBrew.”** A concise action cue follows: **“Drop the sleeve in. Watch it brew.”**

The recognizable format gives the product a distinctive role beyond a standard preparation demonstration. The question is not whether the worker can find a coffee shop or operate equipment; it is whether the desk has what it needs to make coffee. FoldBrew is the unexpected item that completes the setup because its package becomes the serving itself. Clear labeling, uninterrupted action, and before-and-after shots make the transformation unmistakable and prevent the sleeve from being confused with an ordinary paper sleeve.

The series uses familiar moments for urban office workers aged 22-35: a meeting is about to start, a worker arrives before the café opens, or a desk has no coffee equipment. The situations vary, but the desk test and visible dissolve remain constant, creating a repeatable short-form signature that is easy to recognize and remember. By showing that a cup, hot water, and FoldBrew are enough for one serving, the campaign drives first-time trial and makes the product’s unusual behavior the answer to a common workplace coffee problem.
