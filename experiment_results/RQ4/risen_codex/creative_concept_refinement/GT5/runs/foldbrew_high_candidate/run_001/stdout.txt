Creative brief: 

==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.
================== END USER INPUT 1/1 ==================

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**Concept: “Put the Packaging In.”**

FoldBrew turns the moment people expect to throw packaging away into the moment their coffee is made. The campaign’s central line is: **“This is the coffee sleeve. Put it in the cup.”**

Each short-form video opens on a familiar office desk: a hot cup, a FoldBrew sleeve, and no coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet in sight. On-screen text asks, **“What if the packaging was the coffee?”** The creator places the FoldBrew sleeve into hot water. The camera stays close and clearly shows the sleeve dissolving into the drink. The final shot is the finished serving of coffee beside the empty desk setup, with the line: **“FoldBrew. The package becomes your coffee.”**

The hero video would be a simple vertical demonstration made with ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Its visual rule is that the dissolve cannot be hidden: the product must be shown before, during, and after the sleeve dissolves. Cutdowns for Instagram Reels and TikTok would retain the same sequence, using the visual surprise as the hook rather than relying on a celebrity, special setting, or environmental claim. A supporting Instagram carousel could show three frames—**the sleeve, the sleeve dissolving, the coffee**—with the caption, **“No machine. No pod. No disposable coffee sachet. Just put FoldBrew in hot water.”**

FoldBrew is the demonstration, not merely the subject of the ad: its unusual package behavior is the entire reveal. By placing the action at an office desk, the idea directly reflects urban workers aged 22–35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster, lower-waste option at work. It gives them a memorable trial instruction they can immediately understand and repeat: **put the sleeve in hot water and watch it become one serving of coffee.**
