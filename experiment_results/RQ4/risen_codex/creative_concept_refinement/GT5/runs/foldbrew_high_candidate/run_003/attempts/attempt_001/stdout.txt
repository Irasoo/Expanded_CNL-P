Creative brief: 

==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.
================== END USER INPUT 1/1 ==================

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT ===
**The Reverse Unwrap**

FoldBrew turns the familiar act of unwrapping coffee into its opposite: the item that looks like packaging is the serving itself. The campaign makes this reversal the recognizable brand behavior. Every execution begins with the same moment of hesitation: a hand instinctively starts to remove or discard the FoldBrew sleeve, then stops. The message appears:

**“Stop. Put the package in.”**

The next move is immediate and unmistakable. The entire labeled FoldBrew coffee sleeve is placed into a mug of hot water. In one continuous macro shot, the sleeve visibly dissolves and becomes the coffee. The camera holds on the transformation long enough to show three clear states: intact FoldBrew sleeve, dissolving sleeve in hot water, finished coffee.

The recurring end line is:

**“The sleeve becomes the coffee. FoldBrew.”**

A concise final frame adds:

**“No machine. Just hot water.”**  
**“Try it at your desk.”**

The execution is designed as a repeatable one-shot format for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and short-form digital video: an ordinary office desk, one FoldBrew sleeve, one mug of hot water, and a practical close-up of the real dissolve. There are no visual effects, elaborate sets, or additional characters. The labeled sleeve remains visible before and during the dissolve so viewers understand that this is the edible, water-soluble coffee sleeve, not external shipping or non-consumable packaging.

Short cutdowns retain the same mnemonic sequence: the instinct to unwrap, the interruption, the sleeve entering the mug, and the visible transformation into coffee. Still posts can freeze the sleeve halfway through dissolving with the line **“Don’t unwrap your coffee.”**

For urban office workers who buy takeaway coffee but want a faster option at work, the reverse-unwrapping moment creates a distinctive, memorable reason to stop scrolling. The practical demonstration makes the behavior clear: FoldBrew delivers premium instant coffee without a coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet. Most importantly, first-time users leave with one simple action attached to the brand:

**Put the package in.**
