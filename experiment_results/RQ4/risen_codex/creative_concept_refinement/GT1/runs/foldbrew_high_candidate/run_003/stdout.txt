==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.
================== END USER INPUT 1/1 ==================

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**The Coffee Cup That Disappears**

FoldBrew turns the most unexpected part of making coffee into the whole reason to try it: the package disappears into the drink. The campaign presents FoldBrew as the fast office coffee ritual that starts with a sleeve and ends with a serving of coffee, without a coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Each short-form video opens on a familiar workday moment: an urban office worker reaches for takeaway coffee, checks the time, or pauses at a desk with no coffee machine nearby. The action then cuts to a clear, close-up demonstration. They place the FoldBrew sleeve into a cup of hot water. The sleeve visibly dissolves, and the finished coffee is shown in the same cup. The worker takes a sip and returns to work.

The recurring line is: **“The package is the coffee.”** On-screen copy keeps the behavior memorable and direct: **“Drop in the sleeve. Watch it dissolve. Drink your coffee.”** The final frame shows the FoldBrew pack beside the prepared drink with a first-trial prompt such as **“Try the coffee cup that disappears.”**

For Instagram Reels, TikTok, and short-form digital video, the concept can be produced with ordinary product photography, desk-based office scenes, hot water, a transparent cup, and macro shots of the dissolving sleeve. The central visual is always the same unmistakable product proof: the package entering hot water, dissolving, and becoming one serving of coffee. Variations can feature different relatable office moments, but the demonstration remains the payoff in every execution.

FoldBrew is the active solution to the audience’s specific routine: a premium instant coffee concentrate that can be prepared quickly at work without a machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet. By making the unusual preparation behavior the campaign’s repeated visual signature, the idea gives first-time viewers a simple reason to remember FoldBrew and try it.
