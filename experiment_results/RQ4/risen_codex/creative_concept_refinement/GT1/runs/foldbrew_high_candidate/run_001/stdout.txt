Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**The Unboxing You Don’t Open**

FoldBrew turns the familiar social-media unboxing into an office coffee surprise: the best part is not opening the package—it is watching the package become the drink.

Each 10–15-second Instagram Reel or TikTok begins in a recognizable desk-level unboxing format. An office worker sets a FoldBrew sleeve beside a clear cup of hot water, as if presenting a new coffee find. Their hand moves to tear the sleeve open.

The video cuts sharply to on-screen text:

**“Don’t unbox your coffee.”**

The worker places the clearly labeled, intact sleeve into the cup instead. A tight macro shot makes the transformation the hero: the sleeve visibly dissolves in the hot water, followed by a clear shot of the finished serving and the empty water surface. The final frame reads:

**“The package becomes the coffee.”**  
**“FoldBrew. Drop the whole sleeve.”**  
**“Try it at your next desk break.”**

The unboxing reversal gives the product a distinctive role in a familiar short-form format. Viewers expect a package to be opened, but FoldBrew rewards them for doing the opposite. The memorable behavior is therefore built into the opening hook, the product demonstration, and the instruction—not added as a separate message.

The series adapts the same simple setup to specific office pressures: a meeting about to begin, a coffee run that would interrupt focused work, or the afternoon slump at a desk. Each variation opens with a short situation line such as **“Coffee unboxing before the meeting starts”** or **“Today’s desk delivery: don’t open it.”** The sequence stays consistent: present, reach to open, stop, drop, dissolve, drink.

FoldBrew remains central in every frame through its recognizable sleeve, the direct whole-sleeve drop, and the visibly legible dissolve. A clear cup, overhead or desk-level product framing, macro inserts, and ordinary office photography keep the concept practical for repeatable short-form production and cutdowns. The idea speaks directly to urban office workers by turning a familiar online behavior into a faster desk-side coffee ritual, while making FoldBrew’s unusual package-to-coffee function easy to remember and try.
