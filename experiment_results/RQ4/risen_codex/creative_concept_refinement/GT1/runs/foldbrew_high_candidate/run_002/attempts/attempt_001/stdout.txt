=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Concept: “Watch the Cup Disappear”**

FoldBrew turns the most familiar part of the coffee routine—the package—into the unexpected hero. In short, satisfying videos, an office worker reaches for a FoldBrew during a busy workday, skips the coffee machine and pod search, and drops the coffee sleeve into hot water. The camera stays close as the sleeve dissolves visibly into the drink, transforming from packaging into one serving of coffee.

Each video begins with a simple office moment: a meeting about to start, a crowded kitchen, or a worker needing coffee between tasks. The worker places the FoldBrew sleeve into a clear mug of hot water. Close-up footage captures the sleeve softening and disappearing while the coffee forms in the water. The final shot cuts to the finished drink beside the now-empty FoldBrew package, with the line:

**“The package becomes the coffee.”**

On-screen copy reinforces the practical benefit without making additional environmental claims:

**“No machine. No pod. No disposable coffee sachet.”**  
**“Just hot water and FoldBrew.”**

The main Instagram Reel, TikTok, and short-form video would run for approximately 10–15 seconds, using ordinary office photography, a clear mug, hot water, and close-up product shots. The visual payoff—the sleeve visibly dissolving—is shown in real time or a simple speed-adjusted close-up, making the unusual behavior immediately understandable and memorable. A shorter cut can begin directly with the sleeve disappearing into the mug, followed by the product name and “Try FoldBrew at work.”

Still images and carousel posts can extend the idea with a three-step sequence:

1. **Fold** — the FoldBrew coffee sleeve  
2. **Drop** — into hot water  
3. **Disappear** — into one serving of coffee

FoldBrew’s role is central rather than incidental: the product is both the coffee concentrate and the package that creates the drink. By dramatizing that single transformation in an everyday office setting, the concept gives urban office workers aged 22–35 a fast, easy-to-understand reason to try FoldBrew and makes its “dissolve the packaging to make the coffee” behavior the thing they remember.
