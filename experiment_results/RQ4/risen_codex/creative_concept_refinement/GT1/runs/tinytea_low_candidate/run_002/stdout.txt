==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: The World’s Smallest Break, Everywhere at Once**

TinyTea turns the contrast between a globally staged spectacle and a student’s five-second pause into the campaign idea. During TinyTea’s first month, one globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live public event in each of five major international cities. At every event, the celebrity stops, opens a cold, lightly sweetened TinyTea, and says: **“Take the world’s smallest break.”**

The dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application gives students one simple action: point it at a TinyTea bottle to unlock a five-second visual break and record their own version. Students share those clips to Instagram and TikTok using **“Where will you take your tiny break?”** Custom interactive digital billboards in each city show the five-second submissions as a live relay, while the celebrity’s event moment moves from city to city. A single professional cinematic film cuts between lecture corridors, libraries, campus spaces, and the five public events, making the international spectacle feel connected to the ordinary breaks of university students.

TinyTea is the mechanism, not just the sponsor: opening its cold bottle starts the five-second experience, the bottle anchors the AR interaction and billboard visuals, and drinking it gives students a tangible reason to join and share. Local university communities receive the same prompt and short social edits, allowing students who are not at the events to participate asynchronously and make the global relay feel personal and relevant to campus life.

The concept is deliberately structured as one repeatable five-second action across all required elements: celebrity appearances, five live city events, interactive billboards, the dedicated AR application, cinematic content, and student social participation. It is a launch vision that requires every mandatory production condition to be secured in advance, including completion within seven days and delivery within the sub-$1,000 production budget; without that required support, it cannot be presented as an executable first-month campaign.
