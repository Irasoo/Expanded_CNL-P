TinyTea

Increase awareness of TinyTea during the first month after launch.

Key product benefit:
A lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold.

Very low. The total campaign production budget is below $1,000.

Constraints:
The central campaign concept must include a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, a live public event in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, professional cinematic production, and completion of all production within seven days. ==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**TinyTea: No Red Carpet. Just Cold Tea.**

TinyTea turns an oversized global launch into one small, repeatable student ritual: **the Cold Break**. The spectacle can be worldwide, but the refreshment stays simple—a chilled bottle, a crack of the cap, a lightly sweet sip, and a pass to the person who needs a break.

A globally famous celebrity specified by the brief appears in person at live TinyTea events in five major international cities. Each event follows the same 10-second action rather than a long endorsement: the celebrity arrives, ignores the expected red-carpet theatrics, takes a cold TinyTea, opens it, and passes it to a student with the line:

**“No red carpet. Just cold tea.”**

The moment becomes the campaign’s cinematic hero film and a set of short Instagram and TikTok edits. Every version opens with exaggerated launch spectacle—flashes, countdowns, oversized introductions—then cuts sharply to the familiar sound and sight of a chilled bottle opening. The payoff is always the same:

**“TinyTea. Take a cold break.”**

The five-city events, custom interactive digital billboards, and dedicated AR mobile application all use this single mechanism rather than separate ideas. Each billboard begins with a city-specific prompt such as **“Where do you take your cold break?”** and resolves to the same bottle-opening moment and campaign line. The AR application lets students place a virtual Cold Break marker into an everyday scene—outside a lecture hall, on a library desk, or at a campus hangout—and share it to Instagram or TikTok. The experience is not about a complicated game; it makes the ordinary student break visually recognizable and ties every interaction to TinyTea served cold.

Local university students are the campaign’s main social audience. They see the celebrity spectacle through short-form edits, then recreate the Cold Break with their own TinyTea: one student identifies the moment, another opens the chilled bottle, and the drink is passed to a friend. University communities repost the most relatable breaks during the first month, using **#TinyTeaColdBreak** and tagging the next student or campus location that needs one. The celebrity creates initial attention; the repeatable ritual gives local students a reason to participate; and the cold bottle keeps the product benefit visible in every execution.

The campaign uses one script, one visual transition, one line, and one product action across the cinematic film, five events, billboards, AR, Instagram, TikTok, and university-community content. TinyTea is therefore not a prop within a generic celebrity campaign: its lightly sweetened, cold-served format is the entire payoff.

**Production condition:** the mandatory globally famous celebrity, five international in-person events, five custom interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day completion cannot be delivered within the stated sub-$1,000 budget without confirmed external resources or a formal change to the brief. This concept is therefore the unified creative treatment for those requirements, pending the authorization and resources needed to produce them honestly.
