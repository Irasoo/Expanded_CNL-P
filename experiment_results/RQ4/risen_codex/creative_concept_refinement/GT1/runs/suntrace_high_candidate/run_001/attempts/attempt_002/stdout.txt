=== Final Creative Concept ===
**SunTrace: The Face Check**

SunTrace turns its reusable UV-sensitive sticker into a personal reapplication cue. Young adults may swipe past a sunscreen reminder, but they are unlikely to miss one that appears every time they check their own face.

**15-second vertical execution**

The video opens on a tight front-camera selfie. A small SunTrace sticker is visible on the creator’s cheek as their thumb makes a swiping motion across the screen.

On-screen text: **“You can swipe past a sunscreen reminder. You can’t swipe past your own face.”**

The video cuts to the start of the day, using the same simple selfie framing:

1. The creator applies SunTrace SPF 50 to their face, showing that it goes on transparent, then places one included reusable UV-sensitive sticker on a visible cheek area.
2. During an outdoor activity, they check their front camera. The sticker catches their attention. They say, **“Face check.”**
3. They briefly remove the sticker, reapply SunTrace to the whole face, and replace the reusable sticker in the same spot before continuing outside.
4. Later, they remove the sticker on camera. The covered area remains visually unchanged while the surrounding exposed area temporarily reveals the sticker’s shape.

The final close-up holds long enough for the contrast to read on a phone screen, with the clarification: **“A visual sign of exposure—not a UV measurement.”**

The closing frame shows SunTrace SPF 50 and the reusable stickers with: **“Apply. Face check. Reapply. Repeat.”**

The product provides SPF 50 protection and goes on transparently; the sticker makes otherwise invisible sun exposure visible and creates a reminder users can notice during their normal front-camera checks. Each video can be filmed by one person on a phone in an everyday campus, park, or outdoor setting, making the format easy to repeat on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and university social channels.
