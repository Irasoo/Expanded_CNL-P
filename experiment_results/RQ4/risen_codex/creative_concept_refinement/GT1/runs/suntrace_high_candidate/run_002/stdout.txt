==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

=== Final Creative Concept ===
**Concept: “Pause the Break. Reapply.”**

SunTrace turns the moment young adults naturally pause outdoors into a literal pause in the video—and a memorable sunscreen reminder.

In 15–20-second TikTok and Instagram Reels, the creator is about to return to a campus path, sports field, or picnic. Just as they move back into the sun, the video abruptly freezes with bold text:

**“PAUSE. Did you reapply?”**

The video quickly jumps back to before the activity: the creator applies SunTrace SPF 50 normally and places one included UV-sensitive sticker on their skin. At the next outdoor break, the image freezes again. The creator removes the sticker, revealing the temporary contrast between the unchanged covered area and the surrounding exposed area.

A single caption explains:

**“The sticker makes sun exposure visible—not exact UV damage.”**

The creator immediately reapplies SunTrace SPF 50, the freeze releases, and they return outdoors.

The recurring format is:

**Go out. Pause. Reveal. Reapply. Go back out.**

The final frame shows the transparent SunTrace sunscreen and reusable sticker with one clear instruction:

**“Pause your activity. Reapply SunTrace SPF 50 before going back out.”**

The visual pause makes the product’s invisible benefit memorable without claiming that the sticker measures UV damage or proves the sunscreen has stopped working. SunTrace remains central: its transparent SPF 50 protection is easy to overlook, while the included sticker creates a brief, product-specific cue to refresh protection.

University creators can repeat the same “pause” format during class changes, sports breaks, or outdoor meetups using only the real product, the included sticker, and simple editing.
