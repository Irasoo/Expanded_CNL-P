FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

**Concept: “Keep Your Place”**

FreshDrop becomes the portable bookmark for everyday moments. When people briefly pause during a conversation, walk, break, or day out, the bottle marks exactly where they are. They take a drink, and the action continues from the same visual place.

The recurring campaign device is the **bottle bookmark**: FreshDrop stays in a consistent position in the frame while the scene moves around it. The bottle becomes the visual link between a brief pause and the next beat of the moment, giving the campaign a recognizable structure without relying on special packaging, product claims, or distinctive brand history.

**Principal execution**

Create short vertical videos for Instagram and Facebook, supported by stills and carousels. Each execution uses the same three-beat rhythm:

1. **The hook:** Open on a positive everyday moment already in progress, with the FreshDrop bottle clearly visible in the foreground or beside the person. The person briefly pauses at a natural point in the action. On-screen text reads: **“Keep your place.”**

2. **The bookmark:** The person picks up the portable bottle and takes a drink. The camera holds briefly on the bottle, using its position as the visual anchor. The drink is presented as a natural pause, not as the cause of improved performance or renewed energy.

3. **The return:** A simple cut returns to the same composition or bottle position, with the person continuing the conversation, rejoining friends, resuming the walk, or returning to the activity. The closing line appears: **“Your place is here. FreshDrop.”**

For example, a person at an outdoor table places FreshDrop beside them while friends continue talking. After a sip, the edit returns to the same bottle position as the person leans back into the conversation. In another execution, a walker pauses beside a bench, drinks from the bottle, and the next shot picks up from the same marked spot as they continue along the route.

**Static and carousel adaptation**

Still posts use the bottle as a clear visual bridge between two moments. The first panel shows the person briefly paused with FreshDrop marking their place. The second shows them back in the activity with the bottle still visible. The line **“Keep your place.”** appears in the first panel, with **“Your place is here. FreshDrop.”** as the branded close.

Carousels reveal the same sequence one beat at a time: the moment in progress, the bottle and sip, then the return. The bottle’s repeated position, framing, and visibility make the idea recognizable even without motion.

**Brand and product role**

FreshDrop is meaningful because it is the portable drinking water already with the person during ordinary activities. It is carried into the scene, available for a drink during a natural pause, and used as the consistent visual marker across every execution. The idea makes no claim beyond the practical benefit of having drinking water in a portable bottle.

**Strategic and production approach**

The concept is designed for broad adults aged 18–45 and uses familiar situations that work across Instagram and Facebook. The opening line creates immediate curiosity without sounding corrective, while the bottle’s fixed visual position gives the audience a simple pattern to recognize and remember. The payoff is social and emotional rather than functional: the person has a place in the moment and can continue from there.

Production remains practical for a medium budget. Each post needs an everyday location, one or a small group of people, a standard FreshDrop bottle, and a simple locked-off or repeatable composition. The same typography, bottle placement, short pause, and closing line can be reused across conversations, walks, breaks, and casual outings. FreshDrop builds recognition through the repeated “bottle bookmark” system rather than through any product characteristic unavailable to other bottled-water brands.
