==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

**Concept: “The Package Is the Coffee”**

FoldBrew makes the moment of preparation impossible to mistake: the package disappears because it becomes the drink.

Short-form videos open in an ordinary office setting with a worker reaching for a takeaway coffee, coffee machine, pod, or disposable sachet. The action stops before any of those options are used. Instead, they place a FoldBrew coffee sleeve into a clear cup or mug, pour in hot water, and hold the camera close as the sleeve visibly dissolves. The water changes into one serving of coffee, leaving the finished drink and no separate package behind.

The key visual is a simple, satisfying before-and-after: FoldBrew’s recognizable coffee sleeve is clearly visible in the hot water, then breaks down on camera until it has become the coffee. The preparation is shown in real time or with a tight, straightforward time-lapse, using ordinary product photography and short-form video production so the proof remains credible and easy to understand.

Each Instagram Reel, TikTok, and short-form digital video follows the same structure:

1. A recognizable workday coffee problem: needing a coffee quickly at the office.
2. A close-up of the FoldBrew sleeve going into hot water.
3. The sleeve visibly dissolving into the drink.
4. The completed coffee, with the line: **“The package is the coffee.”**
5. A direct trial prompt: **“Try FoldBrew at work.”**

Instagram stills and carousel posts extend the demonstration through sequential product photography: **Fold. Pour. Dissolve. Drink.** The final frame shows the finished coffee beside the absence of a coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet, communicating the product benefit without making broader environmental claims.

FoldBrew’s role is the entire preparation solution: the package contains the coffee, becomes the coffee when dissolved in hot water, and enables a faster workplace serving without requiring a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet. For urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee, the idea turns a familiar workday need into a quick, memorable ritual with a visible payoff. The dissolving package is both the product demonstration and the reason to remember FoldBrew, directly supporting first-time trial.
