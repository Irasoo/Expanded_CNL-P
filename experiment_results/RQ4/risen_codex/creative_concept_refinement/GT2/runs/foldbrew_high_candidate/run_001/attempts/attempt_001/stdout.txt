==================== USER INPUT 1/1 ====================
Brand:
FoldBrew

Product:
A premium instant coffee concentrate packaged inside an edible, water-soluble coffee cup sleeve. When the sleeve is placed in hot water, it dissolves and becomes one serving of coffee.

Target audience:
Urban office workers aged 22-35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster and lower-waste option at work.

Objective:
Drive first-time trial of FoldBrew and make the product's unusual "dissolve the packaging to make the coffee" behavior memorable.

Key product benefit:
The package itself becomes the coffee, so the user does not need a coffee machine, coffee pod, or disposable coffee sachet.

Channels:
Instagram, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Budget:
Medium.

Constraints:
Use ordinary product photography and short-form video production. Do not use celebrities, large public installations, or fictional environmental claims. The central idea must visibly demonstrate the package dissolving into the drink.
================== END USER INPUT 1/1 ==================

==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

**Concept: “The Package Becomes the Coffee”**

FoldBrew turns the moment of opening a coffee package into the product demonstration itself. The creative shows an office worker at their desk placing the edible, water-soluble coffee sleeve into a clear mug, pouring in hot water, and watching the sleeve visibly dissolve into one serving of coffee. The package does not become waste; it becomes the drink.

**Principal Execution**

A series of 10–15-second vertical videos opens with a close-up of the FoldBrew sleeve beside an empty mug and the line:

**“What if the package was the coffee?”**

The worker pours hot water over the sleeve. In a continuous close-up, the sleeve dissolves and the drink forms in the mug. The final frame shows the finished coffee at the desk with:

**“FoldBrew. The package becomes the coffee.”**

The demonstration is filmed with ordinary product photography and short-form video production: a real office desk, a clear mug, hot water, and the FoldBrew product. The visible dissolve is the recurring visual signature across Instagram Reels, TikTok, and short-form digital video.

Still photography and short cutdowns use the same transformation in simple sequences:

**Sleeve → hot water → coffee**

Supporting copy can state the practical product role without adding claims beyond the brief:

**“One serving. No coffee machine, pod, or disposable coffee sachet.”**

**Brand and Product Role**

FoldBrew is positioned as a premium instant coffee concentrate whose package is part of the preparation, not something left behind afterward. The dissolving sleeve is both the product’s functional benefit and its memorable brand asset: viewers understand what FoldBrew is by seeing the package become the coffee.

**Audience and Objective**

The concept is designed for urban office workers aged 22–35 who regularly buy takeaway coffee but want a faster, lower-waste option at work. Showing the entire preparation in a familiar office setting makes the product relevant to their workday, while the unmistakable dissolve gives first-time viewers a reason to stop, understand the product immediately, and remember FoldBrew for trial.
