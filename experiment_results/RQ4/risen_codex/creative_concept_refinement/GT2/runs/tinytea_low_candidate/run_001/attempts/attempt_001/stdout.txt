==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

# TinyTea: Pass the Cold

## Core idea

Turn TinyTea’s chilled bottle into a recognizable **cold relay**. At each event, one person takes a cold sip, holds the bottle toward the camera, and makes a simple handoff gesture—covering the lens with the bottle and revealing the next student doing the same thing. The prompt is:

> **“Pass the cold. Show us your campus.”**

The gesture is specific to the product: the bottle is not just branded merchandise; it is the object that starts and visually “passes” the cold moment from one student to the next. The same action works in a live event, a vertical video, the AR application, and the interactive billboards.

## Principal execution

During the seven-day launch window, the globally famous celebrity appears in person at a live TinyTea event in each of five major international cities. Every event uses the same compact format:

1. The celebrity opens a chilled TinyTea, takes a sip, and says, **“Pass the cold.”**
2. The celebrity covers the camera with the bottle and reveals the first student participant.
3. Students repeat the bottle-to-camera handoff in short vertical clips.
4. The event ends with a group handoff that provides a consistent closing shot for campaign edits.

The events do not require separate games, performances, or city-specific mechanics. Each location is a repeatable version of the same product demonstration and participation ritual.

## AR and billboard mechanic

The dedicated TinyTea AR mobile application has one job: when a student points the phone at a TinyTea bottle or the campaign marker, it places a simple animated “cold pass” effect over the bottle and records the handoff transition. Students then submit the resulting short clip to the campaign.

Each custom interactive digital billboard uses the same interface in all five cities. It displays:

- the latest approved cold-pass clips;
- the number of completed handoffs in that city; and
- a visual relay linking the five event locations.

Students interact with the billboard by selecting a featured clip or adding their own approved submission through the application. The billboard system is standardized rather than independently designed for each city, reducing unnecessary creative and technical variation while retaining five custom installations.

The five cities do not depend on complex live synchronization. Each billboard can update its local relay position and display the same campaign state when connectivity is available, with the event footage and student submissions assembled into the wider five-city story afterward.

## Local university participation

Local university communities are the campaign’s main participation network, not merely an audience for the international events. Before each event, university groups receive one concise invitation: bring or buy a cold TinyTea, record one bottle handoff, and submit it through the AR application. After the events, the same groups receive selected clips and the identical prompt for continued participation throughout the first month.

This gives students a low-effort action they can perform on campus, in residences, or with friends, while the celebrity and international relay supply the awareness hook. Students do not need to attend an international event to take part.

## Content system

Professional cinematic production captures only the essential hero material: the celebrity’s five bottle handoffs, the repeatable event ritual, billboard footage, and representative student participation. A single visual template then turns the material into reusable vertical edits for TikTok and Instagram:

**bottle opens → cold sip → bottle covers lens → next student appears → campus identified.**

The template makes the campaign recognizable without requiring a new concept or production style for every post. University communities can continue producing their own versions during the first month, while TinyTea reposts approved clips and assembles the five-city relay into short awareness edits.

## Brand and product role

TinyTea’s benefit is built into the behavior. The action begins with opening a lightly sweetened bottled tea, taking it cold, and passing that cold moment to another person. The bottle, the sip, and the handoff are the campaign’s repeated visual signature across every required channel.

## Production and approval condition

The concept is deliberately reduced to one event script, one AR effect, one billboard interface, one submission rule, and one video template. This is the most practical form of the mandatory five-city execution: the same kit can be adapted for each location, and the same footage structure can be captured and reused across channels.

However, the mandatory combination of an in-person globally famous celebrity, five international public events, five custom interactive digital billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and completion within seven days still cannot credibly be delivered for TinyTea’s stated production budget of below $1,000. Those requirements must be treated as a production approval gate. The campaign may proceed only after the necessary budget, celebrity and city commitments, technical resources, permits, and schedule are secured; otherwise, this remains a clear creative direction rather than an executable production plan.
