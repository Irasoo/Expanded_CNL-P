FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

## TinyTea: The Three-Minute Cold Spot Relay

**Core idea:** Turn the familiar university study-break pause into one recognizable TinyTea ritual. Wherever a student takes a three-minute break, a chilled TinyTea bottle creates a **Cold Spot**: a visible condensation ring that signals, “This is my reset.” Five international city events pass the same Cold Spot forward to local university students, who complete the relay through Instagram, TikTok, and university communities.

The opening hook is:

> **“Your brain has a break. Give it a Cold Spot.”**

Every piece of content begins with the product, not the spectacle: a cold TinyTea bottle, condensation on its surface, and the simple message **“Lightly sweetened tea, served cold.”** The celebrity and international scale then amplify the ritual rather than replace it.

### The repeatable ritual

At each of the five live public events, the globally famous celebrity performs the same three-second action:

1. Place a chilled TinyTea bottle on the marked **Cold Spot**.
2. Take a sip.
3. Lift the bottle to reveal the condensation ring.

The custom interactive digital billboard immediately enlarges that ring around a TinyTea bottle and sends it visually toward the next city and, finally, toward local university campuses. Students repeat the action at the event and film their own study-break version for vertical video.

The student prompt is deliberately specific:

> **“Take three. Make your Cold Spot. Show your study-break sip.”**

This gives the audience a clear behavior, connects the product to a real university moment, and makes the benefit easy to understand: TinyTea is a cold, lightly sweetened tea for a quick break.

### One digital action

The dedicated TinyTea AR application has one job: when a student points it at a TinyTea bottle, it places a digital condensation ring beneath the bottle. The student records a short vertical clip of the three-second ritual and shares it to Instagram or TikTok with the campaign prompt. No additional games, filters, or features are required.

University communities extend the relay by reposting selected student clips and inviting students to recreate the same Cold Spot during a lecture break, library pause, or commute. Each post remains visually recognizable because it contains the same bottle placement, condensation ring, and three-second action.

### Production system

The execution uses one master template rather than five separately invented events:

- one marked Cold Spot placement;
- one chilled TinyTea bottle and product close-up;
- one billboard animation built around the condensation ring;
- one AR function;
- one celebrity action;
- one student participation prompt;
- one fixed vertical shot list for every city.

The five city teams capture the same sequence, while the celebrity appears in person at each required event. The material is edited into one launch film, five city edits, and short Instagram and TikTok cutdowns. The same assets are then reused in weekly student-clip compilations throughout the first month, keeping the idea repeatable and limiting new creative production.

The campaign’s awareness path is straightforward: the celebrity and billboard create the initial attention; the product ritual makes the moment understandable; student clips and university-community sharing repeat the visual; and the continuing compilations keep TinyTea visible during the first month after launch. Awareness activity can be tracked through the campaign’s social video views, shares, reposts, and volume of student Cold Spot clips, without adding another audience action.

### Feasibility condition

The creative is designed to be modular and fast to reproduce, but the supplied constraints remain materially incompatible with a below-$1,000 total production budget. A globally famous celebrity appearing in person at five international live events, five custom interactive billboards, a dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, international logistics, and completion within seven days cannot honestly be presented as deliverable from that budget on the information provided.

Therefore, this concept proceeds only if celebrity talent, city venues, billboard access and technical integration, AR development, crews, travel, permissions, and other required production resources are confirmed as in-kind or separately funded before the seven-day production window begins. If those resources are not confirmed, the brief must be renegotiated; no amount of creative simplification can remove that constraint without removing a mandatory element.
