TinyTea

A new bottled iced tea sold by a small local beverage company.

Local university students aged 18-25.

Increase awareness of TinyTea during the first month after launch.

Key product benefit:
A lightly sweetened bottled tea that can be served cold.

Very low. The total campaign production budget is below $1,000.

The central campaign concept must include a globally famous celebrity appearing in person, a live public event in five major international cities, custom interactive digital billboards in each city, a dedicated augmented-reality mobile application, professional cinematic production, and completion of all production within seven days. ==================== AGENT OUTPUT ====================

FINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

**TinyTea: The Cold-Sip Relay — A Five-City Launch Gate**

**Core idea**  
TinyTea is the handoff that makes a cold break travel. At each launch event, a student opens a bottle, serves it cold, takes a sip, and passes the same visual cue to the next city. The action is deliberately simple and product-specific: **open cold, sip, pass TinyTea**.

**Execution**  
A single, tightly scripted seven-day production package creates one professional cinematic hero film and short vertical cutdowns. A globally famous celebrity appearing in person performs the opening handoff, which is then edited into the same relay sequence used across Instagram, TikTok, and local university communities.

The relay is staged at live public events in five major international cities. Each event uses the same compact format: one branded cold-sip handoff, student participation, and a feed of the next city’s handoff. One custom interactive digital billboard in each city displays the relay sequence and invites viewers to identify the next handoff. A dedicated TinyTea augmented-reality mobile application connects the five events, allowing students to view the relay and place the cold bottle and handoff cue into their own shared content.

The production stays repeatable by using one script, one visual transition, one participation instruction, and one edit system across all locations. The celebrity and international scale create the opening attention; the bottle, cold serving cue, and handoff make the idea memorable and keep TinyTea’s lightly sweetened, cold-serve benefit at the center.

**Audience and channel role**  
University students aged 18–25 first encounter the relay through local university communities and the live events, then see short vertical versions on Instagram and TikTok. Every post asks for the same low-friction behavior: show a cold TinyTea, take a sip, and pass the relay onward. This gives the first-month awareness objective a recognizable repeatable format rather than a series of disconnected announcements.

**Production condition**  
This concept is complete only as a feasibility-gated launch plan. The required globally famous celebrity appearance, five international public events, five custom interactive billboards, dedicated AR application, professional cinematic production, and seven-day completion cannot credibly be delivered by a small local beverage company for a total budget below $1,000. The seven-day package therefore functions as a production specification, not a claim that the work fits the stated budget. TinyTea must secure funding and operational support sufficient for every mandatory element before authorizing production; otherwise the concept cannot be executed without violating the brief. Within those conditions, the standardized cold-sip relay is the simplest single mechanism linking the product, audience, channels, and launch objective.
